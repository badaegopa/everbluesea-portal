01 · What Does It Measure?

Measures the structural deepening of income and asset inequality based on the Gini coefficient. Beyond the simple Gini coefficient, it also captures intergenerational and regional inequality mobility. Inequality is a core component of the Uprising Index (UI), and GEI is designed for precise measurement of the Λ¹² A3 (social) dimension.

02 · Core Formula

G = 1 − Σᵢ(Xᵢ − Xᵢ₋₁)(Yᵢ + Yᵢ₋₁)

Calculates inequality as the area between the Lorenz curve and the line of perfect equality. From 0 (perfect equality) to 1 (perfect inequality).

SymbolMeaning · Explanation
XᵢCumulative population ratio (i-th quantile)
YᵢCumulative income ratio
G = 0Perfect Equality — Lorenz curve coincides with the diagonal
G = 1Perfect Inequality — One person holds everything
G > 0.4Social Instability Threshold Interval
