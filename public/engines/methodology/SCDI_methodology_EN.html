01 · What is Measured

Measures the disruption intensity and propagation speed of the global supply chain. It tracks the pathway through which geopolitical risks, logistical bottlenecks, and energy price surges are converted into social pressure. Supply chain shocks immediately ripple through all axes of BSLI (clothing, food, and housing) via inflation, and SCDI is a leading indicator linked to Λ¹² A5 (Economy) + A11 (Infrastructure).

02 · Core Formula

SCDI = Σⱼ(Dⱼ · Wⱼ · Vⱼ) / N

Weighted average of disruption intensity, economic weight, and price volatility by tracked item.

SymbolMeaning · Description
DⱼDisruption intensity of item j — delivery delay ratio relative to normal
WⱼEconomic weight of item j — based on GDP input-output tables
VⱼPrice volatility of item j — 30-day σ
NNumber of tracked items
SCDI > 0.6Supply chain crisis threshold
