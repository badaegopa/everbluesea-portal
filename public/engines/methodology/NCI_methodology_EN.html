01 · What is Measured

It measures the institutional capacity of a state to deliver public goods and respond to crises. It consists of four axes: administrative efficiency, rule of law, crisis response, and public finance. Fragile states are extremely vulnerable to external shocks, and the NCI provides an early warning of state collapse risks at the Λ¹² A4 (institution) level.

02 · Core Formula

NCI = w₁·Admin + w₂·Law + w₃·Crisis + w₄·Finance

Weighted sum of the four axes. The sum of weights is 1.0 and is adjusted according to the stage of national development.

SymbolMeaning · Explanation
AdminAdministrative Efficiency — WGI Government Effectiveness
LawRule of Law Index — WGI Rule of Law
CrisisCrisis Response Capacity — Reciprocal of INFORM Risk Index
FinancePublic Fiscal Health — IMF Fiscal Monitor
wᵢWeight of Each Axis (Σwᵢ = 1.0)
