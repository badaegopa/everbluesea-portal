01 · What is Measured

Measures the depth of the parasitic power structure within institutions. It quantifies regulatory capture, elite circulation severance, corruption entrenchment, and media control as four axes. Institutional capture is the ultimate fuse of uprisings, and PPI is a deep corruption detector at the Λ¹² A2 (Governance) + A4 (Institution) dimensions.

02 · Key Formula

PPI = α·Capture + β·Circulation + γ·Corruption + δ·Media

Weighted sum of four axes. Coefficients α, β, γ, and δ are calculated through cross-validation by state institutional structure.
