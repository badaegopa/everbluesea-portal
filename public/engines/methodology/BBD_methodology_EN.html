01 · What is Measured

It detects the formation, expansion, and collapse cycle of asset price bubbles in real time. It analyzes the non-linear price dynamics of stock, real estate, and virtual asset markets to detect threshold collapse points in advance. Financial crises are key triggering factors of social instability, and the BBD operates as a leading signal in the Λ¹² A5 (economy) dimension.

02 · Core Formula

BBD = (P_t − MA_n) / σ_n > θ_c

A bubble signal occurs when the current price exceeds a threshold standard deviation multiple relative to the moving average.

SymbolMeaning · Explanation
P_tAsset price at time t
MA_nn-period moving average — baseline of the trend
σ_nn-period price standard deviation — normal fluctuation range
θ_cThreshold multiple (typically 2.0 to 2.5σ)
BBD > 1Signal for entering the bubble zone
