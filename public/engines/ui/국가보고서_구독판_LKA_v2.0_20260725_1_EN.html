In Sri Lanka, following the July 2022 uprising (storming of the Rajapaksa residence and the president's flight), the primary pressure source A7 departed the band from a peak of 0.489 to 0.408 in 2024, and A8 also receded below the boundary (0.411) — Supernova (Release)'s pressure release is directly imprinted in the time series.
