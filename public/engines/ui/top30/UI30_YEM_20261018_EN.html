1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

On July 13, after an Iranian aircraft landed in Sana'a on July 3, the Sana'a airport runway was attacked (the government's Ministry of Defense claimed responsibility; the Houthis pointed to Saudi Arabia), and the Houthis declared the end of the 'de-escalation phase.' Subsequently, missile and drone attacks on Saudi airports and energy facilities followed, along with a declaration of blockade against vessels linked to Saudi Arabia in the Red Sea. In early September, the Houthis launched a major offensive in Ta'izz, seizing the western coast including Mocha; however, on October 5, the government announced the recapture of areas near Mocha and Bab al-Mandab with coalition air support, and PLC Chairman President Al-Alimi declared the nationwide operation 'Dawn of Yemen.' According to IOM tallies, over 200,000 people were displaced, and approximately 72 UN staff members were detained by the Houthis. The Southern Transitional Council (STC) announced its dissolution on January 9, 2026, but internal bodies declared it invalid.
