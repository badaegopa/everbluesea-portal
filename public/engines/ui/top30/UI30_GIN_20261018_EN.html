1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

The transitional leader Mamady Doumbouya, who overthrew the Alpha Condé government in a September 2021 coup, was elected with 86.72% in the December 28, 2025 presidential election under a new constitution adopted by referendum in September 2025 (term extended from 5 to 7 years; removal of candidacy restrictions for members of transitional bodies), and the Supreme Court confirmed his 7-year term on January 4, 2026. In March 2026, a government decree dissolved some 40 parties including those of Condé, Siguatiou Diallo, and Sidya Touré, and in the May 31 general election (turnout 52.87%), the pro-presidential GMD coalition won at least 100 of 147 seats. On August 24, six bound and blindfolded male bodies were found in Forecariah, and on the same day a presidential decree discharged 173 soldiers—including coup co-conspirators—on grounds of desertion; on September 17, the telecommunications regulator (HAC) banned France 24. In ACLED's collection, political violence averaged 1–7 events per month, and GDELT recorded 0–9 protest events per week.
