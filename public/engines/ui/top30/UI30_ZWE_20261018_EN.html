1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

Constitutional Amendment Bill No. 3 (CAB3) passed the House of Assembly on June 18, 2026 (216 to 42), and the Senate on June 24 (75 to 4, with 1 abstention), extending presidential and parliamentary terms from five to seven years and replacing direct election of the president with election by parliament. The bill has entered the stage of presidential assent. As a result, the 2028 elections are postponed to 2030; Attorney General Ziyambi stated that 'it is not an extension of term,' while critics called it a 'constitutional coup.' Human Rights Watch recorded seven cases of abduction and detention of student leaders opposing the amendment (2026-04-24), and on July 31, during a nationwide shutdown call by the People's Coalition, Harare city center was sealed off by police. Meanwhile, 116,670 Zimbabweans returned from anti-immigrant protests in South Africa (information minister's tally, as of July 28).
