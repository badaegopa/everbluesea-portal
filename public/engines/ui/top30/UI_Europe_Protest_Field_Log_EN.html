Summary of Main Text — Spread of European Protests

  1. Order of Spread. In the fall of 2026, European protests began in Spain (August, housing) and then spread from France (ignited on 9/30) to Belgium (10/7), Italy, and Portugal. Belgium followed France almost identically a few days later (maximum correlation 0.89).
  2. Epicenters and Transmitter Countries Had Different Causes. The new austerity (54 billion euros), low trust (21%), and four prime ministerial changes were unique to France. What distinguished the places where protests spread was high debt and high interest rates (AUC 0.94) and shared language zones (0.93). Greece, with more fuel, and Germany, with lower trust, did not spread by the third week of October (the first student and teacher cost-of-living marches in three cities in Greece opened on 10/21).
  3. Who Goes First, When Does It Turn to Rioting? Among the 43 campaigns, all five that turned into riots were youth/student-led and met with repression. Union-led protests remained peaceful. France's riot conversion rate is 18.5 per 100 protest reports.
  4. Recurrence. In 40 European campaigns from 2010 to 25, retraction of policy was followed by recurrence within 24 months in 0/6 cases, whereas non-negotiated enforcement led to recurrence in 7/12. The government response in 2026 featured repression (29) roughly twice the number of concessions (16).
  5. Common Denominator. When the state's decisions descend into the daily lives of ordinary people (in 2026, 63% targeted specific state decisions), and when the channels to voice opposition to those decisions are blocked, youth emerge first, and when met with repression, riots occur.
  6. Read as an Axiom. It was not the quantity of gunpowder (BSLI) but the absorber binary (A2 institutional trust · A8 governance) that divided them. In places where government trust dimmed below 20%, when governance bypassed parliament to attack institutional trust, brittle fracture (A9) occurred — among 26 countries, only France at 9% and Greece at 19% were below 20%, and only France, which had initiated internal conflict, broke.
  7. Difference from Southeast Asia. European conflicts remain within the institutions through protests and strikes, fighting over living costs. In Southeast Asia, armed conflicts are fought over boundaries and belonging in state-formation periods. However, cost-of-living urban unrest (Indonesia, Philippines, Timor-Leste) resembles both regions.

Collection Limitations (Click to Expand)

Of 556 raw items (516 unique articles after duplicates removed), 279 were used as event sources, and 237 were excluded. Exclusion criteria: sports/military 'strikes', regions with the same name (e.g., Georgia, USA), events in other regions (Middle East, Ukraine, etc.), commentaries, columns, travel alerts, market articles, retrospectives of events before 10-20 (e.g., Liège riots, retrials of Northern Ireland race riots), clashes between football/basketball spectators (Partizan–Crvena Zvezda), aggregators whose reliability could not be verified (shattered.io, tech-insider's Belgian '16 arrests' report, etc.), and protests against foreign affairs (e.g., Iranian execution protests in Germany). Collection limitations: raw data consisted only of Google News RSS headlines and links for the most recent 1 day, and because the links were news.google.com redirects, WebFetch could not open the articles, and AP and Guardian originals were also blocked, so figures were confirmed only from headlines and the English Wikipedia (2026_European_unrest, 2026_French_student_protests collected on 10-23). Events without dates in headlines and unconfirmed texts are marked date='Unknown' (mostly reports from 10-21 to 22). The source lang follows the collection query language notation (some Albanian outlets use English headlines). GBR and IRL queries have few related articles, and jurisdictional boundaries in Northern Ireland are unclear. The 2026_French_protests article is a homonym and contains no content.

Limitations

  1. News searches retrieve up to 50 items per country party for the most recent 1 day, and exposed articles vary depending on search algorithms and language. Small local actions may be omitted.
  2. Event bundling and summaries are based on collected article headlines and partial texts. Events with a single source are marked 'Single Source.' Paywalls and blocked articles were not accessed.
  3. Participation estimates differ significantly between organizers, police, and media reports, and the stated figure was adopted.
