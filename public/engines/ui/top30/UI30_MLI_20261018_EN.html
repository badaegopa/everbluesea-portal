1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

On April 25, 2026, JNIM (affiliated with al-Qaeda) and the Tuareg FLA (Front for the Liberation of Azawad) simultaneously attacked Bamako, Kati, Gao, Kidal, Sévaré, and Mopti; in Kati, Defense Minister Sadio Camara was killed by a suicide car bomb. The FLA seized Kidal, the Russian African Corps withdrew from Kidal, and on April 28 JNIM declared a blockade of roads to Bamako and Kati (the second blockade since the September 2025 fuel blockade). On October 6, 2026, the Malian Armed Forces (FAMa) re-entered Kidal with support from the Russian African Corps in Operation Doukolo-Kolo, and the FLA announced this as a 'strategic and controlled withdrawal' that was temporary. The junta dissolved political parties in 2025, appointed Assimi Goïta to a five-year term without elections (renewable indefinitely if necessary), and Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger withdrew from ECOWAS in January 2025.
