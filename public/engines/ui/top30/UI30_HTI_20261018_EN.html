1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

The Transitional Presidential Council (CPT) dissolved on its expiration date, February 7, 2026, and Prime Minister Alix Didié Piémont has been the sole holder of executive power since. The Gang Suppression Force (GSF), launched under UN Security Council Resolution 2793 (2025-09-30), has a full strength of 5,550 but numbered only 1,083 military personnel as of July 8, 2026; the Security Council extended the mission to March 31, 2027 for six months on September 30 (12 in favor, 3 abstaining). The National Electoral Commission (CEP) scheduled the first round of the presidential and legislative elections and a referendum on constitutional revision for December 13, 2026, the first such elections in ten years. According to BINUH tallies, at least 1,408 people were killed in Q2 2026 (April–June), at least 47 died and more than 50 were kidnapped in the Kenscoff attack of August 23–24, and there are approximately 1.5 million internally displaced persons (AP-cited UN figures).

1986~2001

Incomplete Democratization Transition Following the Collapse of the Duvalier Regime

Dependence within the International Political Economy since Independence

Recurring Structure (Observation): The structure in which dependence on external great powers (France and the United States) and a fragile democracy appear together is described over a long-term time series.
