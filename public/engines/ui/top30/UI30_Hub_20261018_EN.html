Selection — Uprising Index Gap G

G = F − SE⁺, F = m·(−a), m = A2·A8 (Country Analysis Series Λ¹² v2.0), a = second-order central difference of BSLI auxiliary time series v1.2 (2022, 2023, 2024), SE⁺ = A2 + A8. Because E_A2 = A2·[1 − κ_L0·(1 − A8_check)] ≤ A2 in the canonical UI formula SE(t) = E_A2 + E_A8, and because canonical values of κ_L0·A8_check are not available for individual countries at present, the upper bound SE⁺ was approximated (approved by Cheonghae 2026-10-17). Across all 196 countries, |F| is small at ≤ 0.02, so the ranking in fact reflects the order of lowest absorption capacity (A2+A8). The ranking is a record of position, not an assessment.

Structure of Country Editions — Discourse Six-Layer Order

  1. Layer 1 Data — Basis for selection, country analysis indices (Λ¹² 12 dimensions · BSLI · National Capacity Index), event data (ACLED · COW · UCDP · coups · Archigos · NAVCO), current situation and chronology, government announcements, domestic media (with ideological leanings noted), cross-checking official announcements with independent sources
  2. Layer 2 Interests — Countries and actors with interests, reactions of neighboring countries, geopolitical status, remarks by key figures (cited in full text)
  3. Layer 3 Historical Patterns — Recurring structures (observed only)
  4. Layer 4 Perspective Shift — Three collapse paths and the conditions for institutional elasticity in this country (no path determination is made)
  5. Layer 5 Knowledge Frames — Related research identified on publisher and DOI pages, with sources and limitations
  6. Layer 6 — Exclusive to Cheonghae (淸海)

All facts were verified on actually visited pages, and Wikipedia, blogs, and communities were not used. Each party's claims are written side by side with their sources without judgment. Unverified items are disclosed separately at the end of each country section.

Scope of Application of Six-Layer Discourse — The narrative ranges from Layer 1 (data and records) to Layer 5 (interpretation through prior research). Prescriptions, moral evaluations, leader assessments, and predictions are not made. Layer 6 is reserved exclusively for Cheonghae (淸海).
