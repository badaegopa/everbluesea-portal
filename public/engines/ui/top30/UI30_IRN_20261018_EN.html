1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

Nationwide protests that began on December 28, 2025, with bazaar merchants in Tehran protesting the decline in currency value turned into large-scale crackdowns in January 2026; the government counted 3,117 deaths, while HRANA tallied over 7,000. On February 28, 2026, U.S. and Israeli strikes killed Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, Mojtaba Khamenei succeeded him on March 8, and after a 14-day ceasefire proposed by Pakistan in April 8, the United States resumed its port blockade in July and launched 'Operation Economic Outcast' on August 24. In September 2026, annual inflation was reported at 73.6% (Iran Statistical Center), the rial had fallen by approximately 35% against the dollar since July (Bonbast), and sea-borne crude oil exports were reported as 0 from the second week of September onward (Vortexa, as of October 5). The UN investigative mission stated in September that the January crackdown amounted to crimes against humanity, and President Trump announced on October 8 that he would not resume attacks before the November 3 midterm elections.
