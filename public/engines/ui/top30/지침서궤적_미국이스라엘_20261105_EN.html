The same framework, different backlash. The eight steps in which concentrated electoral power shakes a leader's network of loyalty are read side by side for both countries.

Everblue Sea Institute for Social Dynamics · Policy Ignition Simulator · Six-Layer Discourse · 2026-11-05

Elections are not ignition; the power that would have exploded on the streets has been channeled into votes and condensed. As condensed power grows, pressure first shakes the leader's network of loyalty (cronies, family, coalition partners).

If the purpose of the research is a 'leader's guidebook,' reading the causal trajectory from an individual decision to its conclusion forms its backbone. The United States 2026 and Israel 2026 are the first two empirical cases. They share the same cause (the Iran War) but differ in where the popular backlash falls — that difference is the value of this special edition.

Reading Angle — The popular backlash is read not from national indicators but from grassroots perception(Axis 2). The structure of steps ①–④ and the network fracture are in Layers 1–5 (observation and structure), while what to implement first and evaluations of the leader belong to Layer 6, reserved for Cheonghae. The end (⑧) remains open, and Hermes daily collection tracks it every day.
