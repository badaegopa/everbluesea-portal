1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

Following the outbreak of war between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) on April 15, 2023, the RSF seized El Fasher on October 26, 2025, controlling nearly all of Darfur, while the SAF controlled Khartoum, northern, eastern, and central regions, returning state institutions to Khartoum in January 2026. The main front shifted to Kordofan, with the SAF announcing on October 2 the capture of Al-Mazroub in North Kordofan, and President Burhan of the Sovereignty Council rejecting negotiations on October 6 before expressing support for U.S.-led peace efforts in a letter to President Trump on October 16. According to UN and international organizations' estimates, the war spread to 13 out of 18 states, displacing approximately 14 million people, with Sudanese refugees within the region numbering around 4.3 million (February 2026, UNHCR). Following airstrikes on gold mining areas along the border by Egyptian forces on October 10 (estimated), reports emerged of dozens (OCHA) to at least 72 deaths (Emergency Lawyers), followed by the killing of two Egyptians in Khartoum on October 12.

2-3. Geopolitical Status

United States (Department of State · Bulos Special Envoy) (2026-10-07) — The Department of State stated in September (2026) that neither the SAF nor RSF or their leaders represent 'legitimate constitutional governance' in Sudan. Senior Advisor for Arab and African Affairs, Mark Bulos, mentioned on October 5 in X an inclusive national dialogue led by Sudanese and a transition to a civilian-led government. [Source]

UN Security Council (2026-02) — China, Pakistan, and Russia tend to emphasize the sovereignty of the Burhan government, while P3 (France, UK, US) view both SAF and RSF as responsible for atrocities and unfit to govern (SCR February 2026). [Source]

United States · EU (Sanctions) (2026-02) — The United States sanctioned a transnational network centered on Colombians who recruited RSF fighters, while the EU imposed restrictive measures on RSF Deputy Commander Abdelrahim Hamdan Dagalo (U.S. sanctions from September 2023). [Source]

UN · Switzerland · 5+5 Mechanism (2026-10-15) — Joint announcement of the 6th Geneva Conference. The Quartet, 5+5 mechanism, neighboring countries, and permanent members are scheduled to attend. [Source]

UNHCR (2026-02-17) — Approximately 4.3 million Sudanese refugees within the region, with an estimated 470,000 new arrivals expected in 2026; regional response plan of $1.6 billion. [Source]

IOM · IPC · ACLED (Al Jazeera compilation) (2026-10-04) — Approximately 14 million displaced since the war, spreading to 13 out of 18 states; about 19.7% of ACLED-recorded attacks occurred in Kordofan. New displacements in Kordofan increased by approximately two-thirds from February to July to over 219,000. Approximately 19.5 million people (more than 40% of the population) face acute hunger, with famine risk in 14 areas (IPC May). The Sudanese pound in areas under military control has lost about half its value since summer. [Source]

IPC Famine Review Committee (2026-10-12) — October 12 alert: Acute malnutrition among children in two displacement camps in eastern Jebel Marra exceeded famine thresholds at 38% and 30.8%; of 17 nutrition surveys in Darfur, 13 were 'critical.' Over 300,000 displaced in Kordofan since October 2025. As of October 8, less than half of humanitarian appeals had been funded. [Source]

UN Humanitarian Coordinator (Deniz Brown) (2025-12-26) — First UN visit to El Fasher since the siege on December 26, 2025; described the city as a 'crime scene' and estimated that thousands may have died. [Source]

Recurring Structure (Observation): A regime creates an armed force separate from the regular military to suppress rebellions and serve as a check.
