1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

On November 26, three days after the November 23, 2025 presidential election, military officers unilaterally declared themselves the 'Supreme Military Command for Restoring Order,' ousted President Umaro Sissoco Embaló, and suspended the electoral process; the National Electoral Commission stated on December 2 that most of the vote-counting tally sheets and the computerized system had been destroyed during the coup and that final results could not be announced. General Orta Intaah took office as transitional leader for a one-year term, and ECOWAS and the AU suspended Guinea-Bissau's membership. On January 21, 2026, the military issued a decree setting the presidential and legislative elections for December 6. Opposition leader Domingos Simões Pereira (PAIGC) was arrested on the day of the coup and rearrested on July 10, 2026; he was released on July 24 to receive medical treatment in Portugal.
