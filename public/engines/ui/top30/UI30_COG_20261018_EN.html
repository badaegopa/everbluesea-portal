1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

President Denis Sassou Nguesso (82) won a fifth consecutive term in the March 15, 2026 presidential election with 94.90% confirmed by the Constitutional Court (turnout 84.99%) and was inaugurated in Kintélé on April 16. Opposition parties UPADS and UDH-Yuki boycotted the election, hardline opposition called for a boycott, and phone and internet networks were down throughout voting day. On January 11, 2026, two months before the election, a convoy of vehicles from the Directorate General for Presidential Security (DGSP) clashed with former 'Ninja' militiamen loyal to Pastor Étienne Ntoumi in Mbindoulou, Pool Province; the opposition party CNR claimed helicopter bombardments on January 25. The IMF estimated public debt at 97.2% of GDP at end-2025 and noted new accumulations of domestic and external arrears.
