1-4. Current Situation and Chronology

On June 17, 2026, hundreds of residents of the Bayramaly district (mostly women) protested power outages in front of the Mary Governorate building amid a heatwave of 40°C; RFE/RL reported this as an extremely rare protest, and it was dispersed after the governor promised to resolve the issue within days. On September 27, before and after the 35th anniversary independence parade in Ashgabat, shortages of groceries and rising prices along with disruptions to public transport were reported, with a reduction in Iranian supply cited as one cause. Externally, on July 25 the Ministry of Foreign Affairs issued an unusually blunt statement condemning the shooting of an Iranian vessel in the Caspian Sea, and as of September 15, 122.3km of the TAPI pipeline's Herat section had been laid but no sales contract had been signed. The Hermes collection ACLED protest + riot count is very low: 3 events in 2018, 8 in 2020, and 1 through July 2026.

3-1. Recurrence in History — Self-Similarity and Resonance

Layer 3 Historical Patterns — Self-Similarity and Resonance

1991-10-27 ~ 2022 (Succession Structure Since Independence)

After declaring independence from the Soviet Union on October 27, 1991, a personality cult formed under the Niyazov regime, and subsequent presidents largely maintained the system. In 2022, Serdar Berdimuhamedov succeeded his father Gurbanguly as president.

Recurring Structure (Observation): A structure in which power is transferred within the family under a personality cult and strict information control. Political changes are observed to occur within the leadership rather than through street mobilization.

Recurring Shortages in Daily Life (Around National Holidays)

The Times of Central Asia records recurring shortages and rationing of subsidized goods in state-run stores. In September 2026, it also reported shortages and transportation disruptions just before Independence Day.

Recurring Structure (Observation): Hydrocarbon import dependence → decline in currency value → shortage of subsidized necessities → official media emphasize commemorative events and unity, only independent media report shortages. Rarely followed by collective protests.

Hundreds of residents gathered outside the regional administration building amid a power outage during a heatwave; the protest ended with on-the-spot concessions by the governor and subsequent dismissal of lower-level officials.

Recurring Structure (Observation): Single issue in living infrastructure → assembly before local administrative bodies → local resolution through on-site concessions and punishment of lower-level managers. An example where the issue did not escalate into political demands.
