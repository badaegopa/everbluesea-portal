C-05Release / Non-release Spectrum

Non-release (White Dwarf) — Bolivia · Iran: Even after uprisings and regime changes, 4 axes [A2·A5·A7·A8] remained in the band. Pressure was not resolved by the events. (Iran's current assessment worsened further to Black Hole · SE0.21.)
Release (Supernova) — Sri Lanka: A7 peaked at 0.489 → departed band at 0.408 in 2024, only 1 band axis (A5) remains. Bangladesh: Ignited in 2024.08 [A2·A7·A8]; whether release occurred will be judged by 2025+ data. However, release does not equate to recovery — Sri Lanka's grassroots base BSLI remains at −0.2457 (81st).
Pre-ignition Observation (Nepal) — At T-1 (2024) immediately prior to the 2025.09 uprising, the 3 axes [A6·A7·A8] were already captured in advance within the band. However, grassroots base depth is unmeasurable due to the uncollected BSLI blind spot.
Control Group (Korea) — Band 0-axis, lowest ΣP (1.96), and the only positive BSLI (+0.4627). A case where the 2024 impeachment phase was absorbed through the A2 elasticity channel and did not follow the Supernova collapse path (UI Paper §6.2).

C-06Conclusion of Comparison (Observer)

Placing the six countries on a single coordinate system reveals two things. First, the common prerequisite for ignition is that absorber A8 and pressure source A7 enter within the threshold together — this was true for all five uprising countries, and only the control group was not. Second, the event of an uprising does not necessarily resolve the structure — in Bolivia and Iran, load remained even after 5 years, and Sri Lanka released its load, yet grassroots base life remains deep. Structure cannot predict when the fire will catch, but it tells which country retains the margin to absorb shocks. The magnitude of that margin determines the path following the uprising.
