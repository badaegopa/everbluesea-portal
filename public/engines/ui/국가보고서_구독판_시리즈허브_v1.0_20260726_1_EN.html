§ 00  OverviewWhat This Bundle Is

These four papers transition the Uprising Index (UI) from a static score into a time-series vector for the first subscription edition bundle. While existing country reports answered "What is the score now?", this bundle answers "In which direction, and at what speed, is it moving?" That tool is the Pressure Coordinate System — It arranges the 12 dimensions of Λ¹² in the form of a planetary system centered on the BSLI (grassroots base index), plotting each dimension's pressure P as an orbital radius, perturbation chains as angles, and the 2016→2024 changes as trajectories.

There is one core criterion line. When the dimensional pressure P exceeds the threshold band P* = 0.445806, that dimension is designated as "in-band," and the number and composition of in-band dimensions serve as inputs for uprising ignition conditions and collapse path classification. The most striking regularity in the empirical measurement results of this bundle is that, in all five countries that experienced an uprising, A7 (social capital) and A8 (governance) were in-band — a structure designated as the "red spine" in the comparative paper.

P (Pressure)
A pressure value normalizing the deficiency of each Λ¹² dimension to [0,1]. A higher value means that dimension is bearing more of the social load.
P* = 0.445806
Threshold band boundary (exploratory threshold). Dimensions with P ≥ P* are judged as "in-band." The same constant is fixed and used across all four papers.
ΣP
Sum of 12-dimensional pressure = total structural load. Not the risk of an individual dimension, but "the weight borne by society as a whole."
ΔP
Annual pressure change. The sign indicates direction (deterioration/alleviation), and the absolute value indicates activity (speed).
BSLI
Basic Social Life Index. Range [−2,+1], negative is normal (0 = basic living line, negative = depth of sinking below it).
WD / SN / BH
Collapse path classification — White Dwarf (suppression and non-discharge) / Supernova (collapse, discharge, and subsequent realignment) / Black Hole (implosion and no restoration structure). A metaphor, not an astrophysical claim.
A2 / A5 / A6 / A7 / A8
Λ¹² dimension labels — A2 Governance Capacity, A5 Food/Housing Security, A6 Labor Market, A7 Social Capital, A8 External Debt/Fiscal Soundness.

§ 03  Two AxesTwo-Axis Separation — Sinking Depth and Structural Load Are Not the Same

Reading the table vertically reveals no pattern. The country with the deepest BSLI sinking (Bangladesh, −0.6223) has the lowest ΣP (3.097), while Bolivia (−0.1227), with a relatively shallow BSLI, has the highest ΣP (3.707). The two indices measure different things — BSLI measures how deeply the grassroots base has sunk, while ΣP and the number of in-band axes measure how much the structure is bearing. § 05  ObserverObserver Conclusion

① What Was Observed

In all five countries that experienced an uprising, A7 (social capital) and A8 (governance) were within the threshold band, whereas for the control group South Korea, none of the 12 dimensions fell within the band. Because the sample consists of six countries, this is an observation of regularity, not the establishment of causality or predictive power.

② What Is Separated

Grassroots base sinking (BSLI) and structural load (ΣP / number of in-band axes) did not move in the same direction in this sample. Therefore, the unilinear proposition that "people revolt if livelihoods are bad" is not supported by this data. This is why both axes must be examined simultaneously.

③ What Diverges

If the number of in-band axes decreases after ignition, it diverges into discharge (SN); if maintained, into non-discharge (WD). In Iran, following the WD assessment as of 2022, A2 and A8, which served as absorbers, dropped together and shifted toward BH — this bundle empirically demonstrates for the first time that path classification is not a fixed label, but a state that shifts along a time series.

④ What Is Reserved

P* = 0.445806 is an exploratory threshold and subject to recalibration upon sample expansion. Nepal is in an uncomputable BSLI interval, leaving one axis empty on the coordinate plane. Conclusions are not extended beyond these two qualifying reservations.
