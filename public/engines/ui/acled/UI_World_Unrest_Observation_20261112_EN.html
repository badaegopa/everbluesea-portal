Global Unrest, Protests, Uprisings, and Coup Observations Report

Centered on the European Wave of Fall 2026 — Cross-reading of Automated Coding of 197 Countries' Reports, ACLED, and Confirmed Event Lists

Cheonghae (Giseub Kim) · Everblue Sea Institute for Social Dynamics · 2026-11-12

Among the 74 countries with sufficient reported volume over 30 days up to 2026-11-10, 20 countries met the criteria of protest surges or unrest accompanied by violence, and 10 of them are in Europe.

The proportion of protests in Europe (protest events out of all GDELT events) rose from 0.71% during the reference period to 1.34% recently, a factor of 1.89.

Among European countries with the highest multipliers, Portugal (10.6x), France (5.6x), Italy (5.0x), Belgium (3.6x), and Serbia (3.6x) rank in order.

Comparing the same period (01-01 to 06-20) using raw ACLED data, protests in Europe were 16,148 events in 2025 and 14,930 events in 2026, while riots were 419 events and 450 events. The ACLED data ends in June and does not capture the wave after September.

Since 2025, there have been 12 regime change, coup, or foreign intervention events confirmed through public sources (§5).

7. Methods and Limitations

  • Recent window: 2026-10-12 ~ 2026-11-10; reference window: 2026-05-15 ~ 2026-10-11.
  • Protest share = protest events (CAMEO 14) ÷ total events for that country. This is an apparatus to mitigate differences in the volume of reporting itself. Magnification = recent share ÷ reference share.
  • Headline signals are counted according to title word rules. 'strike' overlaps with airstrikes, so it must be accompanied by a labor-related word to count as a strike. Rule words are in params.json.
  • 140 countries with coverage below the baseline (300 rows / 30 rows) were not classified. This does not mean that there is no unrest in these countries.
  • GDELT is machine-coded, so the same event may be captured across multiple articles and rows, and the proportion can fluctuate when war or disaster reporting surges (denominator effect). Countries with high magnification factors are targets for individual re-verification of events rather than conclusions in themselves.
  • In countries with ongoing armed conflicts (Sudan, Yemen, Ethiopia, etc.), reports of armed clashes may be mixed into CAMEO 145 (violent demonstrations and riots), leading to an overestimation of riot proportions. For these countries' 'unrest (with violence)', it is necessary to consider the possibility that it may be influenced by conflict reporting rather than street protests.
  • This report is an observational record. The figures measure the magnitude of the phase, without containing judgments on causes or future developments.
