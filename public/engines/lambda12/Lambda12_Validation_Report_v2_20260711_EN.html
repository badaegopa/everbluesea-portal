Discovery and Calibration of EU Common Tariff Bias

In the first calculation of η, the European region was calculated as the lowest of the 7 regions (average 0.2216). Sweden was 153rd, Finland 179th. Cause: Due to the EU Common Customs Union, 5 tariff indicators were structurally missing, distorting η downward. Decisive proof: Only the United Kingdom (GBR), which left the EU through Brexit, submitted 15/15, ranking 4th in Europe.

Calibration Principle: Substitute WTO Code 918 (EU Entirety) 2023 common tariff values for EU27 tariff missing values — not statistical interpolation, but historical restoration of actually applied tariffs. Results: Europe 7th → 2nd (0.2865), Sweden 153rd → 41st, Finland 179th → 111th.
