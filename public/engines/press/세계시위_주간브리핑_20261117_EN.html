7. Methods and Limitations

  • Data Period — Recent 30 days: 2026-10-17 to 2026-11-15, reference period: 2026-05-20 to 2026-10-16, previous week: 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-08, last week: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-15, headlines after 2026-08-17
  • Data Sources — GDELT 1.0 daily events (aggregated by Everblue Sea Institute Hermes), Hermes country headlines (Google News), ACLED regional classification, confirmed case list (source is the 'Confirmed Cases' sheet)
  • Classification Target — Countries with more than 300 total event rows and more than 30 protest rows in the recent 30 days. Underreporting is marked as 'Data-deficient countries'
  • Protest Frequency — GDELT protest rows (CAMEO 14). Because the same event may be captured in multiple rows, this represents the quantity of reported protests rather than the number of events
  • Protest Proportion Multiplier — (Period protest rows ÷ Period total event rows) ÷ (Reference period protest rows ÷ Reference period total event rows). The formula for the 'Country Classification' P·U·V columns
  • Strength: Strong — Riot rows (CAMEO 145) ÷ Protest rows ≥ 0.15 or 'Fatal Force' headlines ≥ 2
  • Strength: Moderate — Protest proportion multiplier ≥ 2.0 or riots/protests ≥ 0.08
  • Strength: Weak — Sufficient reporting volume but does not meet the above criteria
  • Type · Control · Response — Number of headlines containing protest words, the country's name or an adjective, and category words together in the title. Only categories with 2+ cases are displayed. Main type = most common category
  • Exclusion Rules — Omit articles on airstrikes, sports, prison riots, and armed clashes. Lethal force counts only expressions of firing and deaths directed at protesters
  • Limitations — Headlines have a cap of dozens per country, so category determinations are signals, not complete tallies. 'Insufficient evidence' does not mean there is no category. In conflict countries, reports of armed clashes may be mixed with reports of unrest
