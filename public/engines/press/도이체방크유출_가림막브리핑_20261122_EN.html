Field Case — The 'Gap' in North Korea Sanctions Monitoring (Newstapa)

The following is a report of the facts from Newstapa's "[Inside Deutsche Bank] Transactions with 'North Korea' Also Passed…Deutsche Bank's North Korea Sanctions Monitoring Has a 'Gap'" (2026-11-18, Ji-yoon Kim and Hye-in Kang) — this is a point where networks, intermediation industries, and "saw but passed"overlap in one issue.

  • Saw but Passed : In 2017 and 2020, when German intelligence agencies (BfV) and the Ministry of Finance handed over lists of North Korean and Iranian 'front companies,' the banks had already processed these transactions for several years. Of 50 entities from 2015.1 to 2020.3, 40 were processed, involving approximately 5,000 cases · 700 million euros (approximately 13.3 trillion KRW) before automatic blocking began.
  • Networks (Foreign Exchange Transactions) : Of the 1,301 suspicious transactions across 20 entities in 2010–2017 totaling approximately 166 million euros, 88% were processed through foreign exchange transactions with HSBC, China Merchants Bank, Ping An Bank, and others — even transactions with 'North Korea' noted in the transaction purpose field passed through. This is physical currency that flowed while the correspondent banking network erased its sources.
  • Where the Intermediation Industry Was Seen (Part of Disconnect ③) : 2 Korean addresses, of which B Company in Yeongdo-gu, Busan also had an address in the BVI, and that BVI address was associated with 'an offshore service company that creates paper companies', whose contact was a Russian offshore services firm (Newstapa). This is a point where the shell supplier partially revealed itself in Korean reporting.
  • Discrepancy between Judgment and Outcome : Suspicious transaction reports accumulated (42 from 2007 to 2017), but processing continued, and Dandong Kerhua, which the bank had said had "no sanctions risk," became a US sanctions target within four months.
  • Korean Authorities : The National Intelligence Service responded that it had "nothing to confirm" regarding whether it was aware of two domestic addresses (Newstapa).
