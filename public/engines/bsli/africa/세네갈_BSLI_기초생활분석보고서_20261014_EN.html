§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is low.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized to median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
衣(clothing) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.5209 (Grade E · 99th out of 139 countries).

In Senegal, deprivation (D) accounts for 0.33–0.35, determining most of the BSLI, while additive terms do not reach 0.05. Although the extreme poverty line poverty rate has dropped significantly, the population below the upper-middle-income line ($8.30) remains around 78%.
