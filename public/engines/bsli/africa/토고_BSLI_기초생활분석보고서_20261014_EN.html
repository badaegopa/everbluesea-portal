Togo is far below the basic life line (0) with a Series BSLI of -0.8166 (Grade E · 122nd out of 139 countries). Consumer price inflation moved from 7.97% in 2022 to 0.43% in 2025. Reports were collected indicating an upgrade to lower-middle-income status in the World Bank income classification in July 2026.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight as food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Series value for this country: -0.8166 (Grade E · 122nd out of 139 countries).

In Togo, Deprivation (D) at 0.54 drags down the BSLI, while the additive terms do not reach 0.03. The rise in BSLI from 2018 to 2021 stemmed almost entirely from the decrease in H_s (Palma 2.12 → 1.66).
