§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence lower weight.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (normalized median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.8549 (Grade E · 126th of 139 countries).

Botswana is a rare country where, among BSLI's subtraction terms, distributional disparity (H_s) is larger than deprivation (D). Although per capita income falls into the upper-middle range (GNI $7,390), distributional disparity drags BSLI far below the basic living line.
