§ 01

Executive Summary

Mali sits far below the basic life line (0) with a compendium BSLI of -0.6118 (Grade E · 107th out of 139 countries). The actual measurements in auxiliary time series v1.2 are 2018 (-0.5508) and 2021 (-0.6413), with the remaining cells carried over and 2024 showing data absence. The compendium value lies between the two actual measurements.

Deprivation (D) determines BSLI: 0.4888 → 0.5767 ($3.65/day poverty rate 42.3% → 49.9%). Housing stress (H_s) is nearly unchanged at 0.0932 → 0.0907 (Palma 1.50 → 1.48), while food (F_ML) and housing (H) are very small, moving from 0.0321 and 0.0306 to 0.0273 and 0.0253.

According to Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate rose from 29.4% in 2018 to 36.1% in 2021, the $8.30 rate rose from 84.5% to 88.6%, and the national poverty line poverty rate increased from 42.1% to 45.5%. The Gini coefficient is 35.7, and consumer prices rose by 9.62% in 2022, 2.06% in 2023, 3.21% in 2024, and 3.28% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is lower because food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment degree (normalized by median income) — Weight is higher because housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtractive): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Compendium value for this country: -0.6118 (Grade E · 107th out of 139 countries).

For Mali, D is 0.58, determining most of BSLI. Inequality (Palma approximately 1.5) remained virtually unchanged from 2018 to 2021, and the increase in the poverty rate drove the decline in BSLI.
