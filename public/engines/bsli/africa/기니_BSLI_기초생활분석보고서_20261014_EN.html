§ 01

Executive Summary

Guinea has a compendium BSLI of -0.5316 (Grade E · 100th out of 139 countries), placing it below the basic living line (0). The measured value in supplementary time series v1.2 consists of a single point in 2018 (-0.2166); the cells for 2016–2020 are carryovers of this value, while 2021–2024 are marked by data absence. The compendium value is more than 0.3 lower than this measured value (verification against the authoritative version required).

The four terms of the 2018 measured value are Food (F_ML) 0.0511, Housing (H) 0.0479, Deprivation (D) 0.2294 ($3.65-a-day poverty rate 19.8%), and Housing Stress (H_s) 0.0364 (Palma 1.07). D accounts for the majority, and since Palma is close to 1, H_s is small.

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), according to the World Bank API, the 2021 PPP $3.00-a-day poverty rate decreased from 21.3% in 2012 to 11.7% in 2018, the $8.30 poverty rate decreased from 83.8% to 76.6%, and the national poverty line poverty rate stood at 43.7% in 2018. Consumer price inflation moved from 10.49% in 2022 to 3.57% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Authoritative Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.5316 (Grade E · 100th out of 139 countries).

Guinea's 2018 measured values show a structure in which Deprivation (D) determines the BSLI. The large difference with the compendium value suggests the possibility that the underlying calculation data differ, and this report takes the compendium value as the standard.
