§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canon (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight due to room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized median income) — Housing pressure carries a higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. This country's series value: -0.7564 (Grade E · 117th out of 139 countries).

In Liberia's 2016 data, D is 0.53, determining most of the BSLI. Since household income distribution data is subsequently absent from international databases, changes cannot be observed via the auxiliary time series.
