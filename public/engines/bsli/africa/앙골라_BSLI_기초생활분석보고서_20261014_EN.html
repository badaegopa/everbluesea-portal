§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles/aid, so the weight is lower.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure entails an irreversible cascade of key money (jeonse) → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D(Subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(Subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: -0.9895 (Grade E · 128th out of 139 countries).

Like Zambia, Angola exhibits a structure where both subtracted terms (D and H_s) are large. Due to the Palma^1.5 transformation, H_s rivals D in countries with wide distribution disparities.
