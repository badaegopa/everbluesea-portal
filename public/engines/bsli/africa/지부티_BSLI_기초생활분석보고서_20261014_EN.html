§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have lower weight because they offer room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — Housing pressure carries higher weight as it represents an irreversible chain: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's series value: -0.6517 (Grade E · 110th out of 139 countries).

Djibouti is a country where both deprivation (D) and distributional disparity (H_s) pull down the BSLI. While its per capita income (GNI $3,960) is upper-middle within the region, an unemployment rate of 26% and a Palma ratio of 2.04 demonstrate that this income is not widely distributed.
