§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Edition (4-term subtraction formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight because there is room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life satisfaction degree (normalized median income) — Housing pressure carries a high weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. This country's Series value: -0.8084 (Grade E · 120th out of 139 countries).

In Sierra Leone, D drags down the BSLI at 0.65, while the additive terms do not reach 0.05. The high inflation period of 2022–2024 (27–48%) lies outside the auxiliary time series.
