§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment level (median income normalized) — housing pressure carries a high weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtractive): Deprivation index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -1.0649 (Grade E · 132nd out of 139 countries).

Namibia is a country where distribution disparity (H_s) is overwhelming among the subtractive terms of BSLI. Although in the middle tier in terms of per capita income (GNI $4,340), income concentration drags BSLI down by more than 1 below the basic living line. Because H_s is near the upper limit, further widening of the disparity is less reflected in the index.
