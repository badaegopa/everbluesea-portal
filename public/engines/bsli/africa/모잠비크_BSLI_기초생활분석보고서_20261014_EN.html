§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -1.4078 (Grade E · 138th out of 139 countries).

Mozambique is a country where all four terms of the BSLI lie near the normalization boundaries. With D hitting the upper bound of 1.0 and F_ML·H reaching the lower bound of 0, actual changes below these points no longer register on the index. What drives the BSLI is virtually only H_s.
