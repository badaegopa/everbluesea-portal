§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Income 30th–50th percentile (ML tier) average real income index (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -1.0022 (Grade E · 130th out of 139 countries).

In Uganda, D stands at 0.81, largely determining the BSLI. The gap between the national poverty line (16.1%) and the international extreme poverty line (54.4%) is very large, meaning the same household may not be poor by national standards yet be in extreme poverty by international standards.
