§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(食, Weight 0.40): Average Real Income Index of the 30th–50th Percentile (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence the lower weight.
H(住, Weight 0.60): Basic Living Satisfaction (Normalized to Median Income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty Rate at $3.65 a Day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma Ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: +0.1781 (Grade D · 38th of 139 Countries).

That the compendium value is above the basic living line means the additive terms (F_ML · H) are larger than the subtractive terms (D · H_s). In the absence of an auxiliary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms lies outside the scope of this report.
