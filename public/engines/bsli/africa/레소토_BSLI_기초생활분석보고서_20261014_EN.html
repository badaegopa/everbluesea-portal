§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises offer room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized to median income) — Weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: -0.8483 (Grade E · 125th out of 139 countries).

The Southern Africa region has many countries with wide distributional disparities, leading to large H_s. Lesotho ranks 1st because that disparity is relatively small within the region.
