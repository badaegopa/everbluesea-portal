Executive Summary

Cameroon lies well below the basic living line (0), with a Series BSLI of -0.7954 (Grade E · 119th of 139 countries). The auxiliary time series v1.2 consists of the 2016 cell (2014 data, -0.6823) and the 2021 measurement (-0.5513, covering the 2019–2023 cells), with data absent for 2017–2018 and 2024. The Series value is more than 0.2 lower than the 2021 measured value (canonical verification required).

In this country, both deprivation (D) and housing stress (H_s) are high: in 2021, D was 0.4058 ($3.65/day poverty rate 35.1%), and H_s was 0.1848 (Palma 2.08). In the 2014 data, H_s was higher at 0.2967 (Palma 2.70). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) are low at 0.0378 and 0.0403.

According to Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate fell from 29% in 2014 to 26.7% in 2021, Gini fell from 46.6 to 42.2, while the $8.30 poverty rate rose from 72.7% to 74%. The national poverty line poverty rate stood at 37.7% in 2021. Consumer price inflation went from 7.38% in 2023 to 3.4% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence lower weight.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D(Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.7954 (Grade E · 119th out of 139 countries).

Cameroon is a country with both D and H_s above 0.18. Although BSLI rose as inequality narrowed (Palma 2.70 → 2.08) from 2014 to 2021, the Series value does not reflect this increase.
