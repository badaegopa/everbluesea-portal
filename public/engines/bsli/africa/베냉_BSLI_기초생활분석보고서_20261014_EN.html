§ 01

Executive Summary

With a Series BSLI of -0.7022 (Grade E · 114th out of 139 countries), Benin sits well below the basic life line (0). Supplementary time series v1.2 consists of the 2016 cell (2015 data, -1.1990), 2018 actual measurement (-0.6692), and 2021 actual measurement (-0.5109); the remaining cells are carryovers, and 2024 has missing data. The Series value is close to, and slightly lower than, the 2018 measured value.

There is a substantial divergence between the 2015 data and the 2018 measurement: Deprivation (D) shifted 0.8724 → 0.5772 ($3.65/day poverty rate 75.4% → 49.9%), and Housing Stress (H_s) shifted 0.3355 → 0.1178 (Palma 2.90 → 1.67). In 2021, D dropped further to 0.4652 and H_s to 0.0779 (Palma 1.39), while Food (F_ML) and Housing (H) remained exceedingly small at 0.0335 and 0.0314, respectively.

Based on the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate declined from 65.8% in 2015 to 37.2% in 2018 and 27.2% in 2021; the $8.30 rate fell from 93.2% to 88.1% and 86.5%; and the Gini index decreased from 47.6 to 37.9 and 34.4. The poverty rate at the national poverty line was 38.5% in 2018 and 36.2% in 2021. Consumer price inflation remained between 1.1% and 2.74% from 2022 to 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Form (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight due to short-term recovery potential from food crises via stockpiling and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment degree (normalized by median income) — Higher weight due to the irreversible chain reaction of housing pressure: Jeonse → Monthly rent → Gosiwon → Homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. This country's Series value: -0.7022 (Grade E · 114th out of 139 countries).

In Benin, D determines the vast majority of BSLI (0.47 in 2021). The drop of 28 percentage points in the extreme poverty rate and nearly 10 points in the Gini index across the three-year period from 2015 to 2018 is significantly larger than changes in other years. The underlying cause of this discontinuity (e.g., changes in survey methodology) is not adjudicated in this report but recorded as an anomaly.
