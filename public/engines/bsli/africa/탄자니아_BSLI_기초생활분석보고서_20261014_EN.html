§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight as food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment (median income normalized) — Higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.9945 (Grade E · 129th out of 139 countries).

In Tanzania's 2018 data, D stands at 0.73, determining most of the BSLI. Since the national poverty line (26.4%) is about half the international extreme poverty line (51.3%), the same household could be considered non-poor by national standards yet in extreme poverty by international standards.
