§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises allow room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.3127 (Grade E · 86th out of 139 countries).

Mauritania has a structure where the additive terms are small and the deprivation terms largely determine BSLI. The change from 2014 to 2019 also stemmed almost entirely from the D term.
