§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, Weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H(Housing, Weight 0.60): Baseline living fulfillment (normalized median income) — housing pressure represents an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D(Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) has been treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline of living. Series value for this country: -1.1127 (Grade E · 134th of 139 countries).

In Burundi, because D is near the upper limit, the index change remains small even as poverty deepens further. The 20–34% inflation and the decline in per capita income after 2020 lie outside the auxiliary time series.
