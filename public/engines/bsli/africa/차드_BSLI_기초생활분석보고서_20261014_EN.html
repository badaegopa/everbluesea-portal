§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline living standard. This country's compendium value: -0.8346 (Grade E · 124th of 139 countries).

In Chad, Deprivation (D) ranges from 0.57 to 0.64, determining BSLI, while the additive terms remain below 0.05. Over the same period, the international extreme poverty line rate decreased while the national poverty line rate increased — a directional difference identical to Burkina Faso.
