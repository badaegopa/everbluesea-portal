§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Given that food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, its weight is low.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — Housing pressure has a high weight because it involves an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma Ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.3515 (Grade E · 91st out of 139 countries).

A Series value below the basic living line means that the subtractive terms (D, H_s) are larger than the additive terms. In the absence of an auxiliary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms falls outside the scope of this report. The 2015 Gini of 42.4 is the last international figure showing that the distributional disparity in West Africa was not small.
