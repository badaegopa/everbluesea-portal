§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.6201 (Grade E · 108th among 139 countries).

The difference between Sudan's Series value and the 2014 data (approx. 0.39) is an area requiring verification of how changes during the measurement gap were incorporated into the Series calculation (canonical verification required). The magnitude of the four terms after 2014 cannot be ascertained from the supplementary time series.
