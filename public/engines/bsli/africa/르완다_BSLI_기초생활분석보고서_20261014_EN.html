§ 01

Executive Summary

Rwanda is at 1.1 below the basic living line (0) with a Series BSLI of -1.0931 (Grade E · 133rd out of 139 countries). The actual measurements in auxiliary time series v1.2 are 2016 (-1.0462) and 2023 (-0.7283); the cells for 2017–2018 and 2021–2024 are carried forward (fallback), and data are absent for 2019–2020. The Series value is close to the 2016 actual measurement and is 0.36 lower than the 2023 value.

The increase from 2016 to 2023 stemmed from the decreases in deprivation (D) 0.8437 → 0.6096 ($3.65/day poverty rate 72.9% → 52.7%) and housing stress (H_s) 0.2134 → 0.1432 (Palma 2.25 → 1.83). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) remain small at 0.0259 and 0.0235.

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), World Bank API data show that the 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate dropped from 63.8% in 2016 to 38.6% in 2023, the Gini coefficient decreased from 43.7 to 39.4, and the national poverty line rate was 27.4% in 2023. Consumer prices fell from 17.69% in 2022 and 19.79% in 2023 to 1.77% in 2024 before reaching 5.91% in 2025, and the unemployment rate (ILO modeled) stood at 11.36% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -1.0931 (Grade E · 133rd out of 139 countries).

In Rwanda, D exceeds 0.6, determining most of BSLI. Although D decreased significantly between 2016 and 2023, the Series value remains at the 2016 level. The national poverty line (27.4%) is captured lower than the international extreme poverty line (38.6%).
