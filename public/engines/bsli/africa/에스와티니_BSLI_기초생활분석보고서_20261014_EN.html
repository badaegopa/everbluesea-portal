§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — The weight is high because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -1.1499 (Grade E · 136th out of 139 countries).

Eswatini is a country where deprivation (D) and distributional disparity (H_s) jointly drag down the BSLI. Unlike Botswana, where H_s is larger than D, the two terms are similar in magnitude.
