§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Average Real Income Index for the 30th–50th Income Percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Adequacy of Basic Living (Normalized Median Income) — Housing pressure has high weight because it is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma Ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline living standard. Series value for this country: -0.7475 (Grade E · 116th out of 139 countries).

In Ghana's 2016 data, D (0.57) and H_s (0.21) are much larger than the additive terms (approx. 0.05), placing BSLI below the baseline living standard. The period of 23–38% inflation from 2022 to 2024 lies outside the auxiliary time series.
