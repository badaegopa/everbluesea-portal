§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Income 30th–50th percentile (ML stratum) average real income index (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized to median income) — housing pressure carries high weight because it forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic social life line. This country's Series value: -1.0291 (Grade E · 131st out of 139 countries).

Niger is one of the countries that has sunk deepest toward the lower bound (−2.0) of the BSLI range. With the deprivation term D exceeding 0.8, its structure prevents minor variations in other terms from bringing it anywhere near the basic social life line.
