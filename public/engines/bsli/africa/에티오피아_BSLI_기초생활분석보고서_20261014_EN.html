§ 01

Executive Summary

Ethiopia's series BSLI is -0.8096 (Grade E · 121st of 139 countries), falling far below the basic livelihood line (0). Auxiliary time series v1.2 consists of the 2016–2017 bins (2015 data, -0.5843) and the 2021 empirical measurement (-0.6434, 2019–2023 bins), while data are missing for 2018 and 2024. Between the two points, BSLI declined, and the series value is lower than both.

The decline from 2015 to 2021 stemmed from the increase in Deprivation (D) from 0.5246 to 0.6183 ($3.65/day poverty rate 45.4% → 53.4%) and the decreases in Food (F_ML) and Housing (H) from 0.0296 and 0.0279 to 0.0257 and 0.0233, respectively. Housing stress (H_s) decreased from 0.0883 to 0.0494 (Palma 1.46 → 1.18).

Based on the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate rose from 33% in 2015 to 38.6% in 2021, and the poverty rate at the national poverty line rose from 23.5% to 33.1%. Consumer price inflation stood at 33.89% in 2022, 30.22% in 2023, 21.04% in 2024, and 13.23% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — lower weight because food crises allow short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood fulfillment (median income normalized) — higher weight because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's series value: -0.8096 (Grade E · 121st of 139 countries).

Ethiopia is a country where D at 0.62 drags down the BSLI. Although inequality narrowed between 2015 and 2021 (Palma 1.46 → 1.18), the poverty rate rose, pulling BSLI down. The 2022–2024 period with inflation at 21–34% lies outside the auxiliary time series.
