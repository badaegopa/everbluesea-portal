§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Edition (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible cascade from key-money deposit (jeonse) → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is higher.
D (subtractive): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline of basic living. This country's Series value: -0.5783 (Grade E · 104th out of 139 countries).

The Gambia is a country where BSLI declined as both D and H_s increased between 2015 and 2020. The period of 16.97% inflation in 2023 following 2020 lies outside the auxiliary time series.
