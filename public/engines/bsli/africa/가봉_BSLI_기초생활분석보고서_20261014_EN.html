§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure carries an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is higher.
D (subtracted): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.1118 (Grade E · 67th out of 139 countries).

Gabon has relatively high middle-stratum income (F_ML · H) within the region, but distributional disparity (H_s) offsets it to that extent, configuring the country just below the basic living line.
