§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index for 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises offer room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (median income normalized) — Weight is high because housing stress is an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline of living. This country's Series value: -0.7072 (Grade E · 115th out of 139 countries).

In Guinea-Bissau, D is 0.62, which determines most of the BSLI. Although the disparity decreased between 2018 and 2021 (Palma 1.42 → 1.32), the poverty rate increased, causing BSLI to drop. The shift in the international extreme poverty rate from 75.7% to 37.1% and the Gini from 50.6 to 34.8 between 2010 and 2018 differs greatly in magnitude from the change in the national poverty line poverty rate (48.7% → 47.7%) over the same period. The cause of this disconnect is not judged in this report and is recorded as an anomaly.
