§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, Weight 0.40): Income 30th–50th percentile (ML stratum) average real income index (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H(Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure carries higher weight because it is an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(Subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.5322 (Grade E · 101st out of 139 countries).

Côte d'Ivoire saw its BSLI rise as deprivation (D) significantly decreased from 2015 to 2018, and rose slightly more from 2018 to 2021 as disparities narrowed. Nevertheless, D remains at 0.37, far larger than the additive term (0.04), placing it below the basic living line.
