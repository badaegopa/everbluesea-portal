§ 01

Executive Summary

Indonesia has a Compendium BSLI of -0.3315 (Grade E · 88th out of 139 countries), placing it below the basic living line (0). In the supplementary time series v1.2, all years from 2016 to 2024 are empirically measured and rose every year: -0.4142 in 2016, -0.2654 in 2019, -0.2197 in 2022, and -0.1725 in 2024. The compendium value is lower than recent values in the time series and falls between the 2016 and 2017 values.

Most of the increase came from the reduction in deprivation (D): 0.3490 → 0.2112 → 0.1439 ($3.65-a-day poverty rate 30.2% → 18.3% → 12.4%). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) rose slightly from 0.0421 and 0.0407 to 0.0600 and 0.0556, while housing stress (H_s) shifted from 0.1065 to 0.0860 (Palma 1.59 → 1.45).

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API national poverty line poverty rate decreased from 9.5% in 2022 to 8.5% in 2025, and the 2021 PPP $3.00-a-day poverty rate fell from 7.9% to 3.7%, but the $8.30 poverty rate stood at 64.1% in 2025. The Gini coefficient is 34.4. Consumer price inflation was 1.91% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) average (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment rate (normalized to median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: -0.3315 (Grade E · 88th out of 139 countries).

Indonesia is a country where D steadily decreased over 9 years, causing BSLI to rise annually. Nevertheless, because the additive terms are small, it remains below the basic living line. The gap between the national poverty line (8.5%) and the upper-middle-income line (64.1%) is substantial.

The Compendium BSLI is lower than recent time series values. Published figures are retained as in the original texts (DECISIONS 17·19) and placed side by side.
