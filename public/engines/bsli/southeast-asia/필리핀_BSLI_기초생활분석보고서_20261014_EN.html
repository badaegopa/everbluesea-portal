§ 01

Executive Summary

The Philippines stands below the basic living line (0) with a Compendium BSLI of -0.3407 (Grade E · 89th among 139 countries). Actual measurements in auxiliary time series v1.2 are 2018 (-0.3349), 2021 (-0.3618), and 2023 (-0.2796), while the remaining cells are carry-overs from preceding or subsequent actual measurements (the 2016 cell uses 2015 data). The Compendium value is close to the 2018 value and lower than the 2023 value.

Deprivation (D) determines the BSLI: 0.2669 → 0.2973 → 0.2415 ($3.65-a-day poverty rate 23.1% → 25.7% → 20.9%). Housing stress (H_s) decreased from 0.1167 → 0.0872 (Palma 1.66 → 1.46), and food (F_ML) and housing (H) are small at 0.0508 and 0.0480.

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $3.00-a-day poverty rate dropped from 8.3% in 2018 to 5.3% in 2023, the $8.30 poverty rate fell from 60.2% to 58.7%, and the Gini index declined from 42.3 to 39.3. The national poverty line poverty rate is 9.7% in 2025. In August 2026, the Philippine Statistics Authority announced this as an all-time low.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Edition (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence lower weight.
H(住, 가중 0.60): 기본생활 충족도(중위소득 정규화) — 주거 압박은 전세→월세→고시원→노숙의 비가역 연쇄라 가중이 높다.
D(감산): 박탈지수 = 하루 $3.65(2017 PPP) 빈곤율 · H_s(감산): 주거스트레스 = Palma 비율^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Compendium value: -0.3407 (Grade E · 89th among 139 countries).

The Philippines' BSLI is determined by the deprivation (D) term and small additive terms. In 2021, it declined as D increased, and in 2023, it rose as both D and H_s decreased. The discrepancy between the national poverty line (9.7%) and the international upper-middle-income poverty line (58.7%) is very large.
