§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Edition (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Compendium value: -0.2231 (Grade E · 79th out of 139 countries).

Myanmar's Compendium value is at the level of 2017 data. The auxiliary time series and international price statistics stopped in 2019–2020, so subsequent changes are documented only through reports by international organizations.
