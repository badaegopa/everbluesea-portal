Executive Summary

Vietnam lies just below the basic living line (0) with a Series BSLI of -0.0346 (Grade E · 60th of 139 countries). However, the measured values in auxiliary time series v1.2 are -0.0193 in 2016, -0.0034 in 2018, +0.0096 in 2020, and +0.0080 in 2022, with the 2020 and 2022 values above the basic living line — the signs differ from the Series value (canonical verification required C-1). The remaining cells are carried forward from adjacent measured years. The reference value is the Series.

The rise from 2016 to 2020 stemmed from increases in food (F_ML) 0.1164 → 0.1439 and housing (H) 0.1137 → 0.1412, along with a decrease in deprivation (D) 0.0486 → 0.0291 ($3.65-a-day poverty rate 4.2% → 2.5%), while during the same period housing stress (H_s) increased from 0.0855 → 0.1035 (Palma 1.44 → 1.57). In 2022, F_ML and H slightly decreased to 0.1386 and 0.1349, respectively.

According to the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the national poverty line poverty rate from the World Bank API dropped from 5.7% in 2019 to 4.2% in 2022, and the 2021 PPP $8.30-a-day poverty rate decreased from 29.8% in 2016 to 21.5% in 2022. Consumer inflation was in the 3% range from 2022 to 2025, and the unemployment rate (ILO modeled estimate) hovered around 1.5%.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence lower weight.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (normalized median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade of Jeonse → monthly rent → Gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.0346 (Grade E · 60th out of 139 countries).

In Vietnam, additive terms and subtractive terms nearly balance each other out, causing the BSLI to hover near 0. While D decreased, H_s increased, attenuating the upward gain — poverty reduction and gap widening manifested concurrently during the same period.
