Executive Summary

Vietnam stands just below the basic livelihood line (0) with a compendium BSLI of -0.0346 (Grade E · 60th out of 139 countries). However, the empirical measurements of supplementary time series v1.2 are 2016 (-0.0193), 2018 (-0.0034), 2020 (+0.0096), and 2022 (+0.0080), with the 2020 and 2022 values sitting above the basic livelihood line — showing a sign divergence from the compendium value (verification against canonical text required: C-1). The remaining cells are carried over from adjacent empirical measurements. The reference baseline is the compendium value.

The rise from 2016 to 2020 stemmed from increases in food (F_ML) 0.1164 → 0.1439 and housing (H) 0.1137 → 0.1412, along with a decrease in deprivation (D) 0.0486 → 0.0291 (poverty rate at $3.65/day: 4.2% → 2.5%), while during the same period, housing stress (H_s) increased from 0.0855 to 0.1035 (Palma 1.44 → 1.57). In 2022, F_ML and H experienced slight contractions to 0.1386 and 0.1349, respectively.

According to the World Bank API based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the poverty rate at the national poverty line dropped from 5.7% in 2019 to 4.2% in 2022, and the poverty rate at 2021 PPP $8.30/day declined from 29.8% in 2016 to 21.5% in 2022. Consumer price inflation remained in the 3% range from 2022 to 2025, and the unemployment rate (ILO modeled estimate) hovered around 1.5%.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight because room exists for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood fulfillment degree (median income normalized) — Housing pressure carries a higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: Jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: -0.0346 (Grade E · 60th out of 139 countries).

For Vietnam, additive and subtractive terms nearly offset each other, causing the BSLI to hover near 0. While D decreased, H_s increased, trimming the magnitude of the rise — poverty reduction and gap widening appeared concurrently during the same period.
