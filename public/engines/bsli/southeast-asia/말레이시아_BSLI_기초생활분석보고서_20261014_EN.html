§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized median income) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Compendium value for this country: +0.0948 (Grade E · 46th out of 139 countries).

In countries like Malaysia where $3.65/day poverty is virtually absent, the D term is close to 0, meaning BSLI is determined by the balance between middle-stratum income (F_ML·H) and distributional disparity (H_s). The national poverty line (RM2,589/month) is drawn at a much higher level than the international absolute line.

§ 05

Public Livelihood Situation and Chronology

2022
Poverty Statistics — Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM): Absolute poverty rate 6.2% (8.1% in 2021, 5.6% in 2019), poverty line RM2,589/month (RM2,208 in 2019). Urban poverty 4.5% (3.9% in 2019), rural 12%. Relative poverty rate 16.6% (baseline RM3,169) (DOSM · The Edge).
2024-06-10
Diesel Subsidy Rationalization — Peninsular Malaysia diesel ceiling price from RM2.15 to RM3.35 per liter (56%). Sabah, Sarawak, and Labuan maintained at RM2.15. The government cited leaks as subsidized volume increased from 6.1 billion liters in 2019 to 10.8 billion liters in 2023, projecting annual savings of approximately RM4 billion, and providing RM200/month support to eligible recipients (Malay Mail · The Vibes).

Reactions. A political party in Sarawak demanded that living costs be brought under control before subsidy adjustments, and economists expressed concern that an unphased hike could be perceived as a shock (The Vibes · SCMP). Subsequent changes in diesel prices were not confirmed in this investigation.

Urban Poverty. Between 2019 and 2022, urban poverty increased while rural poverty decreased (DOSM). In the income percentile-based BSLI, urban–rural disparities are not separately distinguished.
