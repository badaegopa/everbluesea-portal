§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized to median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = daily $3.65 (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.4883 (Grade E · 98th out of 139 countries).

That the Series value lies deep below the basic living line indicates that the additive terms (F_ML, H) are small and the subtractive terms (D, H_s) are relatively large. However, as this country lacks an auxiliary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms falls outside the scope of this report.

Discrepancy in Inflation Figures. The IPC report cited December 2023 inflation at 34% year-on-year, which differs from the ADB's 8.7% for the same month. It may reflect a different measurement (such as food prices), but this investigation could not verify it.

Measurement Void. National poverty statistics are from 10 years ago and are absent from the auxiliary time series as well, meaning recent trends in grassroots base livelihood can only be observed through indirect indicators such as food insecurity and inflation.
