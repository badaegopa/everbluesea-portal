§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — the weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — the weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtractive): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.4883 (Grade E · 98th among 139 countries).

That the compendium value lies deep below the basic living line indicates that the additive terms (F_ML, H) are small and the subtractive terms (D, H_s) are relatively large. However, as this country lacks a supplementary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms falls outside the scope of this report.

Discrepancy in inflation figures. The IPC report cited inflation in December 2023 as 34% year-on-year, which differs from the ADB's 8.7% for the same month. It may represent a different measurement (e.g., food items), but this could not be confirmed in this survey.

Measurement gap. Since national poverty statistics are from a decade ago and absent from supplementary time series, recent trends in grassroots base livelihood can only be viewed through indirect indicators such as food insecurity and inflation.
