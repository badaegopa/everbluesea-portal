§ 01

Executive Summary

Laos, with a compendium BSLI of -0.4694 (Grade E · 97th out of 139 countries), lies below the basic living line (0). In the supplementary time series v1.2, actual measurements exist at only two points: 2018 (-0.3962) and 2024 (-0.1977); the remaining cells carry forward adjacent measurements, and 2021 lacks data. The compendium value is lower than both measured values.

Between the two measurements, deprivation (D) decreased from 0.3090 to 0.1696 ($3.65/day poverty rate 26.7% → 14.7%), and housing stress (H_s) also decreased from 0.1314 to 0.0835 (Palma 1.75 → 1.43). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) rose slightly from 0.0455 and 0.0434 to 0.0577 and 0.0537.

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate dropped from 15.7% in 2018 to 7.1% in 2024, and the national poverty line rate dropped from 18.3% to 15%. Over the same period, consumer prices passed through 31.23% in 2023 and 23.13% in 2024 to reach 7.74% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment (median income normalized) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D (subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.4694 (Grade E · 97th out of 139 countries).

Laos is a country where BSLI is heavily influenced by the deprivation (D) term. The rise in BSLI between the two actual measurements in 2018–2024 came almost entirely from the decrease in D. Meanwhile, changes in living conditions during the high inflation period (2022–2024) fall within a measurement gap.

§ 05-B

Published Figures vs. Independent Sources — Examining the Gap

The gap in Laos lies in the divergence between the timing of poverty measurements and inflation measurements. While poverty decreased between the two points of 2018 and 2024, inflation in the intervening years (2022–2024) was 20–31% annually, and in 2026, 9% range inflation was reported again due to a fuel crisis. Furthermore, the compendium BSLI is lower than the two measured values of the supplementary time series. Published figures are preserved verbatim (DECISIONS 17·19) and juxtaposed.
