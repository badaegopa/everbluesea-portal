§ 01

Executive Summary

Laos is below the basic living line (0) with a compendium BSLI of -0.4694 (Grade E · 97th out of 139 countries). The measured points in the auxiliary time series v1.2 consist of only two points: 2018 (-0.3962) and 2024 (-0.1977); the remaining cells are carry-overs from preceding or subsequent measurements, with 2021 showing an absence of data. The compendium value is lower than both measured values.

Between the two measurements, deprivation (D) decreased from 0.3090 to 0.1696 ($3.65/day poverty rate 26.7% → 14.7%), and housing stress (H_s) also decreased from 0.1314 to 0.0835 (Palma 1.75 → 1.43). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) increased slightly from 0.0455 and 0.0434 to 0.0577 and 0.0537.

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate from the World Bank API declined from 15.7% in 2018 to 7.1% in 2024, and the national poverty line poverty rate dropped from 18.3% to 15%. During the same period, consumer prices went through 31.23% in 2023 and 23.13% in 2024, reaching 7.74% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment degree (normalized by median income) — The weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: -0.4694 (Grade E · 97th out of 139 countries).

Laos is a country where BSLI is heavily influenced by the deprivation (D) term. The rise in BSLI between the two measurements from 2018 to 2024 came almost entirely from the reduction in D. In the meantime, living condition changes during the high-inflation period (2022–2024) remain within a measurement void.

§ 05-B

Published Figures vs Independent Sources — Observing the Gaps

The gap in Laos lies in the fact that the timing of poverty measurement and inflation measurement diverge. While poverty declined between the two points of 2018 and 2024, annual inflation in between (2022–2024) stood at 20–31%, and in 2026, 9%-range inflation was reported again due to a fuel crisis. Furthermore, the compendium BSLI is lower than both measured values in the auxiliary time series. Published figures are kept as original (DECISIONS 17·19) and placed side by side.
