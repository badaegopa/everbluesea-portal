§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiling and aid, hence lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: +0.4257 (Grade C · 28th out of 139 countries).

Estonia's BSLI is determined by the addition terms. In 2022, the year of 19.4% inflation, the addition terms shrank significantly, wiping out most of the 2021 gains — since measurements are taken annually, this inflection is directly reflected in the time series.
