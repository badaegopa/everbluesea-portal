§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's Compendium value: +0.0754 (Grade E · 49th out of 139 countries).

In Bulgaria's BSLI, a large H_s (Palma 1.7–2.0) offsets a substantial portion of the additive terms. As the additive terms expanded annually, it entered Grade D in 2020.
