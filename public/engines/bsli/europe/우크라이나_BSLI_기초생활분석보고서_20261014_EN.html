Executive Summary

Ukraine is above the basic living line (0) with a Compendium BSLI of +0.1453 (Grade D · 41st out of 139 countries). Supplementary time series v1.2 shows annual empirical measurements from 2016 to 2020, all in Grade D: rising from +0.1017 in 2016 to +0.1272 in 2019 and +0.1287 in 2020. The 2021 and 2022 cells are carried over from 2020, and 2023 and 2024 lack data. The Compendium value is higher than any single year in the time series.

Additive terms determine the BSLI: food (F_ML) 0.1271 → 0.1595 → 0.1547, housing (H) 0.1151 → 0.1393. Subtractive terms are small — deprivation (D) 0.0010 (poverty rate at $3.65/day: 0.1%), housing stress (H_s) 0.0128 → 0.0157 (Palma 0.87 → 0.89).

As of Hermes collection (2026-10-14), according to the World Bank API, the 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate fell from 17% in 2017 to 11.3% in 2020, the national poverty line rate fell from 34.9% to 23.2%, and the Gini was 25.6 in 2020. These values terminate in 2020. Consumer price inflation was 20.18% in 2022, 12.85% in 2023, 6.5% in 2024, and 12.73% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index for 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — housing pressure entails an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: +0.1453 (Grade D · 41st out of 139 countries).

Ukraine has a small gap (Gini 25.6) and low absolute deprivation, yielding almost no subtractive terms, so low additive terms (F_ML, H around 0.14–0.16) determine BSLI. Household income distribution data during the wartime period after 2022 are absent from the supplementary time series.
