§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises allow room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic livelihood fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure carries an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtractive): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's Series value: +0.5923 (Grade B · 17th of 139 Countries).

The rise in Malta's BSLI was driven by increases in the additive terms (F_ML·H), while annual fluctuations are produced by H_s (distribution gap). The $3.65/day poverty rate is minimal at 0.4%.
