§ 01

Executive Summary

Russia is above the basic living line (0) with a compendium BSLI of +0.1113 (Grade D · 42nd among 139 countries). The supplementary time series v1.2 is empirically measured annually from 2016 to 2023, but is divided into two segments: 2016–2020 is at the Grade D/E boundary with +0.1089 to +0.1187, and 2021–2023 rises to Grade D/C from +0.2516 to +0.3069. The 2024 cell is carried over from 2023. The compendium value remains at the same level as the previous segment (2016–2020).

The difference between the two segments stems from the additive terms: Food (F_ML) 0.2222 → 0.3418, Housing (H) 0.2141 → 0.3354, with raw values shifting from 16.42 → 24.67. The subtractive terms show that housing stress (H_s) decreased from 0.1170 → 0.0614 (Palma 1.66 → 1.27), while deprivation (D) remains small at 0.0016 ($3.65/day poverty rate of 0.1%).

According to the World Bank API collected via Hermes (2026-10-14), the 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate decreased from 2.2% in 2021 to 0.9% in 2023, the national poverty line poverty rate dropped from 11.1% in 2021 to 7.2% in 2024, and the Gini coefficient decreased from 35.1 to 33. Consumer prices recorded 13.74% in 2022, 5.87% in 2023, 8.43% in 2024, and 8.72% in 2025, with the unemployment rate (ILO modeled) standing at 2.13% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight due to room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid during food crises.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment degree (normalized by median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure follows an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtractive): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: +0.1113 (Grade D · 42nd among 139 countries).

Russia's BSLI is jointly determined by the additive terms (F_ML · H) and housing stress (H_s approximately 0.06–0.12). The additive terms jumped roughly 1.5 times within a single year from 2020 to 2021, creating two distinct segments in the supplementary time series. In the same year, poverty rates and the Gini coefficient from the World Bank API did not move significantly. The cause of this step change is not evaluated in this report and is recorded as an anomaly (the same type as Poland's 2019→2020 step change).
