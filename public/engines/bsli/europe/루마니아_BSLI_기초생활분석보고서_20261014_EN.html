§ 01

Executive Summary

Romania stands above the basic life line (0) with a Compendium BSLI of +0.1014 (Grade D · 45th out of 139 countries). Supplementary time series v1.2 features annual empirical measurements from 2016 to 2023: from +0.0787 in 2016, +0.0205 in 2017, and +0.1181 in 2018 (Grade D), passing +0.1490 in 2021, and rising sharply to +0.2222 in 2022 and +0.2848 in 2023. The 2024 entry is carried over from 2023. The Compendium value is close to the 2018 empirical measurement and is 0.18 lower than recent values.

The 2022 rise came from substantial single-year increases in Food (F_ML) 0.1789 → 0.3035 and Housing (H) 0.1690 → 0.2991. Deprivation (D) was 0.0000 during 2018–2021, then recorded 0.0275 in 2022 and 0.0106 in 2023 ($3.65/day poverty rate 0.9%), while housing stress (H_s) stood at 0.0335 (Palma 1.05).

According to Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate fell from 10.8% in 2020 to 5.1% in 2023, the Gini dropped from 34.6 to 29.8, and the national poverty line (at-risk-of-relative-poverty) rate fell from 21.2% in 2021 to 18.4% in 2024. Consumer prices recorded 13.8% in 2022, 10.4% in 2023, 5.72% in 2024, and 7.19% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Compendium value for this country: +0.1014 (Grade D · 45th out of 139 countries).

Although Romania's supplementary time series is empirically measured every year, its year-on-year amplitude is large (a sharp rise in D in 2017, D at 0 in 2018, and a sharp rise in additive terms in 2022). It may reflect varying survey conditions from year to year in the raw income distribution data (recorded without interpretation). The Compendium value lies near the midpoint of this amplitude.
