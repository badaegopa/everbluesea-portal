§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Definitive Version (4-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized median income) — Weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Series value for this country: +0.7738 (Grade A · 6th out of 139 countries).

Austria's BSLI comes almost entirely from additive terms. Even during the high-inflation period (2022–2023), the measured BSLI maintained Grade A, while the Palma ratio rose from 1.08 to 1.18 during that time, causing H_s to grow slightly.
