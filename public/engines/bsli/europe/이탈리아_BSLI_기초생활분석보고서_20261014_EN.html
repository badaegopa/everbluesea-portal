§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The weight is lower because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood adequacy (median income normalized) — Housing pressure has a higher weight because it is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's Series value: +0.4452 (Grade C · 27th among 139 countries).

Italy's BSLI is determined by moderate-sized additive terms. The national poverty line (approximately 20%) is a relative poverty risk line, and '1 in 10 in absolute poverty' is based on the national statistical office's absolute poverty basket, thus measuring something different from the BSLI's international absolute baseline term D.
