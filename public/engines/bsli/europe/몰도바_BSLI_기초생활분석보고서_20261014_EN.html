§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises allow room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized median income) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (Subtractive): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living threshold. This country's Series value: +0.1088 (Grade D · 43rd out of 139 countries).

Because Moldova exhibits small disparity (Gini 26.8) and absolute deprivation, subtractive terms are minimal, and low additive terms (F_ML · H approx. 0.12–0.14) determine the BSLI. The decline from 2021 to 2023 stemmed from the decrease in additive terms.
