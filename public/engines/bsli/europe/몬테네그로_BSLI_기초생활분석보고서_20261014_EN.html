§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D(subtractive): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: -0.0063 (Grade E · 57th out of 139 countries).

The fact that the compendium value is very close to the basic livelihood line means that the additive terms and subtractive terms are almost equal. Since there is no supplementary time series, the specific magnitude of each of the four terms falls outside the scope of this report.
