§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: +0.5862 (Grade B · 18th among 139 countries).

Ireland's BSLI is determined by the additive terms, and as the disparity narrowed (Palma 1.26 → 1.06) between 2016 and 2023, it rose from Grade C to Grade B. Because the national poverty line is a relative benchmark, it measures something different from the absolute benchmark D term in BSLI.
