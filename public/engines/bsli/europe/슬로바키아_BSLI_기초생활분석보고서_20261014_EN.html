§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises allow room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure constitutes an irreversible chain of lump-sum deposit (jeonse) → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence receiving higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living baseline. This country's Series value: +0.3478 (Grade C · 29th out of 139 countries).

In Slovakia, subtraction terms are virtually zero, so BSLI reflects the raw magnitude of the addition terms. Those addition terms decreased after 2019, sliding from Grade C to Grade D — this is a change in middle-stratum income levels rather than disparity.
