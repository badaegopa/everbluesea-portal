Executive Summary

Denmark stands well above the basic livelihood line (0) with a compendium BSLI of +0.7538 (Grade A · 7th among 139 countries). The auxiliary time series v1.2 reflects annual empirical measurements for 2016–2023: rising from +0.6491 (Grade B) in 2016 to +0.7045 in 2020 and +0.7100 (Grade A) in 2021, before declining to +0.6603 in 2022 and +0.6516 (Grade B) in 2023. The 2024 cell is carried over from 2023. The compendium value is higher than any single year in the time series.

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate was 0.6% in 2023, the Gini coefficient rose from 27.5 (2020) to 29.9 (2023), and the national poverty line (at-risk-of-poverty rate) was 12.4% in 2021. Consumer price inflation moved from 7.7% in 2022 to 1.38% in 2024 and 1.89% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood adequacy (median income normalized) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtractive): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: +0.7538 (Grade A · 7th among 139 countries).

Denmark's BSLI originates almost entirely from additive terms. During the high-inflation period of 2022–2023, the additive terms decreased and inequality slightly widened (Palma 0.98 → 1.13), dropping it from Grade A to Grade B.
