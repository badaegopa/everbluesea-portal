§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The weight is low because food crises offer room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living fulfillment (normalized median income) — The weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtractive): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) has been treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline living standard line. Compendium value for this country: +0.4856 (Grade C · 23rd among 139 countries).

Czechia is a country with very small distributional inequality (Gini 25.7, Palma under 1). Thus, BSLI is determined by the magnitude of additive terms, and BSLI declined as additive terms decreased during the high inflation period of 2022–2023.
