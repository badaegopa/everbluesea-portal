§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What It Measures

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic life adequacy (normalized median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. This country's series value: +0.5249 (Grade B · 21st among 139 countries).

The UK's BSLI is determined by large additive terms and a non-negligible H_s (Palma 1.25). Although the international absolute poverty rate is below 1%, domestic research institutes' relative and 'very deep poverty' indicators measure something different.
