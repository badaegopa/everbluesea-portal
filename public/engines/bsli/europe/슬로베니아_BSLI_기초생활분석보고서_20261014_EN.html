§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, Weight 0.40): Average Real Income Index of the 30th–50th Income Percentile (ML Stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Has a lower weight because food crises allow room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H(Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic Life Adequacy (Normalized to Median Income) — Has a higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty Rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma Ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = Basic Life Line. This country's Series value: +0.5624 (Grade B · 19th out of 139 Countries).

In Slovenia, distributional disparity is small (Palma in the 0.8 range), so H_s is close to 0. Consequently, increases in middle-class income translate almost directly into BSLI increases.
