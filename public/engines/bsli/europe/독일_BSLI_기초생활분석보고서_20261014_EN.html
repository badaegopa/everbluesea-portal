§ 01

Executive Summary

Germany stands well above the baseline subsistence line (0) with a series BSLI of +0.7202 (Grade A · 10th among 139 countries). Supplementary Time Series v1.2 records annual empirical measurements from 2016 to 2022, all grading at B: rising from +0.6247 in 2016 to +0.6820 in 2020, then declining to +0.6574 in 2021 and +0.6466 in 2022. The 2023 and 2024 cells are carried over from 2022. The series value (Grade A) is higher than any single year in the time series.

The post-2020 decline was jointly driven by an increase in housing stress (H_s) from 0.0596 → 0.0696 (Palma 1.25 → 1.33), deprivation (D) from 0.0035 → 0.0093 ($3.65/day poverty rate 0.3% → 0.8%), and a drop in food (F_ML) from 0.7654 → 0.7448. Between 2016 and 2020, F_ML and H rose from 0.6942 and 0.6627 to 0.7654 and 0.7315, respectively.

Based on the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate rose from 0.4% in 2019 to 1.6% in 2022, the Gini coefficient increased from 32.2 to 33.7, and the national poverty line (relative poverty risk) poverty rate was 14.8% in 2021. Consumer price inflation shifted from 6.87% in 2022 and 5.95% in 2023 down to 2.17% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Real average income index of the 30th–50th percentile (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises have short-term recovery potential through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (normalized median income) — Higher weight because housing stress involves an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline social subsistence line. This country's series value: +0.7202 (Grade A · 10th among 139 countries).

Germany's BSLI is determined by positive terms, but post-2020 it trended downward as inequality (Palma 1.25 → 1.33) and absolute deprivation expanded simultaneously. Meanwhile, the international standard poverty rate, though modest in baseline scale, quadrupled.
