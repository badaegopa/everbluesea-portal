§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is low.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living satisfaction (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of Jeonse → monthly rent → Gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D(Deductive): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(Deductive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: +0.1755 (Grade D · 39th out of 139 countries).

Poland's BSLI has small deductive terms, so it is determined by the additive terms (F_ML, H). Those additive terms approximately doubled in the single year from 2019 to 2020, dividing the supplementary time series into a Grade D section and a Grade C section. In the same year, the poverty rate and Gini in the World Bank API did not move significantly. The cause of this step increase is not judged in this report but recorded as an anomaly.
