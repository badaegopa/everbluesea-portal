§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid, so the weight is low.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: +0.3308 (Grade C · 30th out of 139 countries).

Portugal has small additive terms within Western Europe, placing it at the bottom of the region. Among the subtractive terms, variations in H_s primarily drive the year-to-year movement of BSLI.
