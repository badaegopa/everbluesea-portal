§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The food crisis has room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse to monthly rent to gosiwon to homelessness, so the weight is high.
D (subtracted): Deprivation index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline of basic life. This country's Series value: +0.0036 (Grade E · 55th out of 139 countries).

The fact that the Series value is closely clinging to the baseline of basic life means that the additive terms and subtractive terms are nearly equal. Since there is no auxiliary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms is outside the scope of this report.
