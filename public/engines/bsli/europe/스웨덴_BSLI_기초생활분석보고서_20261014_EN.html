§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — weighted lower because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (median income normalized) — weighted higher because housing pressure follows an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = daily $3.65 (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline subsistence line. This country's Series value: +0.6877 (Grade B · 14th out of 139 countries).

Sweden's BSLI is determined by additive terms, but in years when inequality expanded (Palma 1.18) such as 2022, the subtractive terms shifted noticeably. Because it is measured empirically each year, this fluctuation is directly reflected in the time series.
