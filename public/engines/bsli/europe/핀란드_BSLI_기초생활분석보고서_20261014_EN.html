§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline livelihood fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: +0.7117 (Grade A · 11th out of 139 countries).

Finland shows both small inequality (Palma 0.96–1.00) and absolute deprivation, so BSLI reflects the magnitude of the additive terms as is. The increase in unemployment (2022–2025) lies outside the supplementary time series.
