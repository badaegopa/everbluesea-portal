§ 01

Executive Summary

Albania is just above the basic livelihood line (0) with a Series BSLI of +0.1044 (Grade D · 44th out of 139 countries). The auxiliary time series v1.2 shows annual empirical measurements from 2016 to 2020, all within Grade E: rising from +0.0078 in 2016 and +0.0135 in 2017 to +0.0660 in 2018, and reaching +0.0844 in 2020. The 2021 and 2022 cells are carried over from 2020, and 2023 and 2024 have missing data. The Series value (Grade D) is higher than any year in the time series.

The increase from 2017 to 2018 was jointly driven by the decrease in deprivation (D) from 0.0340 to 0.0223 ($3.65/day poverty rate 2.9% → 1.9%) and housing stress (H_s) from 0.0603 to 0.0379 (Palma 1.26 → 1.08), alongside the increase in food (F_ML) from 0.1093 to 0.1292. In 2020, F_ML and H were 0.1371 and 0.1295, respectively.

According to the Hermes collection (2026-10-14) based on the World Bank API, the 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate dropped from 1.4% in 2017 to 0.3% in 2020, the $8.30 rate from 32.7% to 19.9%, and the Gini index from 33.1 to 29.4, while the national poverty line rate went from 21.8% in 2019 to 19.7% in 2022. The unemployment rate (ILO modeled) stood at 10.7–10.9% for 2022–2025, and consumer prices rose by 2.15% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Given that food crises offer room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized median income) — Because housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, the weight is high.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Series value for this country: +0.1044 (Grade D · 44th out of 139 countries).

Albania has low additive terms (F_ML and H around 0.13), and in 2016–2017, the subtractive terms (D + H_s around 0.10) offset most of them, placing BSLI near 0. In 2018, as disparity and deprivation decreased, BSLI increased.
