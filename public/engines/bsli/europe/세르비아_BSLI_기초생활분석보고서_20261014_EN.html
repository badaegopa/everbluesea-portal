§ 01

Executive Summary

Serbia stands just above the basic living line (0) with a Series BSLI of +0.0586 (Grade E · 50th out of 139 countries). The supplementary time series v1.2 features annual empirical measurements from 2016 to 2023: from -0.1111 in 2016 and -0.0491 in 2017, it crossed the basic living line to +0.1113 (Grade D) in 2018, dipped to +0.0752 in 2020, and reached +0.1457 in 2023. The 2024 slot is carried forward from 2023. The Series value is lower than the recent time series values and is closer to the 2020 value.

The substantial rise from 2016 to 2018 stemmed from a joint, significant reduction in Deprivation (D) 0.1112 → 0.0047 ($3.65/day poverty rate 9.6% → 0.4%) and Housing Stress (H_s) 0.1261 → 0.0421 (Palma 1.72 → 1.12). In 2020, H_s increased again to 0.0835 (Palma 1.43), while through 2023 Food (F_ML) 0.1646 → 0.2266 and Housing (H) 0.1539 → 0.2228 rose.

As of the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate dropped from 12.6% in 2020 to 9% in 2023, and the national poverty line (at-risk-of-poverty rate) declined from 21.4% to 19.7%. Consumer price inflation moved from 11.98% in 2022 and 12.37% in 2023 to 3.89% in 2025, while the unemployment rate (ILO model) shifted from 8.44% to 7.12%.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is lower because food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (median income normalized) — Weight is higher because housing pressure is an irreversible sequence: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtractive): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's series value: +0.0586 (Grade E · 50th out of 139 countries).

Serbia's BSLI was heavily swayed by the movement of the subtractive terms (D, H_s). In 2016–2018, both subtractive terms declined together, crossing the basic living line, but dropped in 2020 as H_s rose again. The Series value sits at the lower end of this oscillation.
