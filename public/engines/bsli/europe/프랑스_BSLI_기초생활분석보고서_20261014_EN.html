§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's compendium value: +0.6361 (Grade B · 16th among 139 countries).

France's BSLI barely moved from 2016 to 2023 — both additive terms and gaps remained stable.
