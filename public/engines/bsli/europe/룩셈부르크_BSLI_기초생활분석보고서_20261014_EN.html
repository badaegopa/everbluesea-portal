§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized median income) — The weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtracted): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtracted): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Compendium value for this country: +0.8993 (Grade A · 2nd out of 139 countries).

In countries like Luxembourg that set the upper bound of normalization, additive terms cannot rise further (1.0), so changes in BSLI stem solely from the subtracted terms. Relative poverty (17.3%) barely appears in the absolute-standard D term.
