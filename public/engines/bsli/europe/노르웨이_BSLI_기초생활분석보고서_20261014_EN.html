§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: +0.9254 (Grade A · 1st out of 139 Countries).

Norway's BSLI is almost entirely determined by the additive terms. The 12.2% from the national poverty line (relative standard) barely appears in the absolute standard D term of BSLI ($3.65-a-day poverty rate 0.2%).
