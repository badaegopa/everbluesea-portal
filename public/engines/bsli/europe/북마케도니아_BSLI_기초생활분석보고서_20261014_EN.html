§ 01

Executive Summary

North Macedonia, with a Series BSLI of +0.0019 (Grade E · 56th out of 139 countries), clings just above the basic living line (0). In the supplementary time series v1.2, empirical measurements were conducted annually from 2016 to 2019: from -0.0344 in 2016 and -0.0171 in 2017, it crossed above the basic living line to +0.0209 in 2018, and reached +0.0304 in 2019. The 2020 and 2021 cells are carried forward from 2019, while data is absent for 2022–2024. The Series value is lower than the recent time-series value.

The increase was driven jointly by a decline in deprivation (D) from 0.0923 to 0.0670 ($3.65/day poverty rate from 8.0% to 5.8%), increases in food (F_ML) from 0.1299 to 0.1552 and housing (H) from 0.1324 to 0.1599, and a reduction in housing stress (H_s) from 0.0734 to 0.0607 (Palma 1.36 to 1.26).

Based on the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate declined from 27% in 2016 to 20.5% in 2019, while the poverty rate according to the national poverty line (at-risk-of-poverty rate) moved from 23% in 2020 to 21.9% in 2023. The unemployment rate (ILO modeled estimate) dropped from 14.48% in 2022 to 12.26% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weighted lower because food crises offer room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Weighted higher because housing stress follows an irreversible sequence of lump-sum lease (jeonse) → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: +0.0019 (Grade E · 56th out of 139 Countries).

In North Macedonia, the additive and subtractive terms nearly balance each other out, keeping the BSLI clinging to 0. Although all four terms moved in a direction that raised the BSLI during 2016–2019, the absence of empirical measurements after 2020 leaves the shifts during the high-inflation period (14.2% in 2022) unreflected in the time series.
