§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living standard line. This country's Series value: +0.8020 (Grade A · 5th out of 139 countries).

Iceland's BSLI comes almost entirely from the additive terms (F_ML · H). Since the subtractive terms are close to 0, changes in BSLI trace changes in the middle stratum's income level (rising in 2017, declining in 2018).
