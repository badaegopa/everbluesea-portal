§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises allow room for short-term recovery via reserves and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure constitutes an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Daily $3.65 (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: +0.2679 (Grade D · 35th out of 139 countries).

Latvia ranks lowest in the region as its additive terms (F_ML, H) are the smallest within Northern Europe. Its subtractive terms are small (daily $3.65 poverty rate 0.6%, H_s 0.0712).
