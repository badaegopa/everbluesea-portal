§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — The weight is low because food crises offer room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (normalized median income) — The weight is high because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtractive): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline living threshold. Series value for this country: +0.4612 (Grade C · 26th out of 139 countries).

Spain's BSLI is determined by additive terms, and among subtractive terms, H_s is larger than D. As disparities gradually narrowed from 2017 to 2023, the BSLI rose. National poverty lines and 'poverty and social exclusion' indicators are relative and composite criteria, measuring something different from the BSLI's absolute-standard D term.
