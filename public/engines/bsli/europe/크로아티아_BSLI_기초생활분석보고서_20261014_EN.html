Croatia is above the basic living line (0) with a Series BSLI of +0.3177 (Grade C · 31st out of 139 countries). § 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: +0.3177 (Grade C · 31st out of 139 countries).

Croatia's BSLI reflects a structure that moved up from Grade D to Grade C as additive terms expanded year by year. Although subtraction terms are small, the poverty rate at risk of relative poverty actually increased from 2023 to 2024.
