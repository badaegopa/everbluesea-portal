§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-term subtraction formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure has a high weight because it forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: +0.2845 (Grade D · 34th out of 139 countries).

Hungary's BSLI is determined by the additive terms, while the subtractive terms are small. Since international distribution statistics stop in 2017, changes during the high-inflation period of 2022–2023 are absent from the supplementary time series. The reason why the compendium value is higher than the time series lies outside the scope of this report.
