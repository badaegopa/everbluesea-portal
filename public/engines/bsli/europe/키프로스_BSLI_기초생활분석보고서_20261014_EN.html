§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence the lower weight.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood fulfillment (median income normalized) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D(Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) has been treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's Series value: +0.5501 (Grade B · 20th among 139 countries).

Cyprus's BSLI is determined by the additive terms, while the subtractive terms are small. The national poverty line is a relative line at 60% of median income, measuring something different from the absolute benchmark D term of BSLI.
