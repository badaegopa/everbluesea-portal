§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid, hence the lower weight.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Series value for this country: +0.2425 (Grade D · 36th out of 139 countries).

Greece is a country that has risen within Grade D as its additive terms grew slightly each year. The subtractive terms are small. The national poverty line and 'subjective poverty' are relative and perceived standards, measuring something different from BSLI's absolute D term.
