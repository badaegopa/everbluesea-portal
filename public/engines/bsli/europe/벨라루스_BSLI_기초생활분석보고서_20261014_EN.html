§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, thus assigned a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic livelihood fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure entails an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence assigned a higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Series value for this country: +0.2263 (Grade D · 37th among 139 countries).

Belarus has minimal subtractive terms due to low disparity and absolute deprivation; it is a country where additive terms (F_ML and H around 0.2) reflecting the income level of the grassroots stratum determine the BSLI. The increase from 2016 to 2020 also stemmed from the rise in additive terms.
