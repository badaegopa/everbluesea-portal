Tajikistan is below the basic livelihood line (0) with a Compendium BSLI of -0.3229 (Grade E · 87th out of 139 countries). § 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food [食], weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves/aid, hence lower weight.
H(Housing [住], weight 0.60): Basic livelihood fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: -0.3229 (Grade E · 87th out of 139 countries).

Because Tajikistan has small additive terms (F_ML · H), its BSLI structure fluctuates significantly with movements in the subtractive terms D and H_s. As in 2022, if the distributional gap widens sharply in a single year, BSLI decreases even if deprivation declines.
