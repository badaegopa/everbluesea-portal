§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Given short-term recovery room via stockpiles and aid during food crises, the weight is low.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D(subtractive): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.1970 (Grade E · 78th out of 139 countries).

Kyrgyzstan's BSLI is heavily dominated by the deprivation (D) term. In 2020, as D increased, BSLI dropped, and during 2021–2024, as D declined, it rose. The national poverty line (24%) is set much higher than the international extreme poverty line (2.2%).
