§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Standard Text (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Given room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid during food crises, the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (median income normalized) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. Compendium value for this country: -0.0464 (Grade E · 61st out of 139 countries).

Even after deprivation at $3.65 a day reached 0, China's BSLI remains near the basic life line because the additive terms (F_ML, H) are small. The gap between the national poverty line (0%) and the upper-middle-income line (21.3%) is substantial.
