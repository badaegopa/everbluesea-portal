§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life satisfaction degree (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is higher.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic life line. This country's Series value: +0.5020 (Grade B · 22nd out of 139 countries).

In countries with low absolute poverty like Japan, the D term is small, so BSLI is predominantly determined by F_ML and H (middle stratum income levels). Therefore, relative deprivation (disparity within the same society) is only partially reflected in BSLI via H_s (Palma) — this is the point of divergence from the government's relative poverty rate.

§ 05

Public Livelihood Situation and Chronology

2021 (Income Basis)
Relative Poverty Rate 15.4% — 2022 Comprehensive Survey of Living Conditions, poverty line annual disposable income of 1.27 million yen. Children 11.5%, single-parent households 44.5%. Down from 16.1% in 2012 (reports citing Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare data).
2024
Decline in Real Wages — In April, down −0.7% year-on-year, marking 25 consecutive months of decline (longest since 1991). In fiscal year 2024, down −0.5%, declining for three consecutive years (previous year −2.2%) (WAM·Nation Thailand).
2025-02~05
Rice Prices and Reserve Rice — The government released reserve rice for the first time citing distribution disruptions (initial plan: 210,000 tons). In March, the average supermarket price was 4,172 yen per 5 kg, roughly double the previous year. Rebounded after the first decline in 18 weeks in May (Tribune·nippon.com·Asia News Network).

Food (食) Pressure. The 2025 surge in rice prices was reported alongside grocery inflation as a driver of declining real wages. Analyses noted that the reserve rice release policy was implemented without effectiveness evaluation metrics (nippon.com).

Time Lag with the Auxiliary Time Series. Because Japan's measured data in BSLI Time Series v1.2 extends only up to 2020, the 2024–2025 price and rice price shocks are not reflected in the engine values.

§ 05-B

Published Figures vs. Independent Sources — Examining the Gaps
