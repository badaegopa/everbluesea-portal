§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Baseline living adequacy (normalized median income) — Housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline living threshold. Compendium value for this country: +0.4627 (Grade C · 25th out of 139 countries).

Like high-income countries, the Republic of Korea has an almost negligible D term, with its BSLI determined by middle-stratum income and distributional disparity. Relative poverty such as elderly poverty rarely manifests in the absolute-criterion D term of BSLI.
