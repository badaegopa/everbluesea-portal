§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Authentic Text (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises carry lower weight because they retain room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure carries higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtracted): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.0596 (Grade E · 63rd of 139 countries).

In countries with low F_ML and H like Mongolia, additive terms are small, so even slight shifts in D and H_s cause BSLI to cross above and below the basic life line. The decrease in D from 2016 to 2022 is the primary driver flipping the sign in the supplementary time series.

§ 05

Public Livelihood Situation and Chronology

2016~2020
Slowdown in Poverty Reduction — Poverty reduction slowed significantly following the 2016 economic downturn. Official poverty rate in 2020 was 27.8% (0.6%p lower than 2018) (World Bank 2020 Poverty Report).
2022
Resetting the National Poverty Line — 418,090 tugriks per month per adult equivalent (40% higher than the 2010 line). National poverty rate stood at 27.1% based on the 2022 Household Socio-Economic Survey. Inflation peaked at 16.9% in June (World Bank).
2023~2024
Inflation Decline — 9% in October 2023 (National Statistics Office), 7.9% in December, and 7.0% nationwide / 7.2% in the capital in February 2024. Upper ceiling of the central bank target is 8% (ECRC · World Bank).

Another Face of Housing (住) Pressure. Ger district residents in the capital rely predominantly on coal stoves for heating, which is reported as the principal source of winter air pollution whose harm falls concentratedly on low-income residents. Over 800 carbon monoxide poisoning deaths were reported between 2018 and 2025 (Earth Journalism Network). This pressure is not directly incorporated into income-based BSLI terms.

Time Lag with Supplementary Time Series. Because Mongolian actual measurements extend only up to 2022, the 2023–2024 inflation decline is not reflected in the engine values.
