§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.6223 (Grade E · 109th of 139 Countries).

In countries like Bangladesh where additive terms (F_ML, H) are small, BSLI is almost entirely determined by the deprivation term D. Therefore, changes in the $3.65/day poverty rate account for most of the variation in BSLI.
