Executive Summary

Bhutan has a Country Book BSLI of -0.1396 (Grade E · 73rd out of 139 countries), placing it below the baseline subsistence line (0). However, the empirical measurements in the auxiliary Time Series v1.2 consist of two points—2017 (-0.0841) and 2022 (+0.1429)—and the 2022 value is Grade D, above the baseline subsistence line—differing in sign from the Country Book value (Canonical Verification Needed C-1). The remaining cells are carried forward, and 2020 lacks data. The reference value is the Country Book value.

Based on the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate from the World Bank API decreased from 42.5% in 2017 to 9% in 2022, and the Gini coefficient dropped from 37.4 to 28.5. The national poverty line poverty rate stood at 12.4% in 2022. In 2026, reports on the cost-of-living burden on the working poor and rising urban rents were collected.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline subsistence line. This country's Country Book value: -0.1396 (Grade E · 73rd out of 139 countries).

Bhutan is a rare case where all four terms moved in a direction that increased BSLI between 2017 and 2022. Yet the cross-sectional value in the Country Book is negative — the difference in calculation criteria between the two windows changes the sign for this country.
