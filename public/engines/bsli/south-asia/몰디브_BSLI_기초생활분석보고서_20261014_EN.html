§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Text (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (median income normalized) — Housing pressure has a high weight because it forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtractive): Deprivation Index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic subsistence line. Compendium value for this country: +0.1677 (Grade D · 40th out of 139 countries).

Maldives's subtractive terms are small. The $3.65-a-day poverty was 0.0% in 2019, and the Palma ratio was 1.05. Instead, housing pressure that is not captured by income percentiles, such as overcrowded housing in the capital Malé, appears in published data — because BSLI's H is based on median income, it does not directly measure floor space or rental burden.

Livelihood Situation and Chronology

2016
National Poverty Line — Household Income and Expenditure Survey (HIES 2016): National poverty line = half of median total expenditure ($4.79/day), poverty rate 8.2% (citing National Bureau of Statistics reports).
2019
Poverty Rate — World Bank API: National poverty line 5.4%, $8.30-a-day (2021 PPP) 11.2%, Gini 29.3 (Hermes collection 2026-10-14). 93% of the poor reside in atolls outside the capital (World Bank).
2024~2025
Subsidy Reform — 2024 Budget: Plan to convert blanket subsidies for electricity, fuel, staple food, and water into direct transfers postponed to April 2025. World Bank API inflation: 1.41% in 2024, 4.01% in 2025 (Hermes collection).
2025-12
Malé Housing Regulations — New housing regulations and '22,900 units on paper' (Maldives Independent), rent-to-own housing applications for those with monthly income under MVR 60,000 (Edition.mv) — Hermes collection headlines.
2026
Middle East Conflict and Inflation — 'Inflation could reach 6% amid Middle East conflict: World Bank' (Edition.mv, 2026-06-22), 'World Bank Maldives Development Update 2026' (2026-06-11), 'Essential Goods Price Index September 2026 up 7.79% YoY' (IndexBox, 2026-10-11) — Hermes collection headlines.

Housing pressure. Ministry of Housing data stated that approximately 42% of the population lives in 9.3 km² in Malé, and 17,073 households (of which 11,951 are migrant households from outer atolls) live in spaces under 600 square feet. The main driver of poverty in Malé is analyzed to be housing shortage and overcrowding (World Bank).
