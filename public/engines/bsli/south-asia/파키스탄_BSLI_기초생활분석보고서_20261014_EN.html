§ 01

Executive Summary

Pakistan is below the basic living line (0) with a compendium BSLI of -0.4598 (Grade E · 96th out of 139 countries). The empirical measurements of the supplementary time series v1.2 are 2018 (-0.3758) and 2024 (-0.4665), with the remaining cells being carried over from adjacent empirical measurements (the 2016 cell uses 2015 data), while 2021 has data absence. Between the two empirical measurements, BSLI declined, and the compendium value is close to the 2024 value.

The decline was jointly driven by an increase in deprivation (D) from 0.3693 → 0.4280 (poverty rate at $3.65/day from 31.9% → 37.0%), an increase in housing stress (H_s) from 0.0426 → 0.0726 (Palma 1.12 → 1.35), and decreases in food (F_ML) from 0.0392 → 0.0360 and housing (H) from 0.0342 → 0.0327 — all four terms moved in a direction that lowers BSLI.

Based on the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), according to the World Bank API, the 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate rose from 16.5% in 2018 to 23% in 2024, the national poverty line rate rose from 22.2% to 28.9%, and the Gini coefficient rose from 29.6 to 33.5. Consumer price index has no value in the API, and the unemployment rate (ILO model) is around 5.4%.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.4598 (Grade E · 96th out of 139 countries).

Pakistan is a country where all four terms moved in the direction of lowering BSLI between 2018 and 2024. During the same period, both the international extreme poverty line and the national poverty line rose together, showing no divergence in direction across measurement standards.
