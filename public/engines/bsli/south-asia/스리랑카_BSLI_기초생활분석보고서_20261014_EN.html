§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index for 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (normalized median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.2457 (Grade E · 81st out of 139 countries).

In Sri Lanka, deprivation (D) and distributional disparity (H_s) pull down BSLI by comparable magnitudes. With the supplementary time series stopping at 2019, life changes during the 2022 economic crisis and 49.72% inflation period are absent from the time series.
