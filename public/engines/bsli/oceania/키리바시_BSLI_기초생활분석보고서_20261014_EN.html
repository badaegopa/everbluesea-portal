§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(食, Weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have lower weight because they leave room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H(住, Weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure carries high weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(Subtractive): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(Subtractive): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
衣 (Clothing) is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.1944 (Grade E · 77th out of 139 countries).

A compendium value below the basic living line indicates that the subtractive terms (D, H_s) outweigh the additive terms. In the absence of supplementary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms falls outside the scope of this report. The 2023 Gini coefficient of 24.7 is an international figure indicating a small distributional gap (on the H_s side).
