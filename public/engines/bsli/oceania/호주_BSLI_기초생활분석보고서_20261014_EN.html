§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — lower weight as food crises allow short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment rate (normalized median income) — higher weight as housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Compendium value: +0.6538 (Grade B · 15th among 139 countries).

Australia's BSLI is driven by additive terms. The official national poverty line value is not in the World Bank API, and research institutes' relative poverty estimates (1 in 5 children) measure something different from BSLI's absolute-standard D term.
