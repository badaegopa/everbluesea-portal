§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Text (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: +0.6538 (Grade B · 15th out of 139 countries).

Australia's BSLI is driven by additive terms. National official poverty line values are absent from the World Bank API, and research institutes' relative poverty estimates (1 in 5 children) measure something different from the absolute criterion D term of BSLI.
