§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(食/Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence lower weight.
H(住/Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment (median income normalized) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of Jeonse → monthly rent → Gosiwon → homelessness, hence higher weight.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
衣 (Clothing) is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = Basic living line. This country's Compendium value: -0.1833 (Grade E · 75th of 139 countries).

A Compendium value below the basic living line indicates that the subtraction terms (D·H_s) exceed the addition terms. Because auxiliary time series data are absent, the exact values of each of the four terms remain outside the scope of this report. The poverty rate under the national poverty line rose between 2013 and 2018.
