§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (normalized median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade of Jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.0646 (Grade E · 64th out of 139 countries).

That the Series value lies just below the basic living line indicates that the subtraction terms (D, H_s) are slightly larger than the addition terms. In the absence of an auxiliary time series, the individual magnitudes of the four terms remain outside the scope of this report. The cash transfers begun in late 2025 occurred after the computation of the Series value.

§ 05-B

Official Figures vs Independent Sources — Observing the Discrepancies

The discrepancy in the Marshall Islands lies in the scarcity of published figures themselves. Poverty statistics exist only for the single year of 2019, and inflation statistics are absent from international databases. For the same year, 2019, the national line measures poverty at 7.2%, whereas the 2021 PPP $8.30 a day measures it at 36.5%. Living conditions thereafter are recorded solely through reports on the introduction of basic income. Published figures are preserved verbatim (DECISIONS 17·19) and placed side by side.
