§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(食, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have lower weight because there is room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H(住, weight 0.60): Baseline living satisfaction (median income normalized) — Housing pressure has higher weight because it is an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtracted): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtracted): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
衣(clothing) is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline social life line. This country's Series value: -0.1347 (Grade E · 71st of 139 countries).

The fact that the Series value is below the baseline social life line means that the subtracted terms (D, H_s) are larger than the additive terms. In the absence of a supplementary time series, the individual sizes of the four terms remain outside the scope of this report. The 2021 Gini coefficient of 27.1 is an international figure demonstrating small distribution disparity.
