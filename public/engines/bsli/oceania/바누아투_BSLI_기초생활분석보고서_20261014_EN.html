§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (subtracted): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.4177 (Grade E · 95th among 139 countries).

The Series value being below the basic living line indicates that the subtracted terms (D, H_s) are larger than the additive terms. In the absence of a supplementary time series, the individual magnitudes of the four terms remain outside the scope of this report. The 2019 $8.30 poverty rate of 81.3% is the last international figure showing that the additive terms would be small.
