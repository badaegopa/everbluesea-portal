§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is higher.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.5818 (Grade E · 105th out of 139 countries).

The Series value being below the basic living line indicates that the subtraction terms (D, H_s) are larger than the addition terms. In the absence of an auxiliary time series, the magnitude of each of the four terms falls outside the scope of this report.
