Executive Summary

Fiji is below the basic living line (0) with a Compendium BSLI of -0.1200 (Grade E · 68th out of 139 countries). The empirical measurement in auxiliary time series v1.2 is a single point in 2019 (-0.0958); the cells for 2017–2021 are carried forward from this value, and data are absent for 2016 and 2022–2024. Consumer price inflation was 3.89% in 2024 and −1.38% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — Weight is high because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Compendium value: -0.1200 (Grade E · 68th out of 139 countries).

Fiji's BSLI is determined by Deprivation (D) and small additive terms. From 2013 to 2019, the Gini coefficient decreased, but the population below the upper-middle-income line increased — narrowing inequality and expanding poverty occurred over the same period.
