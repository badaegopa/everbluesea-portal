§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Text (Four-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic subsistence fulfillment (median income normalization) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline subsistence line. This country's Compendium value: +0.2964 (Grade D · 33rd out of 139 countries).

Israel's BSLI is determined jointly by large additive terms and a relatively large H_s. The National Insurance Institute's poverty line is a relative poverty line based on median income, measuring something different from the BSLI's absolute standard D term.
