§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is low.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalization) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the high weight.
D(subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: -0.2649 (Grade E · 83rd out of 139 countries).

In countries like Egypt where the additive terms are small, changes in D and H_s significantly shift BSLI. The annual inflation of 14–34% over the 3 years following the 2021 actual measurement will not be reflected in BSLI until income percentile data is updated.
