§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have lower weight due to short-term recovery headroom via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure carries higher weight as it is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = daily $3.65 (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.2649 (Grade E · 83rd out of 139 countries).

In countries like Egypt where additive terms are small, changes in D and H_s significantly shift the BSLI. Annual inflation of 14–34% during the three years following the 2021 actual measurement will not be reflected in BSLI until income decile data is updated.
