§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight because room exists for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life adequacy (normalized to median income) — Housing pressure carries a higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.0150 (Grade E · 58th out of 139 countries).

Türkiye's BSLI hovers near 0 as large H_s (0.17–0.23) nearly cancels out the additive terms. The large increase in the additive terms in 2023 appeared during a year of 50%-range inflation — a point where the divergence between PPP-converted income and perceived inflation can be measured (recorded without interpretation).
