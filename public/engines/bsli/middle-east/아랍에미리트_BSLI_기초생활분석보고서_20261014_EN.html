§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is lower because food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Degree of meeting basic living needs (normalized median income) — Housing pressure has an irreversible sequence from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Series value for this country: +0.8472 (Grade A · 3rd out of 139 countries).

The United Arab Emirates exhibits a composition where additive terms approach the BSLI upper limit (+1.0) and subtractive terms approach 0. However, because empirical time-series measurements exist only for the single year of 2018, annual trends cannot be read through engine values.
