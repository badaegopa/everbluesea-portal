§ 01

Executive Summary

Georgia is below the basic living line (0) with a Compendium BSLI of -0.2697 (Grade E · 85th among 139 countries). The supplementary time series v1.2 consists entirely of empirical measurements for 2016–2024: it rose for 4 consecutive years, passing -0.2418 in 2016 and -0.2294 in 2020 to reach -0.0703 in 2024. The compendium value is lower than the recent time-series value and is near the 2017 value (-0.2579).

The rise came from a decrease in deprivation (D) 0.2096 → 0.0839 ($3.65/day poverty rate 18.1% → 7.2%) and increases in food (F_ML) 0.0579 → 0.0873 and housing (H) 0.0568 → 0.0833. Housing stress (H_s) decreased from 0.1009 to 0.0714 (Palma 1.55 → 1.34).

According to the World Bank API based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the national poverty line poverty rate dropped from 17.5% in 2021 to 9.4% in 2024, and the 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate fell from 10.8% to 4.2%. Over the same period, the unemployment rate (ILO modeled) remained in the 11–12% range.

§ 02

BSLI Four-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (Four-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is low because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (median income normalized) — Weight is high because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse→monthly rent→gosiwon→homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Compendium value for this country: -0.2697 (Grade E · 85th among 139 countries).

Georgia's BSLI is heavily dominated by deprivation (D). Although BSLI increased as D decreased, additive terms remain small, keeping it below the basic living line. A characteristic of this country's figures is that while the poverty rate falls significantly, the unemployment rate hovers around 12%.
