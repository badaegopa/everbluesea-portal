§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weighted lower because food crises offer room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment rate (normalized median income) — Weighted higher because housing stress follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtracted): Deprivation index = Poverty rate at $3.65 a day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. Compendium value for this country: +0.0407 (Grade E · 52nd out of 139 countries).

Tunisia exhibits a configuration where the increase in additive terms is almost entirely offset by the increase in the subtractive term H_s (distribution disparity). The $3.65-a-day poverty rate remains low at 1.4%.

§ 05-B
