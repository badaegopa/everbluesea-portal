§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, so weight is lower.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain from jeonse to monthly rent to gosiwon to homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D(subtracted): Deprivation index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtracted): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.1859 (Grade E · 76th out of 139 countries).

The fact that the Series value lies below the basic living line indicates that the subtraction terms (D, H_s) outweigh the addition terms. In the absence of an auxiliary time series, the individual magnitudes of the four terms remain outside the scope of this report. The 2013 Gini coefficient of 39.5 is the last international figure showing that the distributional gap (on the H_s side) was not insignificant.
