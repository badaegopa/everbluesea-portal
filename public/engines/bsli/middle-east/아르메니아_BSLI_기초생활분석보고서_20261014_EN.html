§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight as there is room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic life satisfaction degree (normalized median income) — Housing pressure carries a high weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: Jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.0163 (Grade E · 59th out of 139 countries).

In Armenia, the additive terms are small (F_ML·H 0.07–0.09) and the subtractive terms (D·H_s) are of comparable magnitude, causing the BSLI to hover near 0. Post-2018, the gap (Palma 1.43 → 0.97) narrowed, bringing it closer to the baseline subsistence line.
