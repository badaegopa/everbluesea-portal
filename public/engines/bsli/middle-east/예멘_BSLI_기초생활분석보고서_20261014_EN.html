§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure constitutes an irreversible chain: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Compendium value: -0.7021 (Grade E · 113th out of 139 countries).

Yemen features an additive term close to 0 and a large deprivation term. However, because the raw data originates from a pre-conflict survey (2014), subsequent conditions are absent from the engine values.
