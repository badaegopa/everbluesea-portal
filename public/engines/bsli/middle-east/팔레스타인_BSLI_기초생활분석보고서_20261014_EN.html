Executive Summary

Palestine stands at a series BSLI of +0.0231 (Grade E · 53rd out of 139 countries), just above the basic livelihood line (0). The measured years in supplementary time series v1.2 are 2016 (+0.0229) and 2023 (-0.0247); the slots for 2017–2018 and 2021–2024 are carried over, while 2019–2020 lack data. Consumer price inflation stood at 5.87% in 2023, 53.67% in 2024, and 9.81% in 2025, while the unemployment rate (ILO model) remained in the 24–26% range during 2019–2022. GNI per capita (Atlas) fell from $4,720 in 2022 to $2,980 in 2024.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, Weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight because room exists for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid.
H (Housing, Weight 0.60): Basic livelihood adequacy (median income normalized) — Housing pressure carries a higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtracted): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtracted): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was removed from the variables and treated as a constant due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic livelihood line. This country's series value: +0.0231 (Grade E · 53rd out of 139 countries).

Palestine's series BSLI reflects the level of 2016 data. In the 2023 measurement, both D and H_s increased, falling below the basic livelihood line, and shifts during the wartime period from 2024 onward are absent from the supplementary time series. The divergence in living conditions between the West Bank and Gaza is also blended into a single national aggregate value.

The same poverty is measured at 32.8% by the national poverty line and at 28.6% by the 2021 PPP $8.30/day threshold (2023). Official figures are presented verbatim (DECISIONS 17·19) side by side.
