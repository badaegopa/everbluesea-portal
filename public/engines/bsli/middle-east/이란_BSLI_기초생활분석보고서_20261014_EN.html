Iran stands below the basic living line (0) with a compendium BSLI of -0.1301 (Grade E · 70th among 139 countries). Consumer price inflation was 43.49% in 2022, 44.58% in 2023, 32.46% in 2024, and 42.17% in 2025. In 2026, reports of expanding poverty amid war and sanctions and UN estimates were collected.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Standard Edition (4-Term Subtractive Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure forms an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.1301 (Grade E · 70th among 139 countries).

Iran's BSLI is determined by annual cross-movements between deprivation (D) and additive terms. Even amid inflation of 30–45% for four consecutive years, the measured BSLI rose through 2023 — capturing the divergence between measured income distribution and perceived inflation. There are no actual measurements after 2024.
