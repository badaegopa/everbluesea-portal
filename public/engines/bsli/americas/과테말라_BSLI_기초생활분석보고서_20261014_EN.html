§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (normalized median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of key money deposit → monthly rent → single-room occupancy → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's series value: -0.5607 (Grade E · 102nd among 139 countries).

In Guatemala, disparity (H_s) and deprivation (D) jointly pull down the BSLI, with H_s exerting a greater effect. Between 2014 and 2023, disparity decreased, but poverty rates at the extreme poverty line and upper-middle-income line rose slightly — the two subtraction terms moved in opposite directions.
