§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises carry potential for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — housing pressure represents an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's series value: -0.1500 (Grade E · 74th out of 139 countries).

In Panama, H_s is 0.37–0.44, larger than the additive terms (F_ML and H around 0.17–0.21), placing BSLI below the basic living line. The annual fluctuation of BSLI closely mirrors movements in Palma (3.05–3.39).
