§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Given that food crises offer room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, its weight is lower.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (median income normalized) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible chain from jeonse to monthly rent, gosiwon, and homelessness, hence its weight is higher.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = Poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: +0.0848 (Grade E · 48th out of 139 countries).

Uruguay's BSLI features a composition where the addition terms contend with H_s. Deprivation at $3.65/day (0.4%) is small, meaning the D term exerts almost no impact.
