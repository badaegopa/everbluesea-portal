§ 01

Executive Summary

Peru has a Series BSLI of -0.3434 (Grade E · 90th out of 139 countries), lying below the basic living line (0). Auxiliary time series v1.2 consists of annual actual measurements from 2016 to 2024: rising from -0.2364 in 2016 to -0.1560 in 2019, plunging to -0.3126 in 2020, recovering to -0.1471 in 2021, and reaching -0.1358 in 2024. The Series value is lower even than the 2020 value, the lowest point in the time series.

The 2020 plunge was jointly driven by an increase in Deprivation (D) 0.0927 → 0.1732 ($3.65/day poverty rate 8.0% → 15.0%), a decline in Food (F_ML) 0.1086 → 0.0770, and an increase in Housing Stress (H_s) 0.1744 → 0.2191 (Palma 2.02 → 2.28). In 2024, the subtraction terms are D 0.0901 and H_s 0.1496 (Palma 1.87).

Based on Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API's 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate was 5.2% in 2021, 5.9% in 2023, and 5.1% in 2024; the $8.30 rate declined from 39.5% to 36.2%; and the national poverty line rate decreased from 29% in 2023 to 25.7% in 2025. The Gini index is 40.1, and consumer inflation dropped from 8.33% in 2022 to 1.53% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — housing pressure entails an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.3434 (Grade E · 90th out of 139 countries).

In Peru, H_s (approx. 0.15) and D (approx. 0.09) exceed the addition terms (F_ML · H approx. 0.10), keeping the BSLI below the basic living line. In 2020, D nearly doubled, dropping the BSLI by 0.16 in a single year, before rebounding in 2021.
