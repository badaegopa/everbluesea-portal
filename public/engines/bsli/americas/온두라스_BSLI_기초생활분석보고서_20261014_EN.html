§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have lower weight because there is room for short-term recovery through stockpiling and aid.
H(Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment degree (median income normalized) — Housing pressure has higher weight because it is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Compendium value: -0.5731 (Grade E · 103rd of 139 countries).

Honduras is a country where H_s is larger than D among subtraction terms (Palma 2.5 or higher). The rise in BSLI from 2016 to 2024 came most significantly from the reduction in the distribution gap.
