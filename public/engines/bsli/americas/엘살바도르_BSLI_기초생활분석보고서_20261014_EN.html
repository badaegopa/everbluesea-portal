§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is low.
H (housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (subtraction): housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.1060 (Grade E · 66th out of 139 countries).

El Salvador has a structure where positive terms and negative terms nearly offset each other, and H_s is greater than D. Between 2021 and 2023, the national poverty line poverty rate rose while the $8.30 poverty rate fell — moving in opposite directions across the two standards over the same period.
