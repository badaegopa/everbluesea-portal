§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, hence a lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — Housing pressure forms an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence a higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and removed from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.2665 (Grade E · 84th out of 139 countries).

In Mexico, among the subtraction terms, H_s (distributional disparity) is greater than D (absolute deprivation). A substantial portion of the BSLI increase from 2016 to 2024 stemmed from the reduction in H_s.
