§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through reserves and aid, so the weight is low.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living sufficiency (median income normalized) — housing pressure is an irreversible cascade of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s (Subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's Series value: -0.3679 (Grade E · 93rd out of 139 countries).

Ecuador's BSLI is determined by a high H_s (0.24–0.31). The additive terms (F_ML and H, each 0.11–0.13) are at a moderate level for Latin America, but the gap around a Palma ratio of 2.5 drags BSLI down below the basic living line.
