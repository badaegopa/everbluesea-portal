§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Weight is lower because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living fulfillment (median income normalized) — Weight is higher because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: +0.7012 (Grade A · 12th out of 139 countries).

Canada's BSLI is almost entirely determined by the additive terms. Over 10% under the official poverty line (Market Basket Measure) barely appears in the absolute-standard D term of BSLI.
