§ 01

Executive Summary

Colombia stands deeply below the basic living baseline (0) with a Compendium BSLI of -0.6808 (Grade E · 112th out of 139 countries). The auxiliary time series v1.2 consists entirely of empirical measurements from 2016 to 2024: it dropped from -0.5024 in 2017 to -0.7935 in 2020 (the time-series trough), and then rose for four consecutive years to -0.6431 in 2024.

What severely drags this country down is housing stress (H_s) — Palma went from 3.43 → 4.26 → 4.16 and H_s went from 0.4456 → 0.6385 → 0.6141, which is more than four times larger than deprivation (D, 0.1361). The 2020 trough was the result of both D ($3.65-a-day poverty rate of 20.1%) and H_s expanding simultaneously.

As of Hermes collection (2026-10-14), the World Bank API national poverty line poverty rate decreased from 39.7% in 2021 to 31.8% in 2024, and the 2021 PPP $3.00-a-day poverty rate dropped from 11.2% to 8.5%. The Gini coefficient remains high at 54.4 (2024).

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Text (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentile (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight because food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — Higher weight because housing pressure follows an irreversible chain of jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D(subtraction): Deprivation index = $3.65-a-day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s(subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant and removed from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living baseline. This country's Compendium value: -0.6808 (Grade E · 112th out of 139 countries).

Colombia is a representative case where H_s (Palma^1.5) determines BSLI. Even if poverty (D) decreases, BSLI does not rise significantly if distributional disparities remain intact.
