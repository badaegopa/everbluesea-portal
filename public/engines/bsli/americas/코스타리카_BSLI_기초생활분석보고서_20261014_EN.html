Executive Summary

Costa Rica lies below the basic living line (0) with a Series BSLI of -0.2546 (Grade E · 82nd out of 139 countries). Consumer prices were 8.27% in 2022, 0.53% in 2023, −0.41% in 2024, and −0.07% in 2025, with the unemployment rate (ILO model) dropping from 11.32% to 6.84%.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Lower weight due to short-term recovery potential for food crises via reserves and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life fulfillment (median income normalized) — Higher weight because housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (subtractive): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtractive): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. Series value for this country: -0.2546 (Grade E · 82nd out of 139 countries).

In Costa Rica, H_s (0.27–0.37) exceeds the additive terms (F_ML·H approx. 0.15–0.22), placing BSLI below the basic living line. The rise from 2020 to 2024 was jointly driven by the decrease in Palma (3.05 → 2.55) and the poverty rate, along with increases in the additive terms.
