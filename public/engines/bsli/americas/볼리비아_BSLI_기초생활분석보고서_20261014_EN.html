§ 01

Executive Summary

Bolivia has a compendium BSLI of -0.1227 (Grade E · 69th out of 139 countries), remaining below the basic living line (0). The auxiliary time series v1.2 consists entirely of empirical measurements from 2016 to 2024: rising from -0.2006 in 2016 to -0.0555 in 2019, declining to -0.1152 in 2020, and standing at -0.0357 in 2021 and -0.0252 in 2024. The compendium value is close to the 2020 empirical measurement and 0.1 lower than the most recent value.

In this country, housing stress (H_s) exceeds deprivation (D): in 2024, H_s was 0.1607 (Palma 1.94), while D was 0.0374 ($3.65/day poverty rate of 3.2%). The rise from 2016 to 2019 and the drop in 2020 were driven most heavily by shifts in H_s: 0.2560 → 0.1725 → 0.2125 (Palma 2.48 → 2.01 → 2.24). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) stand at 0.1701 and 0.1747, respectively.

As of the Hermes collection (2026-10-14), according to the World Bank API, the 2021 PPP $3.00/day poverty rate was 2.1% in 2024, the $8.30 rate was 17.4%, and the Gini coefficient was 40.9. The national poverty line headcount ratio rose from 38.1% in 2024 to 44.7% in 2025. Consumer price inflation jumped from 1.75% in 2022 and 2.58% in 2023 to 5.1% in 2024 and 19.52% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Formula — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Formula)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight because room exists for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized median income) — Housing pressure carries a higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade: jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) is treated as a constant and excluded from the variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's compendium value: -0.1227 (Grade E · 69th out of 139 countries).

Bolivia's BSLI fluctuates considerably with the movements of inequality (H_s). Although it has been empirically measured every year through 2024, the 19.52% inflation rate in 2025 and the surge in the national poverty line lie outside the auxiliary time series.
