Executive Summary

Paraguay sits below the basic living line (0) with a Series BSLI of -0.1377 (Grade E · 72nd out of 139 countries). Gini was 44.2 in 2024, and consumer price inflation was 4.04% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Mean real income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — Food crises carry a lower weight because room exists for short-term recovery through reserves and aid.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic life satisfaction degree (normalized median income) — Housing pressure carries a higher weight because it represents an irreversible cascade from jeonse to monthly rent, gosiwon, and homelessness.
D (Subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (Subtraction): Housing Stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant and excluded from variables due to global oversupply. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's series value: -0.1377 (Grade E · 72nd out of 139 countries).

In Paraguay, H_s stemming from inequality (Palma 2.3 or higher) accounts for most of the subtraction term at 0.23, which is larger than the addition terms (F_ML and H around 0.15). The increase from 2016 to 2021 was driven by the Palma ratio declining from 2.84 to 2.18, while the 2022 decline corresponds to a year when both the Palma ratio and the poverty rate rebounded.
