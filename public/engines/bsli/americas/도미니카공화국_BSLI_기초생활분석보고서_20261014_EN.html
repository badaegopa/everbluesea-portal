Executive Summary

The Dominican Republic stands directly below the baseline living line (0) with a monograph BSLI of -0.0908 (Grade E · 65th among 139 countries). However, in the supplementary time series v1.2, 2016–2020 and 2022–2024 are measured values, with +0.0187 in 2022 and +0.0331 in 2024 being above the baseline living line — differing in sign from the monograph value (Canonical Verification Required C-1). The monograph value matches the 2018 measured value (-0.0913). The 2021 entry is 2022 data. The reference value is the monograph.

Most of the rise came from the decrease in housing stress (H_s): 0.2668 → 0.1385 → 0.1313 (Palma 2.54 → 1.80 → 1.75). Food (F_ML) and housing (H) rose from 0.1281 and 0.1352 to 0.1795 and 0.1822, respectively, while deprivation (D) remains low at 0.0168 ($3.65/day poverty rate of 1.5%).

According to Hermes collection (2026-10-14) from the World Bank API, the national poverty line rate declined from 27.7% in 2022 to 17.3% in 2025, and the 2021 PPP $8.30/day poverty rate fell from 21.8% (2021) to 14% (2024). Gini is between 37 and 39. Consumer inflation moved from 8.81% in 2022 to 3.87% in 2025.

§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML (Food, weight 0.40): Real average income index of the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery via stockpiles and aid, hence the lower weight.
H (Housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized to median income) — housing pressure follows an irreversible chain from jeonse to monthly rent, gosiwon, and homelessness, hence the higher weight.
D (subtraction): Deprivation Index = $3.65/day (2017 PPP) poverty rate · H_s (subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing is treated as a constant due to global oversupply and removed from variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = baseline living line. This country's monograph value: -0.0908 (Grade E · 65th among 139 countries).

The Dominican Republic is a country where H_s is substantially larger than D among subtraction terms (Palma above 1.7). As the disparity narrowed between 2016 and 2021, BSLI crossed above the baseline living line, and even though Palma rose slightly in 2023–2024, the increase in additive terms maintained it above the baseline living line.
