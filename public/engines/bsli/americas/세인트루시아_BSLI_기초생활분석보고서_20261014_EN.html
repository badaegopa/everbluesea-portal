§ 02

BSLI 4-Term Equation — What Does It Measure?

BSLI v7.0 Canonical Version (4-Term Subtraction Equation)
BSLI = 0.40·F_ML + 0.60·H − D − H_s
F_ML(food, weight 0.40): Average real income index for the 30th–50th income percentiles (ML stratum) (World Bank PIP) — food crises have room for short-term recovery through stockpiles and aid, so the weight is low.
H(housing, weight 0.60): Basic living adequacy (normalized by median income) — housing pressure is an irreversible chain from jeonse → monthly rent → gosiwon → homelessness, so the weight is high.
D(subtraction): Deprivation index = poverty rate at $3.65/day (2017 PPP) · H_s(subtraction): Housing stress = Palma ratio^1.5.
Clothing (衣) was treated as a constant due to global oversupply and excluded from the variables. Range [−2.0, +1.0], 0 = basic living line. This country's series value: -0.4011 (Grade E · 94th out of 139 countries).

That the series value is below the basic living line means the subtraction terms (D, H_s) are larger than the additive terms. In the absence of supplementary time series, the respective sizes of the four terms lie outside the scope of this report. The 2015 Gini of 43.7 is the last international figure showing that the distributional gap (on the H_s side) is substantial.
