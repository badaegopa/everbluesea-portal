§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2008
Financial Crisis — Collapse of the three major banks followed by protests and a change of government (general fact). This is a year prior to the trajectory.
2019-11
Fishrot Case — Disclosure of allegations that the fishing company Samherji paid bribes to Namibian officials in exchange for fishing quotas. The company denied wrongdoing while acknowledging the payments (Grapevine · TI).
2023
Lowest Point of Corruption Perception Index — Transparency International's corruption perception index 72 points (1998–2025 average 84.2, peak 97 points in 2005). Transparency International Iceland linked the earlier decline primarily to the failure to handle Fishrot (Trading Economics · Grapevine). In the trajectory, A2 fell in 2022 and 2023.
2023-11
Grindavík Evacuation — Evacuation of Grindavík residents due to earthquakes and volcanic activity on the Reykjanes Peninsula, followed by repeated eruptions (general fact).
2024-11-30
Snap General Election — General election following coalition collapse. The Social Democratic Alliance became the largest party with 15 of 63 seats (20.8%); the Independence Party won 14 seats; the Pirate Party and the Left-Green lost all their seats. Turnout was 80.1%. On December 21, Prime Minister Kristín Árnardóttir's three-party coalition (36 seats) took office, presenting lower inflation and interest rates and a referendum on EU accession by 2027 as its objectives (IDEA · Euronews · Wikipedia).
2025-07
Concluded Investigation — The local prosecutor's office concluded a five-year investigation into Samherji. Nine former and current employees were the subjects of the investigation; whether to file charges is undecided. Iceland rejected Namibia's request for extradition of the suspects. In Namibia, former ministers have been handed over for trial (Grapevine · SeafoodSource · TI).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

2024 Change of Government. In the early general election, the Social Democratic Alliance more than doubled its seats to become the largest party, while the Pirate Party and the Left-Green lost all their seats. Turnout was nearly identical to 2021 at 80.1% (IDEA).

Two legal proceedings against Fishrot. In Namibia, a criminal trial is underway, and in Iceland, an investigation has concluded and only prosecution remains to be decided. More than 1,000 Namibian fishery workers applied for victim status under the Icelandic procedure with TI support (TI·SeafoodSource).

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 28–29 per year in 2020–2022, 40 in 2023, and 95 in 2024. Political violence was 0–2 events per year.
