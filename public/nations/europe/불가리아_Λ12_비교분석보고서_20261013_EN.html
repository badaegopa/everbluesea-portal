§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Changes in the political landscape. After nearly five years of repeated snap general elections, a single force secured a majority in April 2026 (KAS, Wikipedia).

Euro adoption. Lev-euro exchange was free of charge at banks and post offices until June 30 (EC). Protests surrounding the draft budget immediately preceding adoption led to government resignation (Al Jazeera).

Public trends. ACLED protests were 372 cases in 2018, 894 in 2020, 606 in 2022, and 539 in 2024. Political violence ranges from 0 to 11 cases per year.
