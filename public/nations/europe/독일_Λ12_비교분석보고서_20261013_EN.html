§ 03-B

Official Figures vs. Independent Sources — Examining the Gaps

The gaps in Germany lie in the fact that participant counts for the same rally were tallied as a total by organizers but city-by-city by the police, leaving no official total; that organizers' and police figures differed widely for student protests on the same day; and that the government itself downgraded its growth forecast. Official figures are left as in the original (DECISIONS 17·19) and placed side-by-side (Compendium Briefing Numerical Discrepancies section).
