Electoral Authoritarianism
A system that holds elections but is not fair. Pre-determines results through gerrymandering, media dominance, and opposition restrictions. Defined by V-Dem.

EU Sanctions and Funding Freeze
In 2025.01, the EU froze $1 billion in funding due to violations of the rule of law. An unprecedented action in EU history. Hungary's EU subsidies account for 3–4% of its GDP.
