Historical Time Series — Events that Shaped the Structure

2012~
SNS-Led Political Situation — President Aleksandar Vučić and the Serbian Progressive Party (SNS) have led the political situation since 2012 (Series Briefing).
2024-11-01
Novi Sad Canopy Collapse — The concrete canopy of the railway station collapsed, killing 16 people (Series Briefing). On December 30, the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in Novi Sad indicted 13 people, including former Minister Goran Vesić (Euronews·N1).
2025-01-28
Prime Minister's Resignation — Prime Minister Miloš Vučević resigned following the assault on student protesters by ruling party members (Series Briefing).
2025-03-15
Large-Scale Rally in Belgrade — Ministry of Internal Affairs: 107,000; independent counting organization Archive of Public Gatherings: 275,000–325,000 (Series Briefing).
2025-04-16
Makut Cabinet — Parliament approved the cabinet of endocrinology professor Đuro Macut with 153 votes in favor (Series Briefing·RFE/RL).
2025-09 ~ 2026-07
Course of Indictment — The first-instance court dismissed charges against some individuals including Vesić, and prosecutors re-indicted the 13 individuals in September 2025 (Criminal Code Article 288, Paragraph 2). On July 13, 2026, the Novi Sad Court of Appeal overturned the lower court's decision to suspend proceedings and upheld the indictment. As of mid-July, the trial had not begun. A financial investigation by the Prosecutor's Office for Organized Crime and separate indictments (3 individuals) by the Belgrade Prosecutor's Office are also underway (N1·Vreme·Tanjug).
2026-05-24
Clashes with Police — Clashes during student-led rallies demanding early general elections; the Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights expressed concern over attacks on journalists and protesters (Series Briefing·Al Jazeera).
2026-09
Formalization of Early General Election — On September 7, Prime Minister Macut requested the dissolution of parliament; on September 14, the student movement submitted its candidate list for the general election; on September 27, President Vučić resigned to run for the prime ministership, and the following day, Parliamentary Speaker Ana Brnabić became Acting President. The general election is set for October 25, with an early presidential election by the end of 2026 (Series Briefing).
