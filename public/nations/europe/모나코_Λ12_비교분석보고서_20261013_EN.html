§ 03

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Financial monitoring. Following inclusion on the grey list in 2024, the government pledged delisting and received a 'largely addressed' assessment in June 2025. As of the reference date of this report (2026-10-13), the decision of the Paris plenary ending on October 30, 2026 has not yet been issued.

Legislation and royal assent. A case where the monarch did not give royal assent to a bill overwhelmingly passed by the elected National Council (24 seats). Under the constitution, a bill cannot become law without the monarch's royal assent. Abortion was decriminalized in 2019, but is permitted only under the three exceptions of the 2009 law (rape, risk to maternal life, severe fetal impairment) (ConstitutionNet).

Public trends. ACLED protests number 2–6 events per year from 2020 to 2024. Within the verified scope, there are no reports of large-scale collective action.
