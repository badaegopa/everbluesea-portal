§0 · Glossary
Dayton Agreement (1995)
Peace agreement ending the Bosnian War. Divided the state into two entities (Federation and Republika Srpska). Evaluated as having brought peace but created an unworkable state structure.
Republika Srpska (RS)
Serb entity within Bosnia. Approximately 34% of the population. Ruled by President Dodik for 30 years. Repeated threats of secession.
Dodik (Milorad Dodik)
President of RS. In February 2025, sentenced to one year in prison and disqualified from public office for six years for failing to comply with a court ruling. Supported by Serbia and Russia. Subject to EU and US sanctions.
Srebrenica Genocide
July 1995. Massacre of 8,000 Bosnian Muslims. Genocide rulings by ICJ and ICTY. Officially denied by RS. A key unassimilated event of TBI.
High Representative
An international position overseeing the Dayton Agreement. Holds broad legislative and executive powers. RS denies its legitimacy. De facto state of a semi-protectorate.
Population Outflow
Estimated annual outflow of 50,000 people. Large-scale exodus of youth due to delays in EU accession. Threatens the viability of the state.
Ⅰ · Observer Standpoint

The World's Most Complex Democracy — Yet Dysfunctional

Bosnia has a structure in which three ethnic groups (Bosniaks, Serbs, and Croats) jointly run the state. This structure, created by the Dayton Agreement, stopped the war but paralyzed the state. Of the 14 key priorities for EU accession, only one was achieved by 2023.

When Dodik was convicted in February 2025, RS attempted to withdraw its representatives from state institutions. EUFOR increased its troops, and the NATO Secretary-General personally visited Sarajevo. A realistic threat of state division has emerged in the heart of Europe.

Has it happened before: The Balkans were a place where the boundaries of empires overlapped: Ottoman, Habsburg, and Yugoslav.
