§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1960 · 1974
Independence and Partition — Independence in 1960; partition of the island following the 1974 coup and Turkish military intervention (general fact).
2004
EU Accession and the Annan Plan — EU accession after a unification plan referendum failed due to opposition from the Greek Cypriot majority (general fact).
2013
Financial Crisis — A bailout package including sharing losses on bank deposits (ring-fenced) (general fact).
2020-08 · 11
'Cyprus Papers' and Abolition of the Scheme — Following revelations about the investment immigration passport scheme through an Al Jazeera undercover investigation, the scheme was abolished. The government had previously strongly defended it (Al Jazeera).
2021-04
Nicolaou Committee Interim Report — 51.81% of the naturalizations under investigation were found to be 'illegal naturalizations exceeding legal boundaries'; dozens of citizenship revocations recommended; the report was referred to the Attorney General and kept confidential. The European Commission opened an infringement procedure (Al Jazeera).
2022 · 2023
Criminal Prosecutions and Confidential Documents — Four individuals, including a former president of the House of Representatives, referred for criminal prosecution (Cyprus Mail). In November 2023, ICIJ's 'Cyprus Leaks': reports that Cyprus-based financial services were used to protect Russian elite assets (ICIJ).
2026-05-24
General Election — Turnout 66.91%. DISY 27.1% (17 seats), AKEL 23.9% (15 seats), ELAM 10.9% (8 seats, up from 4 in 2021), DIKO 10% (8 seats). DISY and AKEL combined at 51.0% (68.7% in 2001) (The National Herald · Who Governs).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Political Landscape. In the 2026 general election, the combined vote share of the two traditional parties fell to 51.0%, ELAM doubled its seats, and two new parties entered parliament. One analysis assessed that the president had lost most of his original parliamentary support base (Who Governs).

After the Passport Scheme. The final report of the Nicolas Commission recommended reviewing the revocation of citizenship in 85 cases, and a criminal trial followed (International Adviser · Investment Migration Council). The '53%' figure in the final report is reported both as an 'illegal' rate and as a rate at which passports were issued to family members or executives rather than to the investors themselves, leading to divergent interpretations.

Public Trends. ACLED protests peaked at 394 events in 2022 and stood at 201 in 2024. Political violence rose from 13 events in 2020 to 42 in 2024 — the violence against migrants in August–September 2023 (general fact) is part of this trend.
