§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2015
The Divergence of GDP and National Income — The globalization activities of a small number of multinational corporations began to drive a large divergence between GDP and national income indicators. The purchasing-power-adjusted GDP per capita index jumped from 138 in 2014 to 181 in 2015 (CSO).
2023
GDP −5.5%, GNI* +5.0% — As the multinational corporation sector contracted, GDP fell from €520.9 billion to €510.0 billion, while adjusted national income (GNI*) grew by 5.0% to €290.9 billion. GNI* is 57% of GDP (CSO).
2023-11-23
Dublin Riots — Following a knife attack outside Parnell Square School, far-right activists spread anger over immigration on social media, and buses and trams burned in the city center. Sixty police officers were assaulted, and 57 people had been arrested by November 2024 (Wikipedia).
2024-11-29
General Election — After the general election, a Fianna Fáil–Fine Gael coalition government took office in January 2025 (general fact).
2025-10
Emergency Shelter Records — The Department of Housing recorded 16,766 emergency shelter placements (5,274 children). In December, there were 16,734 placements, 1,870 more than a year earlier (Department of Housing · Dublin Simon · Novas).
2025-10-24
presidential elections — Left independent Catherine Connolly was elected with approximately 63% of first-preference votes, and candidate Pádraig Ó Gadhra received approximately 29% (Al Jazeera·Euronews).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Two economic indicators. GDP is heavily affected by the transfer of multinational corporations' intellectual property and profits, whereas CSO publishes a separate figure, GNI*, that excludes these transfers. In 2023, GDP declined while GNI* increased, moving in opposite directions (CSO).

Housing. The Department of Housing's count of emergency accommodation remained at record levels throughout 2025. This figure does not include homeless people, squatters, domestic violence shelters, hidden homelessness, or asylum seekers for international protection (Novas·Dublin Simon).

Public Trends. ACLED recorded 283 protests in 2020, 357 in 2023, and 434 in 2024. Political violence ranged from 1 to 12 incidents per year, with no conflict fatalities.
