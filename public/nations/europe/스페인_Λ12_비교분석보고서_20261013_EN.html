§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Built the Structure

2011
15-M Movement — The 'Indignados' (Outraged) movement that began with the occupation of Madrid's Sol Square (general fact). The 2026 Sol Square sit-in was compared to this (Series Briefing).
2025-04-05
Housing Protests across 40 Cities — The first simultaneous protests under the slogan 'End the Housing Business' (Series Briefing · Euronews). Rents have approximately doubled on average over the past 10 years.
2025-06-12
Ruling Party Kickback Allegations — PSOE Organization Secretary Santos Cerdán resigned from his party post and parliamentary seat over allegations of illegal commissions amounting to €620,000. Prime Minister Sánchez apologized and stated there would be no general election before 2027 (Series Briefing · Catalan News).
2026-09-23~27
Eviction and Sol Square — An 87-year-old tenant who lived in Madrid's Retiro district for over 70 years was evicted after rent increased from €500 to €2,650 per month. Following an anti-eviction march on the 27th, a sit-in began at Sol Square, and protests were also held at Plaça de Catalunya in Barcelona. Protesters succeeded in postponing another eviction until October 14 (Series Briefing · NPR · Reuters).
2026-09-29
Two Housing Royal Decrees — Eviction protection for vulnerable tenants until 2030, regulation of short-term and room rentals, ban on housing acquisitions by speculative funds until 2028, and automatic lease renewal (Series Briefing · Catalan News).
2026-10-02~03
Parliamentary Rejection — Due to opposition from PP, Vox, Junts, and UPN, the housing royal decree was rejected 178 to 172, and the automatic renewal royal decree was rejected 184 to 166. Media reported the Prime Minister was considering snap elections, while the leader of Vox demanded snap elections (Series Briefing · The Olive Press).
