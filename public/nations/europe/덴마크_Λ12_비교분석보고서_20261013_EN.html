§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Political Landscape. In the 2026 early general election, the Social Democrats were reported to have garnered around 22%, their lowest vote share in 120 years (Robert Schuman Foundation), and coalition negotiations took a record-long 69 days. The Greenland issue was reported as a background factor in the election (AP).

Poverty Measurement. Denmark has no official poverty line except for a brief period between 2013 and 2015. Statistics Denmark (DST) defines relative poverty as below 50% of median income, while EU-SILC uses 60%. Under EU-SILC, relative child poverty was 10% in 2024 (EU average 19%), and working-age poverty was 12% in 2024 after peaking at 14.5% in 2018 (Scottish Government international review). The Economic Council of the Labour Movement calculated that approximately 50,000 children are in relative poverty, with increases observed in 31 municipalities (The Local).

Public Trends. ACLED recorded between 368 and 833 protests per year from 2020 to 2024, with virtually no fatalities. Within the confirmed scope, there are no reports of large-scale unrest in 2026.
