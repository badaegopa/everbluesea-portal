2025-10-03~04

Spolu took 23.4% and 52 seats; STAN, 22 seats; the Pirate Party, 18 seats; SPD, 15 seats; and Drivers, 13 seats. Turnout was 68.95% (the highest since 1998). President Pavel made the resolution of Agrofert conflicts a condition for appointing the prime minister, and Babiš announced the transfer of Agrofert to an independent trust (Wikipedia·IDEA·eurotopics).
