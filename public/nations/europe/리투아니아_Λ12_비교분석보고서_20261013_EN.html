§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Migration and Population. According to Statistics Lithuania, emigrants in 2024 numbered 28,700, an increase of 32.4% from the previous year, and 70.1% of emigrants were aged 15–44 (this age group accounts for 36.6% of the total population). Net international migration peaked at 72,000 in 2022 (including Ukrainian refugees) before declining to 23,000 in 2024 (EMN·OSW). The population stands at approximately 2.9 million as of 2025.

Research and Development. Total R&D expenditure was approximately 1.04% of GDP (provisional) in 2024, less than half of the EU average of 2.27% (2023), and the government R&D budget stood at 0.39% of GDP in 2023 (republished by Eurostat · ERA Country Report). This directly reflects the actual measurement of A5, the rank 1 pressure.

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 59–151 per year between 2020 and 2024, with almost no political violence. Within the verified scope, there are no reports of large-scale unrest.
