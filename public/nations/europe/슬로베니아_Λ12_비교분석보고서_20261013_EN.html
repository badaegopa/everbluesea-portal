Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1991 · 2004
Independence and EU Accession — Independence in 1991, accession to the EU and NATO in 2004, adoption of the euro in 2007 (general facts).
2020 · 2021
Suspension of STA Funding and Protests — The Janša government suspended funding for the state news agency STA for most of 2021 and resumed it in November under a new contract (MFRR). Anti-government protests, including Friday bicycle protests, occurred at the same time (general facts). ACLED recorded 141 protests in 2021.
2022-04
Regime Change — The Freedom Movement (GS) won the general election under Robert Golob, and the Golob government took office (general facts).
2023-11~2025
Prime Minister's Investigation — The Anti-Corruption Commission (KPK) launched and resumed an investigation into the prime minister over allegations of pressure on police appointments; the prime minister denied the allegations in testimony in December 2024. In May 2025, a separate investigation was opened into the 'Karigador' allegation. The Court of Audit found that the purchase of the Ministry of Justice building did not conform to principles of efficiency and economy (Jurist · 24ur · SGI).
2026-03-22
General Election — Electoral Commission: GS approximately 28.6% (29 seats), Slovenian Democratic Party (SDS) approximately 28% (28 seats), turnout approximately 68%. The opposition challenged the validity of the election procedures twice before the Constitutional Court (InterCapital · OSW).
2026-04 · 05
Minority Government — Golob's coalition formation failed; the president did not nominate a candidate for prime minister. 43 lawmakers directly proposed Janša under constitutional procedure, and parliament elected him as the fourth prime minister on May 22 — a minority government of 43 out of 90 seats (OSW · Euronews).
