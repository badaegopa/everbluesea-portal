§0 Overview
§0 · Overview
Why View the Three Balkan Countries in the Same Table

The dissolution of Yugoslavia, which began with the declarations of independence by Slovenia and Croatia on June 25, 1991, was sealed by the Dayton Peace Agreement on December 14, 1995. However, sealing was not resolving. The same war left different types of scars on the three societies,, and 30 years later, in 2026, the distribution of those scars determines the Λ¹² coordinates of the three countries.

Croatia joined the EU, NATO, and the Eurozone (EU 2013, Euro 2023, Schengen 2023), while Bosnia has been trapped for 30 years in the three-ethnic veto structure created by Dayton,, and Serbia maintains its EU candidate status while wavering among Kosovo, Russia, and China. This report reads these three paths simultaneously through 12 variables.

§8 Quote
§8 · Observer Notes
What Were the Determining Variables of the Divergence?
"Dayton succeeded in stopping the war, but failed to build peace. Bosnia has lived in a frozen conflict for 30 years."
— International Crisis Group, "Bosnia at 30" estimated quote frame (2025)

Although the three countries started from the same point, the progress speed of the EU integration path determined the divergence. Croatia pursued a consistent EU/NATO entry strategy after 1995, passing through NATO in 2009, EU in 2013, and both the Eurozone and Schengen in 2023. Serbia's EU entry stalled due to the Kosovo issue, and the vacuum was filled by Russian and Chinese influence. Bosnia is trapped in a structure where the Dayton Constitution itself conflicts with EU standards, making progress impossible without constitutional revision.

That is, the external variable that determined the Λ¹² divergence is the stage of EU integration, and the internal variable is Σ (leader-people disconnect). When both variables pointed in the same direction, a stable attractor was formed (Croatia); when the two variables clashed, oscillation occurred (Serbia); and when both variables were blocked, it entered the chaotic region (Bosnia).

§9 Open Questions
§9 · Open Questions
Post-2026 — Observation Points
▌ 5 Observation Variables
1. RS Separation Attempts When Republika Srpska's attempts to establish its own military, tax authority, and constitutional court ignore the decisions of the Constitutional Court, BIH is practically divided. Λ_BIH rises above 0.65.
2. Kosovo-Serbia Negotiations If the EU-mediated negotiations are settled, SRB's Σ could improve and CFD could be realigned. If they fail, Λ_SRB is at risk of breaking through 0.60.
3. Croatia's Population Decline Accelerated youth outflow after joining the EU. HDI of 0.858 is maintained, but LPI_net may worsen. A signal of cracks in the stable attractor.
4. 30th Anniversary of Dayton (Dec 2025) The 30th anniversary already passed on December 14, 2025. If constitutional revision attempts are frustrated, the structural freezing of BIH is officially finalized.
5. External Variables in the Balkan Region The manner in which the Russia–Ukraine war ends will directly impact Serbia's Σ. Changes in the EU expansion policy will affect all three countries.

Answering these five questions is outside the scope of this comparison edition. the fact that the same war created three different coordinates 30 years later is the core thesis of the Λ¹² framework — shocks are universal, but divergence is determined by local variables.
