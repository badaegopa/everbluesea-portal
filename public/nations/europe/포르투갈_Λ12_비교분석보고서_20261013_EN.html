§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Party Landscape. After two snap general elections within two years, Chega became the main opposition party ahead of the Social Democratic Party, and in the 2026 presidential runoff, its leader, Rui Rio, ran (CNN · Essential Business).

Poverty and Housing. The National Institute of Statistics (INE) at-risk-of-poverty rate was 15.4% in 2024, 1.2 percentage points lower than the previous year. According to the same INE data, new rents rose by 49% between 2017 and 2022, and in October 2025, residential property valuations exceeded €2,000 per square meter for the first time (INE · Euronews).

Public Trends. ACLED recorded 364 protests in 2020, 618 in 2021, 333 in 2022, 581 in 2023, and 536 in 2024. Conflict fatalities numbered 0–2 per year.
