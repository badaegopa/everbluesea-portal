Recent Situation (2025–2026)

War-time Governance. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the Koroletsky cabinet, which took office in July 2026, identified the development of the defense industry, preparations for the heating season, support for frontline areas, and continued European integration as its top priorities, and reorganized foreign policy around sector-specific point persons. In the first half of 2026, there were 1,396 fatalities and 7,978 injuries, a 37% increase over the first half of 2025. Anti-corruption and Civil Society. The protests of July 2025, the restoration of the law, and the resignation of the presidential chief of staff in November 2025 record that channels through which civil society and independent anti-corruption bodies can check the government operated even under martial law. No nationwide anti-government protests have been reported within the scope confirmed in 2026 (Series briefing).

Economy.The IMF lowered its 2026 growth forecast to 2% (4.5% in the October 2025 forecast). The state of emergency has postponed the presidential and parliamentary elections (Series briefing).
