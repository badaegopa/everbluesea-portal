§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Direction of Institutional Reform. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), after the rejection of the judicial constitutional reform, the government shifted its focus to electoral system reform; reports in April 2027 considered an early general election (Bloomberg), but no official decision has been made. The new far-right party 'Future Italians' was reported at 6–7% in opinion polls.

Grassroots Base. According to Istat, absolute poverty in 2024 stood at 2.2 million households (8.4%) and 5.7 million people (9.8%), similar to the previous year; however, absolute poverty among minors reached 13.8% (approximately 1.3 million), the highest since 2014. Households with foreigners accounted for 30.4%, and southern households 10.5%. The at-risk-of-poverty or social exclusion rate in 2025 fell to 22.6% (ANSA · Il Sole 24 Ore · MF).

Fiscal. Italy's growth rate for 2025 was revised from 0.5% provisional in March to 0.6% confirmed in September, the debt ratio from 137.1% to 136.7%, and the deficit remained at 3.1% (Series briefing).
