Historical Time Series — Events That Built the Structure

2019
Country Name Change — Country name changed to 'North Macedonia' pursuant to the Prespa Agreement with Greece (general fact).
2022
EU Negotiation Framework — A constitutional revision explicitly including Bulgarians was incorporated as a condition for opening negotiations (BTA · Eunews).
2024-06
Government Transition — Parliamentary approval of VMRO-DPMNE leader Hristijan Mickoski's cabinet following the May elections (AP · Eunews).
2025-03-16
Kočani Nightclub Fire — Stage pyrotechnics spread to a combustible ceiling. 59 died at the time, later reaching 63 due to injuries (62 or 64 depending on reports), over 200 injured. Nearly double the venue capacity was admitted; unlawfully issued permits and omitted inspections were subject to investigation (Balcanica Caucaso · European Western Balkans).
2025-11-15
Skopje Marches — The bereaved families' 'March of Angels', which continued every Saturday in Kočani, spread to Skopje with thousands participating. The opposition pushed for the formation of a parliamentary inquiry committee (European Western Balkans).
2025-11-19
Trial Commencement — 35 individuals, including former and incumbent ministers, former Kočani mayor, and inspectors, along with 3 corporations indicted; all pleaded not guilty. 26 police officers under separate investigation (Balcanica Caucaso · DJ Mag).
2026-01
Conditions for Constitutional Revision — Prime Minister Mickoski presented two conditions in parliament: guaranteeing the rights of ethnic Macedonians in Bulgaria, protecting identity and language, and ending bilateral vetoes (BTA).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

EU negotiations. The European Commission President stated that an 'agreed constitutional revision' prior to opening negotiations is the sole next step, while the Prime Minister responded that there would be no revision without conditions (BTA). This report documents both positions side by side.

Post-fire. The bereaved families expressed concern that the trial would drag on and full accountability would not be established; a memorial was held on the first anniversary, March 16, 2026, at 2:12 AM (European Western Balkans).

Public trends. ACLED recorded 84 protests in 2018, 225 in 2020, 187 in 2021, 163 in 2022, 208 in 2023, and 141 in 2024. Political violence stands at 1 to 7 incidents per year.
