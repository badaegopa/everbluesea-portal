Prologue — What Country Analysis Does

1. What Is Measured and What Is Not

Λ¹² was designed based on the minimum viable operational scale of a state. There are two reasons it was not calibrated against modern advanced civilization. First, it allows advanced, middle-income, and underdeveloped nations to be accommodated within a single coordinate system; second, even underdeveloped nations possess areas that are distinctly superior to those of advanced nations.

Accordingly, what this series quantifies is a country's foundational capacity. It is not an assessment classifying good countries and bad countries. While the diagram of the same grammar is applied to all countries, the topography rendered by that grammar differs for each nation.

Conversely, there are things this series does not measure numerically: the uprising itself—namely, people's uprising consciousness and groupthink. Uprisings are nonlinear; they may withstand massive external pressures, yet a single small incident can serve as a fuse. Therefore, the amplification and attenuation gates and the consciousness layer of the uprising pipeline are not included in formulas or threshold calculations, but are described only at the scale of perturbing around the coordinate system.

2. The Observer's Position

Predictions are drawn from historical patterns. It is not a matter of guessing events like lottery numbers. Each country report identifies patterns of similar events from the time history began being recorded, draws parallels with other countries from conflict data, and, after describing them, presents only up to the direction currently unfolding and halts. Subsequent judgment is the responsibility of that country's leaders and citizens.

If a prediction misses the mark and that society finds tranquility, that outcome is preferable. If leaders take heed upon seeing a diagnosis of an impending uprising and reverse course, the prediction becomes false. It is a prediction that becomes right only by being proven wrong.

3. Data Is Raw Material

Data is like building materials. A data-rich country yields precise analysis, while a data-poor country yields thin analysis; that is an honest result. Filling in data-deficient countries with estimates is meaningless. The final measured year of that country is explicitly stated and used as the baseline year.

If a specific indicator is missing only for one country, that blank space itself is information regarding that country's institutions and national infrastructure. For items where figures differ significantly across sources, such as death tolls, that discrepancy itself is a signal. This series uses figures from official government channels as the baseline, noting alongside them figures from other sources and the possibility of bias. Whether submitted data is accurate is the responsibility of that state. We merely attach caution markers to anomalous values relative to comparison countries.

4. Policy Is Described by Trajectory

Reading indicators solely by standard values leads to misinterpretation. Even with the same score, placing it alongside the country's actual policy trajectory reveals whether it is a rational choice or an act of severing future foundations. Thus, each country report contrasts the trajectory of the previous government with the policies of the current administration without arbitrating outcomes, merely stating "shifting direction toward ○○." Items reduced due to fiscal constraints are denoted not as 'abolished,' but as 'temporarily suspended for fiscal reasons.'

5. Where This Research Began

The conceptual starting point of this research was the youth uprising in Nepal in September 2025. An uprising by the youth generation against social media bans, privileges of the ruling elite's children, and corruption brought down the government. In countries that have recently risen up, the structure where the youth form the backbone and the movement spreads to the grassroots stratum repeats itself. The Λ¹² country analysis was created to examine the structure of "why the youth rise up in society." The measurement objective is not the deprivation of the youth, but the social structure in which any young person can actively engage—that is, active possibility.

1.1 Physical Metaphors as Structural Isomorphisms

Λ¹² does not equate society with physical systems. Borrowing physical concepts such as impedance, elasticity, and resonance is grounded in the determination that the structures of pressure, resistance, and propagation are computationally isomorphic across physical and social systems. This differs from the reductionism of "society is a machine." It is a computational metaphor applying the mathematical structures of mechanical vibration models to social phenomena, aiming to quantify previously unmeasurable sociodynamics. Because human society cannot be explained by a single law, physical laws, chaos and fractals, abstract concepts, and philosophical reasoning are combined depending on the subject.

Part 2 BSLI — Measuring Economic Living Stability of the ML Layer

Nomenclature: In the academic manuscript, to avoid misunderstandings arising from the name 'OO Index', it is referred to as 'Measuring Economic Living Stability of the ML Layer' (Decided on 2026-10-02). The abbreviation BSLI and the calculation formula remain unchanged.

2.1 Why a Separate Channel?

Λ¹² measures macro-structures at the national level. However, national-level aggregation absorbs the real-life pressures on the lower end of the distribution—namely, the grassroots stratum—into the average. Even if the growth rate is high, if that growth is concentrated in the upper stratum, the grassroots dining table may shrink. Between 2019 and 2021, Bangladesh's GDP growth rate was over 6%, yet the 2024 Monsoon Revolution erupted (Combined Paper §4.1).

Historically, it is the grassroots stratum that leads nationwide protests and uprisings. Only by measuring the pressure on the stratum that instigates uprisings can the structure of the uprising be seen. BSLI measures that stratum separately in its own language. Axiom A0 (Independent Existence) prescribes the separation of the two channels.

The ML layer resides within national institutions but bears only obligations. While it is the weakest link, once a trigger is activated, it becomes the strongest force. This layer is not substituted by national indicators and often moves in the opposite direction from national indicators. An uprising is a phenomenon in which an incident touching human altruism, common sense, and emotion acts as a fuse, unleashing what has been suppressed by emotionless institutions and capital. Therefore, quantities such as collapse acceleration or m = A2·A8 must not be read merely as objects.

2.2 Clothing, Food, and Housing — The Philosophy of Classification

The classification of BSLI is based on clothing, food, and housing (衣食住), the universal survival foundations of humanity. Clothing is treated as a constant and excluded from variables because cross-national variance has disappeared due to global manufacturing oversupply. The weights of food and housing stem from the difference in recoverability. A food crisis can be recovered in the short term through the release of government stockpiles or international aid. Housing pressure, however, is an irreversible chain leading from jeonse to monthly rent, to gosiwon, and to homelessness.

2.4 Negative Values Are Normal

In BSLI, 0 is the basic living line, and negative values represent the depth sunken below that line. Out of 139 countries, 83 are negative, and the median value is -0.1301. Grade boundaries are fixed values established at design time and were not retrofitted to empirical distributions. The fact that Grade E constitutes the largest share with 94 countries (67.6%) is an empirical finding showing that the grassroots base in the majority of global nations lies below the basic living line.

2.5 Independence of the Two Channels

The Pearson correlation between η and BSLI across 138 shared countries is r = +0.7551. It does not meet the falsification conditions of Axiom A0 (r > 0.95). The combined paper reports that this aggregate correlation conceals interval-specific divergence (upper tier r = 0.6463, middle tier r = −0.0638, lower tier r = +0.1097), and that the more crisis-ridden a country is, the more macro indicators decouple from the grassroots base (Combined Paper §4.6). 3.3 Feedback Structure

  • B1 Absorption-Defense Balancing Loop. Absorbers (A2·A8) defend the grassroots base (+), and when the grassroots base deteriorates, defense rallies (−, Axiom A8). It is a self-regulating structure with a negative polarity product.
  • R1 Binary Reinforcing Loop. A2 and A8 move virtually together like a binary star with an empirical coupling of +0.955. When healthy, they constitute the strongest defense; when eroded, they suffer mutual collapse.
  • B2 Pressure-Erosion Path. Pressure sources (A7·A9·A12) erode the absorbers (−, Axiom A1). It is a pathway reducing the capacity of B1 from the outside. Once B2 sufficiently erodes the absorbers (E_A2 < 0.2, Axiom A9), R1 flips from a defense amplifier to a collapse amplifier.

Part 4 How to Read Time Series

4.1 Measuring Only Year-to-Year Movements

All indicators are tracked over time series. Not only position (P) but also direction (sign of ΔP) and year-to-year changes are recorded together. Derived composite metrics that collapse the entire period into one (such as net displacement rate or straightness) are not created. Direction and annual velocity changes are sufficient.

4.4 Common Patterns in Uprising-Observed Countries (Reference)

According to KSDS v10.0 Ⅴ.4, all four uprising-observed countries (Bolivia 2019.11, Sri Lanka 2022.07, Bangladesh 2024.08, Nepal 2025.09 — Nepal being T−1 given data scope) had 3 to 4 dimensions within the critical band at the time of the uprising, and in all four countries, this composition included A7 and A8. In contrast, the control case of the Republic of Korea had 0 dimensions within the band across the entire period. If the number of dimensions within the band decreases after the uprising and the trajectory moves toward escape, it is an emissive type (Supernova class); if the number of dimensions is maintained and descent continues, it is a non-emissive type (White Dwarf class).

Regional reports do not use this pattern for assessment. They merely record, as measured values, each country's 2024 dimensions within the band and whether A7 and A8 are included.

5.3 Limitations

  • Coupling coefficient r was calculated across the 2023–2025 cross-section of 138 countries, whereas dimension time series were derived from separately collected 2016–2024 data, resulting in mixed data vintages. Consequently, P in the cross-sectional diagram and 2024 P in the trajectory may differ.
  • Until the label–measurement gap (Section 1.4) is resolved, reading contents solely based on axis names risks misinterpretation. Read the node's "Empirical:" text first.
  • A2, A8, and A9 in the trajectory file all originate from a single source, the WGI. Strong co-movement among these three axes may reflect an intra-source correlation rather than social coupling.
  • The critical band is an exploratory candidate derived from n=4.
  • Narrative briefings were collected from public sources as of 2026-10-07. Although bias indications are attached to each source, each entry requires verification against the original text.
