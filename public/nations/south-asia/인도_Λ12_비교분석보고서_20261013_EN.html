Historical Time Series — Events that Shaped the Structure

1991
Economic Liberalization — Market opening began after the foreign exchange crisis (general fact).
2019 · 2024
Modi 2nd and 3rd Term — Re-elected in 2019 and launched the 3rd term in June 2024 (Series Briefing). In 2019, former Chief Economic Advisor Arvind Subramanian published a paper arguing that growth rates from 2011/12 to 2016/17 were overestimated by 2.5 percentage points annually, and the statistics department rebutted by claiming the methodology was accepted (The Week·The Wire).
2025-04 · 05
Operation Pahlad and Sundar — On April 22, 26 militants were killed in Kashmir's Pahalgam attack, and on May 7, the Indian army struck nine locations in Pakistan and Pakistan-administered Kashmir ('Operation Sundar'), followed by a ceasefire on May 10 (Series Briefing).
2025-11
IMF 'C' Rating — The IMF gave India's national accounts a 'C' rating for two consecutive years in the 2025 Article IV consultation. Reasons: inconsistency in base year 2011/12, wholesale price index deflator, and GDP discrepancy between production and expenditure methods. The IMF criticized the methodology rather than the authenticity of the data, and promised a re-evaluation after the release of the new national accounts series in February 2026 (Business Standard·VisionIAS).
2026-02 · 04
US Trade and Mid-Term Elections — May 7, US and provisional trade agreement framework (personal import tariffs 25%→18%, additional 25% withdrawn). April state elections: BJP wins in West Bengal (294 seats, 207) and Assam (126 seats, 92), Kerala UDF, Tamil Nadu sees new TVK as the first party (Country Analysis Series briefing).
2026-05 ~ 07
'Cockroach' youth movement — In May, NEET-UG 2026 test papers were leaked, leading to the cancellation and re-conduct of the exam, and the online youth movement CJP emerged. On July 20, a march to the New Delhi parliament was stopped by police tear gas, resulting in at least 178 injuries, including 118 police officers — AFP reported it as 'one of the largest street gatherings in Delhi in about five years.' On July 25, the Minister of Education resigned (Country Analysis Series briefing).
