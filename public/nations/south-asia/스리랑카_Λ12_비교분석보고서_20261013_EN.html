§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Government Direction. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government maintains IMF program requirements while pursuing welfare programs, and the tax-to-GDP ratio rose to approx. 15.4%. On anti-corruption, it has taken a hard line with high-profile investigations and arrests of former presidents, politicians, bureaucrats, and drug syndicates. The promise of local council elections and constitutional reform is assessed as unfulfilled.

Citizen Response. In the 2026 phase of energy price increases, citizen responses were divided between disappointment and patience, and human rights lawyers assessed that protests on the scale of 2022 have not materialized. Reports of nationwide large-scale protests and general strikes in 2025–2026 fall outside the scope of verification covered by the Series.

Economy. The 2025 growth rate is 5% according to the IMF (cited quarterly at 4.9–5.4%) (Series briefing).
