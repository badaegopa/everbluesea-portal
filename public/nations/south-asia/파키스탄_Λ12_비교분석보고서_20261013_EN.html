Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

Partition and Independence — Partitioned from India to gain independence (general fact).
1958–2008
Recurring Military Rule — The military directly ruled during three coups in 1958, 1977, and 1999, accounting for nearly half of the country's post-independence history (general fact). The 2025 constitutional amendment codified the military's status as a constitutional revision rather than a coup.
2025-05
Conflict with India — Military clashes following India's 'Operation Meghdoot' strikes, power cut on May 10 (compendium briefing).
2025-11-13
27th Constitutional Amendment — Passed by the National Assembly (234 in favor, 4 against) and Senate, signed by the President. Army Chief Asim Munir became 'Chief of the Armed Forces' under the constitution, and a Federal Constitutional Court dedicated to constitutional cases was established. The government cited 'reducing judicial burden,' while opposition PTI questioned the process (Arab News · The Tribune).
2026-01 · 02
Deterioration in Relations with the Taliban — International Crisis Group: Pakistan-Taliban relations deteriorated to 'unprecedented levels,' with over 600 security personnel killed in armed attacks in 2025. On February 6, an Islamabad mosque bombing (reported as IS-K), and on February 21, airstrikes hit seven locations in Afghanistan's Nangarhar, Paktika, and Khost (compendium briefing).
2026-03-16
Kabul Rehabilitation Facility Airstrike — A drug rehabilitation facility near Kabul (Omid Camp) was struck by airstrikes, resulting in multiple deaths. Casualties were reported as 'over 400' by the Taliban and 'at least 143' by the UN (compendium briefing).
2026-08-21
Domestic Crackdown — The military announced that it had killed 49 militants in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (12) and Balochistan (37) (AP).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Institutional Reorganization. According to the Country Analysis Compendium Briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government is restructuring institutions by codifying military command structures in the constitution and transferring constitutional review authority from the Supreme Court to a newly established court. Since February 2026, it has pursued an aggressive counterterrorism line including cross-border airstrikes against TTP and IS-K bases in Afghanistan under Operation Gajab Ril-Haq.

Borders. Border disputes with Afghanistan saw reports of border closures and school suspensions on both sides (EUAA, Compendium Briefing). Figures announced by belligerents do not verify each other.

Public Trends. Between November 2025 and September 2026, large-scale public protests were not reported in the media coverage (Compendium Briefing). According to ACLED tallies, protests numbered between 4,000 and 8,000 per year, while political violence increased to 2,574 cases in 2024.
