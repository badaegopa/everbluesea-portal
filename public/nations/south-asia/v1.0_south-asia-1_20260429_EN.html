══ Sri Lanka Special ══
⚡  Sri Lanka Special — Living Proof of the SR Reverse-Causality Hypothesis
2022~2025: From Bankruptcy to Recovery — Tracking the Λ¹² Trajectory
Sri Lanka in 2022 is the clearest empirical demonstration of the Λ¹² §3 SR Reverse-Causality Hypothesis.

Rather than a structure where SR was lowered due to suppression like the Hasina regime — the shock was greater because SR was low. Superficial stability was maintained for a long time, and citizens had no channel to express their grievances. Inflation 69.8%, foreign exchange reserves 0.3 months' worth, and shortages of fuel and medicine exploded simultaneously — SR crossed the threshold in an instant.

Recovery Path (2023~2025):
IMF EFF $2.9 billion initiated → inflation 4% stabilized → foreign exchange reserves $6.1 billion recovered → GDP growth +5.0%(2024) → debt restructuring 98% completed (2024).

Λ¹² Reading:
Current Λ-Score 0.341 Watch zone. Although recovering, debt GDP 104.6% remains a structural vulnerability. Estimating the Λ-Score before 2022 puts it above 0.50 Danger — in that state, SR surged and transitioned into a systemic shock. Post-recovery poverty rate is 24.5% — twice that of 2019. The speed at which economic recovery reaches the masses (PWT-r) is the key variable.
══ Bhutan GNH Special ══
🏔  Bhutan Special — Collision of GNH vs Universal Data
GNH 0.781 vs Λ-Score 0.198 — Where Two Indices Meet
Bhutan is a country that achieved GNH (Gross National Happiness) of 0.781. However, looking at the universal standards of Λ¹², it is in the Stable zone at 0.198. Do these two values conflict? No — rather, they complement each other.

While GNH measures the "level of happiness achievement," Λ¹² measures "structural instability." CPI 72 points (1st in South Asia)·GPI 17th·Press Freedom 33rd — Bhutan performs well even in universal indicators. However, two fractures are visible.
Fissure ① — Youth Emigration and Population Crisis
More than 10% of the population emigrated to Australia and Canada between 2022 and 2024. Not 'Brain drain', but 'Population drain'. The gap between happiness measured by GNH and the economic foundation of actual life. F_reach S (self-sufficiency) is favorable, but population decline threatens the long-term food production base.
Fissure ② — Dependence on India and Rapprochement with China
90% of electricity, food, and consumer goods depend on India. Vulnerable F_reach I (import stability). 2023 border negotiations with China — India reacted sensitively. Controversy over whether the 'sustainability' domain of Bhutan's GNH sufficiently reflects geopolitical vulnerabilities. Goodhart's Law: If the GNH index becomes a policy target, there is a risk of overestimating actual happiness.

Korean Peninsula Perspective
Sri Lanka 2022 is a type of shock that Korea has never experienced. A situation where fuel and medicine are cut off simultaneously due to a foreign debt default. Yet, Korea also experienced the 1997 IMF crisis — the difference being that Korea already possessed a manufacturing base. The shadow of Korea 1997–2001 is visible in Sri Lanka's recovery path.

Bhutan is the country most different from Korea. The very idea of adopting happiness as a policy target conflicts with Korea's GDP growth paradigm. However, does Korea, with one of the world's highest youth suicide rates, have the right to mock Bhutan's GNH? — Let us leave this question as a detonator on the 3rd layer (history) of the Six-Layer Discourse.

Bangladesh revolution structurally resembles Korea's 1987 June Democratic Struggle. The youth generation brought down a dictatorship. However, Korea institutionalized democracy after 1987, while Bangladesh is still on that path. What that difference is — is pointed to by PWT-r.

Supply Chain: Bangladesh is a core production hub for Korean apparel brands. If political instability persists, it directly links to the logistics risks of Korean companies.
