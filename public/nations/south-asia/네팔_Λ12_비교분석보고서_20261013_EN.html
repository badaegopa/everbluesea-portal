Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2008
Federal Republic — Abolition of the monarchy and declaration of a federal democratic republic (general fact). The year preceding the trajectory.
2025-09-08~09
Youth protests and prime ministerial resignation — Triggered by social media blockades and corruption issues, 9-day K.P. Sharma Oli resigned as prime minister amid youth-led protests. Deaths: initial reports at least 72, later 74, Wikipedia at least 76 (over 2,000 injured), Anadolu 77, ICJ·HRW·Amnesty International at least 76 (mostly illegal killings). Official government final tally unconfirmed (Compendium Briefing·HRW·Himal Press).
2025-09-12
Dissolution of Parliament and Caretaker Government — President Poudel dissolved parliament and appointed former Chief Justice Sushila Karki as Nepal's first female head of government as acting prime minister. The caretaker government formed an investigation committee (chairman Gauri Bahadur Karki) (Compendium Briefing·Deccan Herald).
2026-03-05~12
General Election — Turnout approximately 59%. RSP secured 122 out of 165 directly elected seats and 182 out of 275 total seats confirmed — first single-party majority since 1999. Balendra Shah defeated former Prime Minister Oli by a wide margin in the Jhapa constituency (Compendium Briefing).
2026-03
Investigation Report — A 1,000-page report investigating approximately 200 people was submitted to Acting Prime Minister Karki and leaked prior to release. The leaked version listed former Prime Minister Oli and former Home Minister Lekhak as targets for prosecution (Kathmandu Post·AIR).
2026-03-27
Inauguration of Prime Minister Balendra Shah — Formed cabinet with RSP and 17 independents. Cabinet reshuffling followed, including the April dismissal of the Labor Minister, the resignation and June return of the Home Minister (Compendium Briefing).
2026-05-27
National Human Rights Commission Recommendations — Designated former Prime Minister Oli, former Interior Minister Lekhak, and former ICT Minister Gurung as human rights violators, and demanded an investigation into the September 9 SNS post of the committee chair (ISAS).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Leadership (as of 2026-10-07, Compendium briefing). Head of State President Ram Chandra Paudel, Head of Government Prime Minister Bhim Bahadur Rawal (RSP, 2026-03~). The political system is transitioning from a coalition politics centered on the three major parties to a single-party government led by a new party.

Clarification of Responsibility. HRW, Amnesty International, and ICJ urged the release of the investigation report in February 2026, and it was decided to be released after leaking in March. Separate recommendations from the National Human Rights Commission came out in May (HRW, Kathmandu Post, ISAS).

Public Trends. ACLED protests were 1,541 in 2018, 2,445 in 2019, 3,074 in 2021, 2,514 in 2022, 2,277 in 2023, and 1,391 in 2024 (no tally for 2020). There are no reports of large-scale collective action after the general election within the scope of the Compendium briefing.
