Country Overview

Kiribati is a nation located in the central Pacific Ocean, consisting of 33 atolls. With three island groups (Gilbert Islands, Phoenix Islands, and Line Islands) scattered across the vast Pacific, its total marine area is larger than that of the British Isles. The land area is 811 ㎢, and the population is approximately 120,000.

Kiribati is the first in the world to be a climate refugee nation that purchased land in another country. In 2014, it purchased 2,000 acres of agricultural land in Fiji. This is a real-world precedent for the CSZ (Climate Sovereign Zone) concept proposed by the Director.

§2 History & Culture
§02

History and Culture — Masters of the Vast Ocean

The Kiribati people (I-Kiribati) are Micronesian people who have inhabited Pacific atolls for over 3,000 years. Their most important heritage is Open Ocean Navigation — a knowledge system that enables thousands of kilometers of navigation by reading constellations, wave patterns, and bird migrations.

Maneaba — Kiribati's communal meeting house. The roof is woven from pandanus leaves, and the entire village gathers to sit on the ground and make decisions. This single building is the heart of the community.

The Maneaba can be rebuilt in a new settlement. However, the memories of the coral floor beneath it cannot be relocated.

Fiji Land Purchase — A Real-World Precedent of CSZ

🌏 The World's First Overseas Land Purchase by a Climate Refugee Nation

In 2014, the Kiribati government purchased 2,000 acres (approx. 8 ㎢) of agricultural land in Fiji. The rationale was food security and migration preparation. This is proof that the CSZ (Climate Sovereign Zone) concept proposed by the Director has already begun in reality.
