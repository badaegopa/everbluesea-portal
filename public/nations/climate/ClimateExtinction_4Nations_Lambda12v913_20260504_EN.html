GEI captures situations that shatter the assumptions of the Λ¹² basic engine (§1 to §26). The basic engine was designed assuming the physical existence of territory as a premise. GEI is a separate diagnostic system for countries where that very premise is disappearing.

GEI = (SLR_rate × EAI × Pop_density) / (Support_intensity × CSZ_probability)
SLR: Sea Level Rise Rate | EAI: Elevation Inverse Index | Pop: Population Density

Both situations represent cases where the assumptions of the existing Lambda normalization scale break down.

Connection with §33 BBD (Bubble Boundary Dynamics): For GEI target countries, no matter how high the FTI (Film Thickness Index) is, it is meaningless. This is because the plate on which the bubble sits is itself disappearing. This is the most brutal case in BBD.

Falepili Union — Hope or Neo-colonialism?

Signed in November 2023 by Australia and Tuvalu, the Falepili Union is the world's first bilateral agreement on climate mobility. It officially took effect in August 2024. 'Falepili' in Tuvaluan culture means the act of caring for neighbors without expecting anything in return.

Aspects of Hope
Permanent residency for 280 people annually — offering options to 2.5% of the population
AUD 38M Coastal Adaptation Project (expanding habitable land)
AUD 50M Undersea Fiber-optic Cable — digital connectivity
Legally binding recognition of the permanence of Tuvalu's sovereignty (world's first)
Free movement — maintaining mobility between Tuvalu and Australia
Guaranteed access to social protection, healthcare, and education
Aspects of Concern
Security Veto: Australia's prior consent required for security agreements with third countries
Opposition party: "We auctioned off our sovereignty in exchange for migration rights."
No public hearings for Tuvaluan residents during the negotiation process
Agreement published only in English — absence of local language translation
Criticism as a tool for Australia to expand its influence in the Pacific
Absolutely no obligation for actual CO₂ reductions
Λ¹² Analysis: The Falepili Union is a real-world case of a structure where a small bubble is absorbed by a large bubble described in §33 BBD. Australia secured a strategic asset, namely Tuvalu's maritime sovereignty (900,000 km² — the size of Nigeria), while Tuvalu gained a survival option. However, this structure is the exact opposite of the original meaning of Falepili — caring without expecting anything in return.

Marshall Islands — The Brutality of Double Damage

"for the good of mankind" — These were the words spoken by a US Navy Admiral to 167 residents of Bikini Atoll in 1946.
They did not return for 20 years. And now, that land is disappearing again due to climate change.
This was not a mistake. It was intentional and planned.

The Marshall Islands is the only country in the world to suffer from both nuclear damage and climate damage simultaneously. Between 1946 and 1958, the United States conducted 67 nuclear tests on these atolls. The equivalent of 20 years' worth of Hiroshima bomb yields exploded over these small islands.
