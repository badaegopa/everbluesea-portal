§ 03-B

Published Figures vs. Independent Sources — Examining the Gap

Haiti's gap lies in the fact that government administrative capacity is limited to parts of the capital metropolitan area, leaving virtually no official mortality statistics, with all figures being reports based on UN tallies; that tallies from the same agency are cited differently across reporting periods; and that the gang control ratio of the capital differs by about 20%p depending on reports. Figures are kept as originally written (DECISIONS 17·19), placing the numerical discrepancies of the Series Briefing side by side.
