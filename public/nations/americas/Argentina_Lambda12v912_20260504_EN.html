§03 Historical Event Context

§03 · Historical Trajectory — Repeatability of Structural Crisis (TBI Analysis)

Argentina's sociodynamics exhibit a pattern of 'repeated collapse and incomplete restoration.' The 1976 military coup, 1983 democratization, 2001 default, 2015–2019 Macri austerity, 2019–2023 return of Peronism, and 2023 Milei shock therapy—this cycle is not a simple political rotation but has formed a TBI (Traumatic Big Bang Index) structure. A feedback loop is active wherein historical trauma is solidified into institutional distrust, which in turn justifies extreme choices.
1976–1983
Military Dictatorship (Proceso). Estimated 30,000 missing/dead. Archetypal trauma of economic distortion and institutional distrust. All three elements of TBI H·C·N exceed thresholds.
1983
Restoration of democracy. Inauguration of the Alfonsín government. However, failed to complete the transition due to hyperinflation (1989). The OLI structure remains intact.
2001–2002
Default, corralito, peso devaluation. Five presidents changed in two weeks. Imprinted on collective memory as the '2001 trauma' → direct comparison baseline with the current Milei model.
2015–2019
Macri austerity attempt. IMF $57B bailout. Failed re-election. Incomplete precedent of market-opening experiments—a structure that Milei references yet repeats.
2023.12
Milei inaugurated. 'Chainsaw' austerity begins. Immediate peso devaluation, elimination of subsidies, public spending cut by 30–50%. EPI spikes.
2024–2026
Success in curbing inflation (31.5%), but real wages fell below 2001 levels. 2,000 manufacturing businesses closed down, 73,000 employees laid off. Press blockade, $LIBRA cryptocurrency scandal, press access banned (2026.04). ADI §31 rebound phase.

§10 6-Layer Discourse Analysis

§10 · Six-Layer Discourse Analysis — Argentina

Layer 1 Data: Inflation 31.5%, poverty rate 31.6%, GDP +4.4%, FDI -$1.52B, RSF 98th. Surface indicators present a mixed picture of 'improvement' and 'deterioration.'

Layer 2 Interest Structure: The beneficiaries of Milei's austerity are financial asset holders and foreign debt creditors. The costs were paid by pensioners, informal workers, and manufacturing workers. CFD=0.78 is a material expression of this structure.

Layer 3 Historical Patterns: The collective memory of the 2001 default operates in two directions. To supporters, it gives a desire for differentiation ('it is different from back then'), and to opponents, a warning signal ('it is that pattern again'). History becomes an explosive when there is no reconciliation (Layer 3 Duality of the Six-Layer Discourse Reasoning Method).

Layer 4 Perspective Reversal: Milei's 60% approval rating is not simple economic satisfaction. The 'desire to punish La Casta (the establishment)' sustains this support. This is why his approval rating holds up even as the economy deteriorates.

Layer 5 Knowledge Framework: The Milei model is a radical application of Hayek-Mises liberalism. However, the abandonment of monetary sovereignty in a dollarized economy has shown a pattern of short-term stability and long-term growth deceleration in the comparative case (Ecuadorian dollarization in 2000).

Layer 6 Questions (Director's Judgment Domain): To curb inflation, is 'bringing real wages below 2001 levels' a justifiable means? Has this cost allocation received democratic approval? Λ¹² merely poses this question.

§13 · Conclusion Trajectory

Argentina is currently trapped between two numbers: inflation at 31.5% and real wages below 2001 levels. The former is hailed as proof of success, while the latter is calculated as the cost of success. Showing upon whom and how heavily that cost is concentrated is precisely what Λ¹² does.

In a situation where these three values—SSI (Social Structure Instability)=0.68, CFD (Capital Flight)=0.78, and EPI (Explosion Proximity)=0.72—simultaneously receive risk weights, a Λ of 0.451 indicates that it is still below the threshold, but it is also a warning that if even one of these three variables moves in a direction exceeding 0.65, it will rebound to over 0.48.

In April 2026, Milei emptied the entire government Press Briefing Room. It was a declaration of replacing the press with social media. What ADI §31 captures is precisely this moment—the process in which institutions, while alive on paper, are gradually emptied out in actual operation. The reason this is not a collapse is because Congress overrode two vetoes. The reason this is not safe is because the power of that Congress has diminished since the October 2025 elections.
