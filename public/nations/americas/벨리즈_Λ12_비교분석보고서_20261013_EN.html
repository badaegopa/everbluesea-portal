§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Consecutive Governance. The PUP remained in power with 26 seats, identical to 2020. The opposition leader lost his constituency to a candidate from his own party (Wikipedia · CBC Barbados).

Public Security. Police-recorded homicides were 87–89 in 2023–2024, tallied at approximately 21.7 per 100,000 people (Statista). Total major crimes decreased in the same year (Amandala).

Public Trends. ACLED protests ranged from 13 to 31 per year, excluding 76 events in 2021. Political violence is predominantly targeted at civilians (36 out of 48 events in 2024).
