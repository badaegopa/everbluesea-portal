2025-01
Constitutional Amendment for Co-Presidency — The National Assembly ratified the constitutional amendment (IDEA January 28, other sources January 30): Vice President Rosario Murillo as co-president without an election, presidential term 6 years. In the event of presidential vacancy, Murillo succeeds without a new election (Verfassungsblog, IDEA, ICTJ).
