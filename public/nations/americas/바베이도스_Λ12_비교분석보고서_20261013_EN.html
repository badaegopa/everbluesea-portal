The vote share was approximately 70% (69.8–70.1% across reports). A parliament with no elected opposition in the House of Assembly continues for the third consecutive term (Barbados Today).

The gap in official figures opens up in the electoral system: approximately 70% of the vote translated into 100% of the seats. Both numbers are official outcomes and products of the single-member district plurality system (Westminster model).
