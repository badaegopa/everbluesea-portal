Transition (2025~): Paz elected, right-wing coalition takes power. V-Dem classifies it as a "democratizing country." However, the economic crisis is projected to continue until 2027 (World Bank).

§ Comprehensive Conclusion Trajectory

The most dramatic contrast in the 3rd standalone edition lies between Uruguay (0.192) and Cuba (0.841). Both share the same continent, the same Spanish-speaking culture, and a similar population size. Yet, Lambda shows a 4.4-fold difference. It is self-evident that this difference does not stem from geography or language.

Bolivia inherited both the light (poverty reduction and political participation of indigenous peoples) and the shadow (politicization of the judiciary and economic illusions) of 20 years of MAS. Paraguay shows a paradox where economic growth and democratic backsliding coexist as a result of 77 years of ruling power. Cuba recorded the highest Lambda in history as external blockades and internal failures overlapped.

And Uruguay shows what a functioning democracy looks like — with no drama, in a way where the loser accepts the outcome and the winner-elect appoints a moderate minister of economy. For Λ¹² to reveal this contrast in figures, that alone is the purpose of this report.
