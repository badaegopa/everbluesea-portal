Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1999 · 2013
Chávez and Maduro — Hugo Chávez assumed power in 1999, succeeded by Nicolás Maduro in 2013 (general fact).
2014–2019
Economic Collapse and Great Migration — Following the drop in oil prices, hyperinflation and production collapse ensued, along with outward migration of approximately 7.9 million people (R4V platform tally, general fact). Major nationwide blackout in March 2019 (general fact). ACLED recorded 3,277 conflict fatalities in 2019.
2024-07-28
Presidential Election Dispute — The National Electoral Council (CNE) announced Maduro's victory, while the opposition claimed victory for Edmundo González. The Carter Center's final report in February 2025 supported the opposition's claim (compendium briefing).
2025-09~12
U.S. Military Vessel Strikes — U.S. Southern Command began striking vessels suspected of transporting narcotics, totaling 35 strikes and at least 115 deaths by the end of 2025. On December 24, the first direct U.S. military strike within Venezuelan territory occurred (compendium briefing).
2026-01-03
Maduro's Arrest — U.S. forces launched airstrikes on Fuerte Tiuna in Caracas and other targets, arresting President Maduro and his spouse and transporting them abroad. Delcy Rodríguez became acting president (compendium briefing).
2026-01-30 · 02-20
Oil Sector Opening and Amnesty — Amendment of the Hydrocarbons Law abolished the requirement for PDVSA to hold a majority stake in joint ventures, and U.S. Treasury General Licenses eased sanctions. In February, the Amnesty Law was promulgated, and the National Assembly announced the release and lifting of restrictions for 3,231 individuals (compendium briefing).
2026-07 · 09
Trial Schedule and Election Pledges — In July, the New York Federal District Court tentatively scheduled the trial of the Maduros for June 1, 2027. On September 24, the acting president promised elections at the UN General Assembly without specifying a date, and the U.S. removed Venezuela from its list of illicit drug transit/producing countries. On September 27, rallies demanding Machado's return and presidential elections were held in major cities (several hundred participants) (compendium briefing).
