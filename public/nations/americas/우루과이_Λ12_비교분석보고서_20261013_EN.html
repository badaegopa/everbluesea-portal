§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Public Safety. According to the Ministry of the Interior yearbook, homicides in 2025 totaled 371 (10.3 per 100,000 population), a 3.1% decrease from the previous year. In October 2025, the minister described it as an 'epidemic level of homicide rate' (montevideo.com.uy·El Observador).

Grassroots base. Under the new INE method, the 2025 income poverty rate is 16.6%, household-based 13.2%, extreme poverty 1.3%, and multidimensional poverty 18.7%. Under age 6 is 29.1%, and ages 6–12 is 27.3% — pointed out as the country in the region where the 'childification' of poverty is most pronounced (montevideo.com.uy).

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 47–113 per year from 2018 to 2024, with almost no political violence.
