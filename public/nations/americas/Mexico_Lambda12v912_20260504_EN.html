§03 · Historical Trajectory — Genealogy of Cartel-State Symbiosis

1929–2000
71 years of PRI one-party system. Establishment of an implicit coexistence structure between drug cartels and the state. This is the historical origin of Mexico's OLI.
2006–2012
Calderón declares 'Drug War'. Militarization (MFI) surges. Cartel fragmentation and escalating violence intensify. Estimated 150,000 deaths. The prototype of the paradox where militarization failed to reduce violence.
2018–2024
AMLO's 'Abrazos, no balazos' (Hugs, not bullets) policy. Informal non-violence pacts with cartels. 37 journalists killed (2019–2024). CPI plunges from 31 to 26.
2024.10~
Sheinbaum takes office. 'Operation Swarm' — arrest of cartel-linked politicians, including AMLO allies. Prosecution success rate at 30% (compared to Mexico's average of 7%). Structural reform attempts.
2026.04
US Department of Justice indicts 10 people, including Sinaloa Governor Rubén Rocha Moya (implicated in Sinaloa Cartel). Maximum diplomatic and domestic political crisis for Sheinbaum. GPI and CFD expected to rise in tandem.

§09 · Six-Layer Discourse Analysis — Mexico

Layer 1 Data: CPI 26 · RSF Around 140th · 37 Journalists Killed · Cartels Controlling 450+ Municipalities · US Indictment of Sinaloa Governor (2026.04.29)

Layer 2 Interest Structure: There are estimates that cartels generate 2 to 4% of Mexico's GDP. In this structure, cartels are not merely criminal organizations but a parallel state running the regional economy. The blurring of the boundaries between that parallel state and the official state is what makes OLI=0.72.

Layer 3 Historical Pattern: Calderón's militarization (2006) failed to lower the EPI. AMLO's 'hugs' (2018) lowered the CPI. If both approaches failed, it means the problem lies in the structure, not the strategy.

Layer 4 Perspective Inversion: It is notable that Sheinbaum's 'Operation Swarm' recorded a prosecution success rate of 30%. However, whether the indictment of the Sinaloa governor would have been possible without US pressure is another question. Do judicial sovereignty and anti-corruption function without external pressure?

Layer 5 Knowledge Framework: The judicial election system (2024) inherently possesses both the justification of 'democratic legitimacy' and the side effect of 'weakened judicial independence' simultaneously. If elected judges are captured by cartel election funds, ADI will skyrocket nonlinearly.

Layer 6 Question: In a society where the state and cartels partially coexist, what does the 'normalization of the state' mean?

§11 · PPR

PPR-1 · ADI Suppression — Post-Management of Judicial Election System
If there is no independent monitoring system to block cartel intervention in election funds after the implementation of the judicial election system, ADI could skyrocket to 0.75 or higher. Strengthening the fund-tracking capacity of election administration bodies and verifying candidate identities constitute the minimum line of defense.
PPR-2 · MFI Suppression — Nonlinear Risks of Militarization
What the 20 years since Calderón have proven is that rising MFI does not lower the EPI. There is scholarly evidence that diverting part of the militarization budget to regional judicial capacity and economic infrastructure could be a more effective path for EPI damping.
PPR-3 · GPI Management — Sovereignty Balance with the US
The indictment of the Sinaloa governor maximizes tension between Mexico's domestic judicial sovereignty and US intervention. For Sheinbaum, a 'preemptive containment' strategy that proactively conducts internal cleanups while reducing justifications for US intervention could be effective in suppressing GPI.

§12 · Conclusion Trajectory

Mexico is now seeing two forms of legitimacy competing in the same territory. One is the legitimacy of the official state created by the constitution and elections, and the other is the de facto legitimacy of the cartels that substantively control the regional economy and security. Λ¹² calls the point where these two legitimacies intersect the 'grey zone'.

Sheinbaum's Operation Swarm is the first systematic attempt to reduce this grey zone. The 30% prosecution success rate is unprecedented in Mexican history. However, the judicial election system was implemented around the same time. While forces to reduce OLI were acting on one side, a system that could expand ADI was put into operation on the other.

Λ=0.498. There is only 0.002 left until the threshold line of 0.500. This is not to claim numerical precision — it is a coordinate showing which section Mexico is currently in. In which direction the Sinaloa indictment of April 2026 will move this coordinate is a story yet unwritten.
