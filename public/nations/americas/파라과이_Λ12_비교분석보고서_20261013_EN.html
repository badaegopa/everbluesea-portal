§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1954~1989
Stroessner Regime — 35-year prolonged rule and 1989 coup (general fact). The Colorado Party continued to hold power for most of the period thereafter (general fact).
2021-03
Protests — Large-scale protests surrounding the COVID-19 response (general fact). ACLED demonstrations peaked at 1,264 events in 2021, the maximum in the recorded period.
2023-04-30
Presidential Election — Santiago Peña (Colorado Party) 43.93%, Efraín Alegre 28.25%, Paraguayo Cubas 23.56%. Colorado Party majority in Congress (Wikipedia).
2023-05
Election Fraud Claims — Cubas claimed election fraud and supporters protested. The Superior Tribunal of Electoral Justice denied the possibility of fraud. Acevedo demanded a 10% audit of electronic voting machines and manual tallying, while Alegre demanded a manual recount of 10% of ballot boxes. On May 5, Cubas was arrested on charges of disturbing public order (Semana · Poder360 · NTN24).
2024-02-14
Expulsion of Senator — Opposition Senator Kattya González expelled with 23 votes in favor for 'influence peddling.' The opposition asserted that the rules required 30 votes and that due process was violated, while the Organization of American States (OAS) expressed concern. Subsequently, the prosecution dismissed all charges cited as grounds for expulsion; Supreme Court ruling pending (as of 2025-02) (Buenos Aires Herald · ABC Color).
2025-09-28~10-03
Youth Protests — 'Generation Z' protests, estimated 600–1,000 participants, government deployed 3,000 police, 31 arrests (IDEA). The Chamber of Deputies condemned 'police repression.' Following indigenous groups joining, on October 3 the government withdrew the relocation of the National Institute of Indigenous Affairs (INDI) and accepted demands for the replacement of its president (IDEA · Wikipedia · LatinNews).
§ 03

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Institutional Debate. Regarding the expulsion of the senator, the ruling party stated that the evidence was 'sufficient and irrefutable,' while the opposition, church, business sector, and student groups opposed it (ICTJ · Buenos Aires Herald). This report records both sides' claims as they are.

Youth Demands. The protest organizers demanded anti-corruption measures, accountability, and social justice (IDEA). The government accepted demands related to the National Institute of Indigenous Affairs.

Public Trends. ACLED demonstrations numbered 281 in 2018, 740 in 2020, 1,264 in 2021, 1,105 in 2022, 795 in 2023, and 824 in 2024. Reported fatalities decreased from 43 in 2021 to 6 in 2024.
