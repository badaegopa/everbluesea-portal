§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Leadership (as of October 7, 2026, Compendium Briefing). President Rodrigo Paz Pereira (Christian Democratic Party, 2025-11-08~). Reorienting from a state-led economy toward market opening, and restoring ties with the United States to resume US Drug Enforcement Administration operations after 17 years.

May–June 2026. Transport workers, miners, and the COB mounted nationwide blockades protesting fuel prices, low-quality imported fuel, and land-related legislation, with miners demanding the president's resignation. The president temporarily relocated to Sucre, and the Ministers of Defense and Education resigned. The US government supported the Paz administration, characterizing the protests as a 'coup' (Compendium Briefing). This report records the positions of each party as stated.

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 310 in 2018, 726 in 2020, 948 in 2021, 1,133 in 2022, 1,208 in 2023, and 1,561 in 2024. Political violence fatalities were 26 in 2018 and 19 in 2024.
