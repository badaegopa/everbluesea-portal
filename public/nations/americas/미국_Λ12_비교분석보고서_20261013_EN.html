§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Trade and the Courts. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), following the Supreme Court ruling in February 2026, the administration shifted the legal basis of tariffs to a 150-day temporary tariff under Section 122 of the Trade Act. Measures after the expiration of the 150 days and the status of the federal budget and tariffs for the second half of the year were not officially confirmed even in the Series briefing.

Shutdown and Statistics. The BLS failed to release the October 2025 Employment Situation report. Although October values for the Current Employment Statistics (CES) establishment survey were published alongside November data, the Current Population Survey (CPS) household survey could not collect October data at all due to budget lapse and will not perform retrospective collection. For October consumer prices, neither the headline index nor the core index was released, and the November CPI published on December 18 partially omitted month-over-month percentage changes (BLS).

The Streets. The scale of the October 18, 2025 'No Kings' protest was contested, with organizers claiming approximately 7 million participants, independent and academic estimates ranging from 2 million to 5.2 million, and crowd counting analyst G. Elliott Morris estimating a median of 5 million (upper bound of 6.5 million). For the March 28, 2026 protest, only the organizers' claim of 'at least 8 million' was confirmed.

Public Trends. According to ACLED tabulations, protests decreased from 22,362 events in 2020 to 10,500 events in 2024, and political violence fatalities decreased from 98 in 2022 to 33 in 2024 (2020–2024).
