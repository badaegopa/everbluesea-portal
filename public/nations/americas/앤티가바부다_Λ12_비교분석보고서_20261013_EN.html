Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2013
CBI Program Codification into Law — Established by an act of Parliament with the goal of expanding foreign direct investment (CIJN).
2017-09
Hurricanes Irma and Maria — Prime Minister's remarks on the day: 90% of Barbuda structures destroyed. PDNA: approximately 95% of Barbuda housing damaged or destroyed, damage of 367.5 million Eastern Caribbean dollars ($136.1 million), losses of 51.2 million ($18.9 million), total damage and losses approximately 9% of 2016 GDP, recovery needs of $222.2 million. 2017 growth forecast revised from 4.6% to 3.5% (GFDRR).
2020
Pandemic — IMF staff: Economy contracted by more than 20% in 2020 (IMF 2022 mission press release).
2023-01-18
General Election — Antigua and Barbuda Labour Party (ABLP) won 9 of 17 seats (15 seats in 2018), United Progressive Party (UPP) 6 seats, Barbuda People's Movement 1 seat, Independent 1 seat. Vote share: ABLP 47%, UPP 45%. Prime Minister Browne achieves historic first third consecutive term (Wikipedia, Freedom House, Jamaica Observer).
