Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1984
Launch of Citizenship by Investment Program — Known as the world's oldest CBI (general fact).
2022-08-05
Snap General Election — Terrance Drew's Saint Kitts and Nevis Labour Party (SKNLP) won 6 of 11 seats, marking a change of government after approximately 7 years (St. Kitts & Nevis Information Service, Wikipedia).
2023
Peak in CBI Revenues — IMF: CBI revenues at 22% of GDP, slight fiscal surplus, debt at 54% of GDP (IMF PR 24/164).
2024
Fiscal Reversal — IMF: Fiscal deficit reached 11% of GDP due to a sharp drop in CBI revenues amid program-strengthening reforms (IMF PR 25/139).
2025
Persistent Deficit — IMF estimate: 2025 deficit at 11.7% of GDP, public debt at 58.4%, ongoing depletion of government deposits. The CBI windfall effects of 2021–2023 are dissipating (IMF press report).
2025-12
Announcement of Homicide Reduction — Government Budget Address: 28 homicides in 2024 (16 solved), 6 homicides year-to-date in 2025, a 78% decrease (St. Kitts & Nevis Information Service).

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Fiscal Structure. CBI revenues surged to 22% of GDP in 2023 before plunging in 2024, and during the same period, the fiscal balance shifted from a surplus to an 11% GDP deficit (IMF).

In 2023, the homicide rate compiled by World Bank-affiliated sources stood at 64.2 per 100,000, ranking 2nd among 145 comparable countries (Statistics of the World).

Public Trends. ACLED protests were 2 in 2018, 1 in 2020, and 0 in 2021, with records empty for 2022 and 2024.
