Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2021-04
La Soufrière Volcanic Eruption — Evacuation of northern residents (16,000–20,000 reported at the time, later estimated at approx. 22,400). The Minister of Finance preliminarily estimated April–May damages could reach 50% of GDP; subsequent financial requirements were assessed at $175 million, or 23% of 2021 GDP (Bloomberg · iWitness News).
2024-07-01
Hurricane Beryl — 90% of houses on Union Island severely damaged or destroyed (initial prime ministerial announcement), CDEMA estimated 98% of buildings. Over 1,000 people, representing 20% of the island's population, evacuated within 48 hours (One News St Vincent · World Bank).
2024~2025
Beryl Damage Assessment — Initial IMF estimate at 20–25% of GDP, World Bank assessment of Southern Grenadines damage at $230 million or 22% of GDP, Prime Minister's estimate around EC$1 billion or approx. 30% of GDP, Minister of Finance's final estimate exceeding $1 billion or one-third of GDP (iWitness News · World Bank).
2025-11-27
General Election · Change of Government — New Democratic Party (NDP) won 14 out of 15 seats with 57.74% of the vote (37,207 votes), Unity Labour Party (ULP) 1 seat with 42.14%, voter turnout 62.53%. Godwin Friday assumed office as Prime Minister, replacing the Gonsalves administration after approximately 25 years (Wikipedia · Searchlight · Jamaica Gleaner).
