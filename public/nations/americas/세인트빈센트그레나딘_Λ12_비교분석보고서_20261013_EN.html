§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2021-04
La Soufrière Volcanic Eruption — Evacuation of northern residents (16,000–20,000 reported at the time, later estimated at approximately 22,400). In April–May, the Minister of Finance preliminarily estimated damages could reach up to 50% of GDP; subsequent resource requirements were calculated at $175 million, or 23% of 2021 GDP (Bloomberg, iWitness News).
2024-07-01
Hurricane Beryl — 90% of homes on Union Island severely damaged or destroyed (initial Prime Minister announcement), CDEMA estimated 98% of buildings. Over 1,000 people, representing 20% of the island's population, evacuated within 48 hours (One News St Vincent, World Bank).
2024~2025
Beryl Damage Assessment — Initial IMF estimate: 20–25% of GDP; World Bank estimate for southern Grenadines damage: $230 million, or 22% of GDP; Prime Minister: approximately 1 billion Eastern Caribbean dollars, or about 30% of GDP; Minister of Finance final: exceeding $1 billion, or one-third of GDP (iWitness News, World Bank).
2025-11-27
General Election and Regime Change — New Democratic Party (NDP) won 14 of 15 seats with 57.74% of the vote (37,207 votes); Unity Labour Party (ULP) won 1 seat with 42.14%; voter turnout was 62.53%. Prime Minister Godwin Friday took office, ending roughly 25 years of the Gonsalves government (Wikipedia, Searchlight, Jamaica Gleaner).
