§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2020-03-02~08-02
Five Months of Vote Counting — Following the March 2 election, disputes arose as the Region 4 tabulation was conducted via spreadsheet instead of polling station Statement of Polls; recount began in May; on July 30, the Court of Appeal ruled that results be finalized based on the recount; on August 2, the Guyana Elections Commission (GECOM) declared President Irfaan Ali the winner. Carter Center: an outcome that should have taken days took five months, concluding in a peaceful transfer of power (Carter Center · Global Voices).
2022~2024
Oil Growth — IMF: Average annual real GDP growth of 47% since 2022 (highest in the world); in 2024, oil GDP grew by about 58% and non-oil GDP by over 13% (IMF PR 25/132).
2024
Growth Rate Announcements — President: estimated at 49.7% for the first half. Ministry of Finance: projected at 42.3% annually (non-oil 11.8%). IMF: projected at 33.9% in April 2024, subsequently revised to 43.8% (Department of Public Information · OilNOW).
2025-09-01
General Election — Ruling PPP/C secured 36 of 65 seats (an increase of 3 seats), newly formed WIN took 16 seats (24.90% or 24.87% of votes), APNU 12 seats, FGM 1 seat. WIN, led by U.S.-sanctioned businessman Azruddin Mohamed, became the main opposition party, altering the long-standing two-party structure. Reports diverge on the timing of the result declaration, citing either the night of September 6 or midnight of September 7 (Wikipedia · OilNOW · LatinNews).
