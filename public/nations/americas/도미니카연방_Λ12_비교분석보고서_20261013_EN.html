§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2017-09-18
Hurricane Maria — Total damage and loss of approximately 226% of 2016 GDP (damage $930.9 million, loss $380.2 million), approximately 90% of homes damaged, complete shutdown of electricity supply. Government reported 30 dead and 34 missing (as of November 2017); other sources report higher casualties (PDNA · GFDRR).
2017
IMF Assessment — Analyzed that inflows from the Citizenship by Investment program reached approximately 10% of GDP, deepening fiscal dependence (IMF Selected Issues).
2022-12-06
Snap General Election — Opposition parties UWP and DFP boycotted citing inadequate electoral reform. The ruling Labour Party won 19 of 21 seats, with 6 candidates elected unopposed. Turnout fell below a majority for the first time in history at 31.6%. CARICOM observer mission assessed the election as peaceful and orderly while looking forward to electoral reforms (Wikipedia · Freedom House · CARICOM).
2024
IMF Consultations (Media Reports) — 2022/23 fiscal year CBI revenue reached 37% of GDP, primary fiscal balance excluding CBI recorded a deficit, recommended reducing 'excessive reliance' (IMI Daily report).
2025-03
Passage of Electoral Reform Bills — Bills tabled in November 2024 regarding an independent electoral commission and voter registration were passed. Opposition criticized the lack of consultation; 24 people arrested during protests (Wikipedia).
§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Fiscal Structure. The share of Citizenship by Investment revenue in GDP is reported differently depending on the source and year: approximately 10% (IMF 2017), approximately 20% (IMI independent analysis), 31% (Astons), and 37% (IMF 2024 report). The IMF has consistently recommended using these inflows for savings and investment rather than current spending, citing the risk of sudden cessation (IMI Daily).

Electoral System. Confrontation over the electoral system continued following the 2022 opposition boycott and the 2025 passage of electoral reform legislation (Freedom House · Wikipedia). Subsequent election schedules and results were not confirmed in this investigation.

Public Trends. ACLED protests recorded 11 incidents in 2018, 5 in 2020, 13 in 2021, 16 in 2022, 4 in 2023, and 3 in 2024.
