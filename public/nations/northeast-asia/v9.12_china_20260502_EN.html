Key concepts used in this report

TBI (Trauma Big Bang Index)
Historical Shock (H) × Cultural Unassimilation (C) × Narrative Non-integration (N). Structural risk signal when threshold θ=0.70 is exceeded.

A structure in which the leadership diverts targets or suppresses public dissatisfaction so that it cannot be directed inward.
ADI (AI Dependency Index)
The ratio of external AI platform dependency and data sovereignty loss rate divided by self AI capability and infrastructure. The lower it is, the more independent the AI state.

Composite calibration coefficient for statistical reliability, institutional maturity, and data accessibility. China OLI=0.51 applied.

DCI (Generational Fault-line Index)
Fault-line of values and system perception between the generation that experienced growth (parents) vs the generation that experienced stagnation (children). Second derivative of irreversible fracture.

General Secretary Xi Jinping abolished the presidential term limits through a constitutional amendment in 2018, establishing a de facto lifelong ruling structure.
