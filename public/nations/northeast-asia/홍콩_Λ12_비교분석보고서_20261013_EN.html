§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Public Safety. The fire at Wong Fuk Oi in Kowloon City in November 2025 was Hong Kong's worst fire in decades, with 168 deaths confirmed (NBC). The Chief Executive directed the establishment of an independent inquiry commission led by a judge; the police arrested construction company personnel on charges of gross negligence manslaughter; and the Independent Commission Against Corruption investigated corruption related to the construction. Reports differ on whether the fire-retardant standards for external netting were met. Within the scope of reports reviewed in this investigation, no large-scale collective protests occurred after the fire.

National Security and Judiciary. On February 9, 2026, the High Court sentenced Jimmy Lai, founder of Apple Daily, to 20 years in prison, and eight others, including former Apple Daily staff members, to 6 to 10 years. Human rights organizations and governments in the UK and the US criticized the sentences. The Hong Kong government stated that the economy had reached its highest level since the implementation of the National Security Law on the sixth anniversary of the law in June 2026 (RTHK; government claim). In March 2026, China summoned the US consul general in Hong Kong over a new regulation penalizing the refusal to provide mobile phone passwords (SCMP).

Economy. The fiscal secretary assessed that the economy in Q1 2026 showed resilience despite stock market volatility and Middle East conflicts (quoted by SCMP; government statement). The government is preparing for public consultation on its first five-year plan. This report records only government claims as government claims and does not use them as numerical evidence. The Series includes Hong Kong as a data-edition country and does not publish a narrative briefing.

§ 03-B

Published Figures vs. Independent Sources — Observing the Gap

The gap in Hong Kong is largest in the way inequality is measured. The Census and Statistics Department publishes the Gini coefficient used for international comparisons, while independent organizations calculate the income ratio between the top and bottom households using the same department's household survey data. Because the two figures measure different objects and years, they are presented side by side as originally stated (DECISIONS 17·19). On the engine side, A3 (measured: Gini coefficient) is missing and BSLI is not calculated, so this gap does not enter the Series value.

The decisive variable distinguishing SN from BH is C_internal (elite internal conflict energy). This is a metaphor and does not claim astrophysical correspondence.

On the basis of Series values alone, the pathway cannot be determined. Canonical values for E_A2 and C_internal are unavailable, and the verifiable upper bound of E_A2, 0.8355, is higher than the WD (below 0.3) and SN (0.3–0.5) ranges.

Event records present a different picture. The large-scale protests of 2019 disappeared from the streets after the National Security Law in 2020, and subsequent political mobilization has declined sharply through judicial processing and electoral system reforms. This sequence resembles the white dwarf (latent) description in the UI definition.
