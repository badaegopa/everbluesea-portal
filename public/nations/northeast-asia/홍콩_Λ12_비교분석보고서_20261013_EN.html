§ 01

Executive Summary

Hong Kong's state vector shows high values in Infrastructure (A1 0.9773), Mobility (A12 0.9053), Institutional Trust (A2 0.8355), and Governance (A8 0.7640), and low values in Economic Stability (A4 0.0964), Education Access (A5 0.1766), Inequality (A10 0.0304), and External Shocks (A11 0.1804). Among these, the measured inputs for A4, A5, and A10 are GDP·manufacturing share, R&D/GDP, and tourist arrivals, respectively; thus, the characteristics of a financial and service-oriented city with a very small manufacturing share largely account for the low scores. The only dimension within the pressure band (P ≥ 0.446) is A5 (P 0.515).

The most distinct change in the nine-year time series is that pressure on the absorber binary A2·A8 has steadily grown. The pressure on A2 rose from 0.093 in 2016 to 0.158 in 2024, and on A8 from 0.132 to 0.207, while the policy shock mass m = A2·A8 decreased from 0.7112 to 0.5556. Although the values themselves are still in the upper tier of the region, the trajectory has moved in the same direction throughout the nine years. The grassroots base BSLI is not measured due to the absence of canonical data. From an observer's perspective, while Hong Kong's measured upper-tier indicators remain high, they are consistently weakening, and the grassroots base itself remains beyond the engine's view.

§ 03

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Public Safety. In November 2025, the Wang Fuk Court fire in Tai Po District was the worst fire in Hong Kong in decades, with fatalities confirmed at 168 (NBC). The Chief Executive ordered a judge-led independent commission of inquiry, police arrested contractor officials on charges of gross negligence manslaughter, and the ICAC investigated corruption related to the construction work. Reports diverged on whether the exterior netting met flame-retardant standards. Within the scope of reports verified in this investigation, no large-scale collective protests were identified following the fire.

National Security and Judiciary. On February 9, 2026, the High Court sentenced Apple Daily founder Jimmy Lai to 20 years' imprisonment, and eight others, including former Apple Daily staff, were sentenced to 6 to 10 years. Human rights organizations and the British and US governments criticized the rulings. Marking the 6th anniversary of the National Security Law in June 2026, the Hong Kong government stated that the economy had reached peak levels since the law's enactment (RTHK, government claim). In March 2026, China summoned the US Consul General to Hong Kong regarding new regulations penalizing the refusal to provide mobile phone passwords (SCMP).

Economy. The Financial Secretary assessed that the economy demonstrated resilience in Q1 2026 amid stock market volatility and Middle East conflicts (SCMP quote, government statement). The government is preparing public consultations for its first Five-Year Plan. This report records government announcements solely as government claims and does not use them as quantitative evidence.

Concurrent Weakening of the Absorber Binary (A2·A8). A2 (measured input CPI·Control of Corruption) and A8 (WGI 6 indicators) form a binary moving together with a coupling coefficient of +0.9554, serving as core axes through which society absorbs shocks. One should note that this captures demographic movements rather than survey-measured 'social trust.'

A10 (tourist arrivals) has been frozen via carryover since 2020, so post-pandemic tourism recovery is not yet reflected.

Data Absent
No corresponding entity in bsli_timeseries_v12_20260726.json — Because the World Bank PIP does not provide income percentile data for the Hong Kong SAR, F_ML·D·H_s cannot be calculated using the canonical method. Substitute calculations using alternative statistics are avoided as they would yield an index divergent from the canonical version.

Hong Kong's BSLI is absent from both canonical files (139-country canonical and consistent-criteria time-series edition). This absence is significant. Hong Kong is a city frequently cited for having among the world's highest housing cost burdens, and the BSLI terms H_s (housing stress) and H (housing adequacy) are precisely designed to measure that domain. This indicates that what the engine most critically needs to observe is currently unobservable. Rather than performing arbitrary calculations with substitute indicators, it is retained as 'Data Absent'.

Collapse Path Classification — WD / SN / BH

Pathway classification criteria (UI canonical, stellar evolution metaphor)
WD: E_A2 < 0.3, C_internal < 0.4 · SN: E_A2 0.3~0.5, C_internal > 0.5 · BH: E_A2 < 0.2, C_internal > 0.7
White Dwarf (WD) = suppression and latency · Supernova (SN) = collapse and regeneration · Black Hole (BH) = implosion and irreversibility
The decisive variable distinguishing SN from BH is C_internal (elite internal conflict energy). This does not claim an astrophysical correspondence, but is a metaphor.

The pathway cannot be determined based on canonical numerical criteria. There are no canonical values for E_A2 and C_internal, and the verifiable upper bound for E_A2 of 0.8355 is higher than the ranges for WD (below 0.3) and SN (0.3–0.5).

The event records show a different picture. The large-scale protests of 2019 vanished from the streets following the 2020 National Security Law, and subsequent political mobilization was substantially curtailed through judicial proceedings and electoral system overhaul. This sequence resembles White Dwarf (suppression and latency) in the UI definition. In other words, the numerical values (high A2·A8) and the event pattern (latency following suppression) point to different pathways. This report does not choose one over the other but records the discrepancy as it stands. One plausible explanation is that the empirical inputs for A2 (CPI, control of corruption) and the WGI indicator group for A8 heavily reflect administrative efficiency and corruption control, thus under-reflecting the curtailment of citizens' political voice channels. This is an instance of the 'notation–measurement gap' and requires canonical verification. The three cells below are conditions for each pathway, not probabilities.
