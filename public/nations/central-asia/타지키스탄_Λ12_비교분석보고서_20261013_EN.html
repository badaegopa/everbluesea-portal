§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Economy Outside Borders. According to the World Bank, migrant worker remittances in 2024 were approximately $5.8 billion, representing 45.3% of GDP (cited by Times of Central Asia). Most remittances originate from Russia. The government announced an 8.4% growth rate for 2025 (President's annual address, Statistical Agency). The same report noted that analysts point out a low statistical baseline and dependence on state-led investment.

Settlement of Borders. The 2025 border treaty with Kyrgyzstan and the tri-border junction treaty formally settled the undelimited border segments that had been the cause of armed clashes in 2021 and 2022. Within the verified scope, there have been no reports of large-scale border clashes since 2023, and ACLED fatalities were 6 in 2023 and 0 in 2024.

Domain of Control. Amnesty International's 2025 report (published 2026-04) documented the deaths of five Pamiri activists in custody between January and July 2025, lengthy prison sentences for two journalists (10 years and 4 months; 8 years), and impunity for torture. The Series includes this country as a data edition without narrative briefings — this section was written from public sources within the scope of this investigation.
