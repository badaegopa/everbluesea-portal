2026-02-10
Dissolution of Co-rule — Following a letter signed by 75 prominent figures demanding early presidential elections, the president dismissed Deputy Prime Minister and GKNB Chairman Tashiev, purged close associates, and reorganized the GKNB (Series briefing · East Asia Forum).

Suspended Sentence and Re-election Bid — In July, Tashiev was sentenced to a 4-year suspended prison term on charges of attempting to incite unrest. § 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Power Structure. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), since February 2026, the president has been removing close associates of the former GKNB chairman from parliament, the cabinet, and local government, restructuring the GKNB, and realigning the power structure around a single presidential axis. Reports indicate that the practice of the GKNB extracting money from business suspects is being reduced.

Term-Length Controversy. Under the 2021 Constitution, the presidential term is 5 years, but Japarov was elected to a 6-year term, leading to interpretive debate. The date for the 2027 presidential election varies across reports between January 24 and 27 (Series data note).

Public Trends. ACLED recorded protests peaking at 451 cases in 2021, decreasing to 145 cases in 2024. In early 2026, a letter demanding early presidential elections and the detention of signatories were documented.
