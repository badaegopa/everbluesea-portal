§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Power Structure. According to the National Analysis Compendium briefing (as of 2026-10-07), since February 2026, the president has been restructuring the power apparatus into a single presidential axis by purging confidants of the former GKNB chairman across parliament, cabinet, and local governments, and reforming the GKNB. The practice of the GKNB extorting money from business suspects was reported to be scaling down.

Term Length Debate. Under the 2021 constitution, the presidential term is 5 years, but Japarov was elected to a 6-year term, leading to interpretive disputes. The date for the 2027 presidential election varies between January 24 and 27 across reports (Compendium data note).

Public Trends. ACLED protests peaked at 451 events in 2021 and decreased to 145 events in 2024. In early 2026, the letter demanding early presidential elections and the detention of signatories were recorded.

The determining variable separating SN from BH is C_internal (elite internal conflict energy). This is a metaphor and does not claim an astrophysical correspondence.

E_A2 (institutional trust effect) is on the lower condition side because A2 and A8 are both within the band. For C_internal (elite internal conflict), a public signal was observed with the rupture between the President and the GKNB Chairman in February 2026.

Current observations show both elements toward the BH condition variables (low E_A2 + C_internal signal) and a restructuring that absorbs them around the president. This is an enumeration of observations, not an assessment.
