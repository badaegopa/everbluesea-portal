§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Economic Opening. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government is moving toward trade and regulatory opening aimed at WTO accession, aligning 68 legal acts with WTO standards during 2025 and abolishing mandatory certification for 747 items. Under IMF evaluation, the real growth rate in 2025 was 7.7%, and the year-end inflation rate was 7.3% (reported by Kun.uz and Times of Central Asia).

Poverty Figures. According to a survey conducted by the State Committee on Statistics in collaboration with the World Bank, the national poverty line-based poverty rate was officially published as 11% in 2023 → 8.9% in 2024 → 5.8% in 2025 (announced by the Center for Economic Research and Reforms CERR; same figures in World Bank country outlooks). The baseline differs from the figures based on the international poverty line (§03-B).

Domain of Control. The same briefing and the HRW 2026 annual report noted that the stance of controlling religion and expression remains maintained. In 2025, at least 14 individuals were prosecuted for 'separatist' posts related to Karakalpakstan, and cases of bloggers being forcibly placed in psychiatric institutions were reported. Within the verified scope (2024–2026), there are no reports of large-scale collective action. According to ACLED tallies, protests peaked at 245 cases in 2021 and dropped to 92 cases in 2024.
