§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Economic Opening. According to the Country Analysis Series Briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government is moving toward trade and regulatory liberalization with the goal of WTO accession, aligning 68 legal acts with WTO standards during 2025 and abolishing mandatory certification for 747 items. Under IMF assessment, real growth rate in 2025 was 7.7% and year-end inflation rate was 7.3% (reports by Kun.uz and Times of Central Asia).

Poverty Figures. In a survey conducted by the State Statistics Committee together with the World Bank, the poverty rate based on the national poverty line was officially announced as 11% in 2023 → 8.9% in 2024 → 5.8% in 2025 (published by the Center for Economic Research and Reforms CERR, same figures in World Bank country outlook). The threshold differs from figures based on the international poverty line (§03-B).

Domain of Control. The same briefing and the HRW 2026 annual report noted that the stance of controlling religion and expression is maintained. In 2025, at least 14 individuals were prosecuted for 'separatist' posts related to Karakalpakstan, and cases of bloggers being forcibly committed to psychiatric institutions were reported. Within the verified scope (2024–2026), there are no reports of large-scale collective action. According to ACLED tallies, protests peaked at 245 in 2021 and stood at 92 in 2024.
