§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Enforcement of Social Media Blocking. The Prime Minister's Office announced that over 4.7 million accounts for users under 16 were deactivated, deleted, or restricted in the first month of implementation in January 2026. The eSafety Commissioner stated that some minor accounts remain active and it is premature to declare full compliance. Subsequent first official compliance report revealed that while overall account holdings decreased, a substantial number of minors under 16 maintained their accounts, identifying Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and YouTube as investigation targets. Reddit complied with the law while filing a constitutional challenge (Reuters report).

After Bondi. The government pursued banning hate groups (outside terror group designation), streamlining visa cancellations, restricting firearm possession, and establishing a national firearms buyback scheme. The buyback was structured with 50% cost-sharing between federal and state governments; reports indicated Queensland, Tasmania, and the Northern Territory had objections (Compendium briefing). The status of the Royal Commission and trends in the second half of 2026 were also unconfirmed in the Compendium briefing.

Public Trends. According to ACLED tallies, protests increased from 708 in 2021 to 1,040 in 2024, with political violence fatalities at zero except for seven in 2022 (2021–2024). New South Wales' post-Bondi protest restrictions are in tension with this trend.
