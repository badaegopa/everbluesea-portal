§ 01

Executive Summary

Papua New Guinea's trajectory: all dimensions in 2024 P lie above the band (0.4458) in the order A1 Infrastructure (measured: electricity and internet 0.6776), A5 (measured: R&D/GDP 0.6234), A7 (0.5725), A8 Governance (0.4803), A2 Institutional Trust (0.4625), and A4 Economic Stability (0.4536). On the political axis, 2024 was the year of three events: The Port Moresby and Lae riots began in January with protests over police and public servant pay cuts (at least 22 dead), the Enga tribal massacre in February, and the Enga landslide in May. The Autonomous Bougainville Government announced plans to declare independence on September 1, 2027, and Prime Minister Marape did not commit to a timeline, citing parliamentary ratification procedures.

The gap in published figures is largest in 'the act of counting itself.' The 2024 census counted 10,185,363 people, but prior to that, the statistical office's model estimate was 1.178 million. Enga landslide victims were reported by the government as over 2,000 and by the International Organization for Migration (IOM) as 670 — recovered bodies numbered five in the first few days.

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1975
Independence — Gained independence from Australian trusteeship. It is a multi-ethnic state composed of over 800 language groups (general fact).
1988–1998
Bougainville Civil War — The secessionist conflict stemming from the Panguna mine dispute ended with a ceasefire in 1998 and a peace agreement in 2001 (general fact).
2019-11 · 12
Bougainville Referendum — In an unbinding independence referendum, an overwhelming majority voted for independence (general fact, vote share original source not cross-checked). The PNG parliament's ratification of the result became a subsequent point of contention.
2024-01-10
'Black Thursday' Riots — Protests erupted over police and public servant pay cuts explained by the government as 'administrative error,' spreading looting and arson in Port Moresby and Lae. Fatalities rose from 15 initially → at least 22 (15 in Port Moresby, 7 in Lae) (Islands Business, Inquirer). The government declared a state of emergency.
2024-02
Enga Tribal Massacre — A tribal ambush killed many. Police revised the death toll to 26, but reports diverged between 26 and 64 (AP, etc.). There are analyses suggesting that Enga tribal violence escalated following the 2022 election.
2024-05-24
Enga Landslide — Yambari village was buried. The acting National Disaster Management Center informed the UN of over 2,000 burials in a letter, while IOM maintained its estimate at 670 (AP · NBC).
2024-06 ~ 2025-10
Census — From June 16, 2024 to October, 10,185,363 people were counted across 30,419 enumeration areas (final figure: October 2025). The previous model estimate was 11.78 million (BPS · UNFPA).
2027-09-01 (planned)
Bougainville Independence Declaration Plan — President Taurama of the Bougainville Autonomous Government announced plans for a unilateral declaration of independence on September 1, 2027. Prime Minister Marape stated that 'the schedule cannot be promised in advance' and emphasized parliamentary procedures (BenarNews · PINA).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Bougainville. Prime Minister Marape views the 2027 deadline as a deadline for 'settlement,' not independence, arguing that independence is one of several options requiring PNG parliamentary ratification. Opposition leader Belden Namado also argued that parliamentary ratification was necessary. Analysts believe unilateral declaration remains possible if negotiations completely break down, but a return to civil war is unlikely (USIP, etc.). The latest developments in 2026 could not be confirmed during this survey.

Ethnic Violence. Ethnic clashes involving illegal miners and mercenaries continue in the Enga Highlands (AP). According to ACLED tallies, conflict fatalities rose from 241 in 2021 to 349 in 2023 and 325 in 2024.

Challenges of Aggregation. The 2011 census was criticized for flaws (costing 150 million kina), with some electoral districts having voter rolls larger than the surveyed population (BenarNews). In disaster response, population uncertainty led to death estimates ranging from dozens to thousands.

The decisive variable distinguishing SN from BH is C_internal (elite internal conflict energy). This is a metaphor and does not claim astrophysical correspondence.

E_A2 (institutional trust effect) is a low-side condition because A2 and A8 are both within the band. C_internal (elite internal conflict) is observed in election competition and Bougainville ratification debates, but most of this country's violence occurs between groups outside the state (tribes).

Compared to the single-state collapse framework of WD/SN/BH, it has aspects closer to the 'fragmented' type (MMR·BFA·COD) of D-3 in the authoritative edition — although it is a form where the state's reach itself is narrow rather than armed rebels seizing territory. The 2027 Bougainville event is an observation point. This is not an assessment, but a listing of observations.
