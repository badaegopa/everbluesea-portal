§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2006
Military Coup — Commander Frank Bainimarama's coup, new constitution in 2013, election in 2014 (general facts). Year prior to the trajectory.
2020
Tourism Shutdown and Contraction — Tourism (about 38% of GDP, UNDP estimate) virtually halted due to border closures. Central Bank Governor forecast −21.7%, IMF estimate −15.7%, government subsequent announcement −15.2% (record high) (OECD · IMF · Fiji Ministry of Finance).
2022-12-14~24
General Election and Regime Change — Out of 55 seats, Fiji First 26, United Fiji Party 21, National Federation Party 5, SODELPA 3. A three-party coalition elected Citybeni Labuka as prime minister by secret ballot in parliament 28 to 27. Bainimarama and Fiji First refused to accept the results for several days immediately following the election (Wikipedia · Al Jazeera · AP).
2023-10 ~ 2024-11
Trial of a Former Prime Minister — October 2023 Magistrate acquitted → March 2024 High Court convicted → Magistrate 'absolute discharge' → High Court sentenced to one year imprisonment → November 2024 released after serving six months. In October 2025, convicted in a separate case, 12-month prison sentence suspended for three years (JURIST · ODPP · Asia Pacific Report).
2025-03 ~ 08
Constitutional Amendment Requirements — A bill to ease the March constitutional amendment requirements fell one vote short of a three-quarters majority of all members. In response to the government's request, the Supreme Court issued an advisory opinion on August 29: a two-thirds majority of all members and a simple majority in a referendum would suffice for a constitutional amendment (IDEA · ConstitutionNet).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Constitutional Order. The issue that the 2013 Constitution's amendment requirements practically prevent amendments was settled in the Supreme Court's 2025 advisory opinion. The Supreme Court conditionally recognized the 2013 Constitution (ConstitutionNet).

Tourism Economy. After contracting in 2020, it recovered with the resumption of tourism (general fact). Engine A4 (measured: GDP · manufacturing) ranks second in pressure.

Public Trends. ACLED protests were 12 in 2021, 2 in 2022, 9 in 2023, and 12 in 2024. There are no conflict fatalities.
