§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

External Finance. Funds from the Compact of Free Association are divided into trust funds, debt repayments, government operations, and infrastructure, flowing in over 20 years. The 2025 agreement to accept transferees includes support for pension reform to prevent collapse (Kaniva Tonga).

Tourism. Monthly reports from the Palau Visitors Authority indicated that Taiwanese tourists dropped from 1,596 in June to 1,274 in July following the airport incident, while Chinese tourists increased from 1,194 to 1,709; however, the reporting year could not be verified (Island Times).

Public Trends. ACLED has virtually no events for this country. There are no reports of large-scale collective action within the scope of verification.
