Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2018-04~05
Regime Change — Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan takes office following large-scale protests (general fact). ACLED: 369 protests in 2018.
2020-09~11
Second Karabakh War — Ministry of Health of Armenia (mid-November): forensic examination of 2,317 military remains. Later tallies: 3,825 military deaths and 187 missing (Ahram · Wikipedia).
2023-09
Displacement of Karabakh Residents — Armenian government: 100,417 people. UNHCR: over 106,000 recorded. Reports also indicate that approximately 120,000 people left (news synthesis).
2024-02~08
Suspension of CSTO Participation — In February, the Prime Minister announced a de facto freeze, and on August 31, a freeze at all levels of participation was confirmed. The Kremlin stated that it had not received an official withdrawal notice (Euromaidan Press · Armenpress).
2024-04~06
Tabusi Border Protests — Protests led by Archbishop Bagrat Galstyan against the return of four border villages spread to Yerevan. One rally drew estimates of 18,000–20,000 participants (the largest since 2018), and police announced the arrest of 196 people in a single day (Novaya Gazeta Europe · PBS · Mirror-Spectator).
2025-08-08
Joint Statement of Washington — Joint request to sign an agreement on peace and to dissolve the Minsk Group. Azerbaijan demanded a constitutional amendment renouncing territorial claims (OSW · ARKA).
2026-06-07
General Election — Central Electoral Commission: Civil Contract 49.74%, 64 of 105 seats (61 + 3 minority seats), Strong Armenia Union 29 seats, Armenian Union 12 seats. It fell short of the constitutional amendment threshold of 70 seats. The opposition rejected the results and demanded a rerun. The commission invalidated results in three of 2,005 polling stations but decided not to hold a rerun (JAMnews · PISM · Interfax).
