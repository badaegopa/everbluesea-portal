§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Government Replacement. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the RNI-led three-party coalition that took power in 2021 saw its RNI seats decline from 102 to 66, and PAM has become the largest party, with coalition formation underway. Following GenZ 212, the king urged accelerated social reform, and the government promised expanded health and education support in the 2026 budget.

Poverty Statistics. The High Commission for Planning (HCP) reported that the multidimensional poverty rate fell from 11.9% in 2014 to 6.8% in 2024 based on the 2024 census, and that 72% of the poor live in rural areas (Hespress). The UNDP 2025 report counted approximately 2.4 million people in multidimensional poverty — because the methods and years differ, they are not compared directly.

Public Trends. GenZ 212-related trials continued into June 2026. Whether protests resumed during 2026 cannot be confirmed within the scope of the Series. The Western Sahara issue is moving toward negotiations based on a self-governance proposal under Resolution 2797.
