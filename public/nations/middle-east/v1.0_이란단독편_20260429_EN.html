§0 Overview
§0 · Overview
Where is Iran Now?

The protests, which began on December 28, 2025, with economic protests by Tehran merchants, transformed into the largest uprising since the 1979 Islamic Revolution in just two weeks. The massacre carried out by the Revolutionary Guards (IRGC) over two days on January 8–9 resulted in at least 12,000 to over 36,500 deaths, a figure that is not yet finalized. Khamenei was killed in a joint US-Israeli airstrike on February 28, and a ceasefire was established on April 8, but negotiations are deadlocked. Now, Iranian workers are taking to the streets again. Labor Day on May 1 is approaching.

This report dissects Iran through the Λ¹² v9.11 framework. It simultaneously reads six layers: economic destruction (BRI, LPI_net, F_reach), leader-public disconnect (Σ), accumulation and explosion of resistance resilience (SR), traumatic fuse (TBI), capital flow reversal (CFD), and the parliament's trigger-suppression tactics.

§6 · CFD
Capital Flow Orientation — The Paradox of the IRGC Economy

The reason Iran's CFD is unique is the IRGC is the economy itself. The IRGC handles about 50% of oil exports, collects Strait of Hormuz tolls, and is directly involved in major industries such as petrochemicals, construction, and telecommunications. In this structure, CFD does not head toward the masses — capital flows from the IRGC to proxy wars in Lebanon, Iraq, and Yemen.

─── Iran CFD Structure ─── Masses' Taxes, Oil Revenues ↓ IRGC Internal Circulation ↓ ┌───────────────────┐ │ Hezbollah Support │ ← "Neither Gaza nor Lebanon, but Iran" │ Hamas Support │ Economic basis of protesters' slogans │ Houthi Support │ │ Nuclear/Missile Program │ ← 150% increase in security budget └───────────────────┘ ↑ Masses' wage increase at 2/5 level F_reach 0.32 Meat prices exceed minimum wage

Since the war, the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz has cut off the IRGC's main source of revenue. Paradoxically, this has made the IRGC more dangerous — armed groups that have lost their economic base control their interiors more violently. April's unpaid wages and accelerated executions are in the same vein.
