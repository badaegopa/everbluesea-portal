Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2010
World Cup Bid — Selection as host of the 2022 FIFA World Cup (general fact). Subsequently, large-scale infrastructure construction and an influx of migrant workers.
2017-06~2021-01
Severance of Diplomatic Relations with Neighboring Countries — Severance of diplomatic relations and blockade by Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, and Egypt; lifted by the Al-Ula Declaration in 2021 (general fact). In the trajectory, A8 fell by +0.0151 and +0.0273 in 2017 and 2018.
2021-02
'6,500 People' Report — The Guardian: more than 6,500 South Asian migrant workers died after the bid was won (not all were connected to World Cup projects). Qatari authorities disputed this, arguing that it attributed all foreign worker deaths in the country to the World Cup (CBS · UPI).
2021-10
First Shura Council Election — 30 of 45 seats elected for the first time by vote (general fact, IDEA).
2022-11
World Cup and '400–500 People' — Al-Thawadi, Secretary-General of the organizing committee, stated in an interview that worker deaths related to World Cup projects numbered 'approximately 400, 400–500'. The organizing committee explained this as national statistics on work-related fatalities across all sectors and nationalities from 2014 to 2020. The organizing committee's official tally recorded 3 work-related deaths and 37 non-work-related deaths during stadium construction (CBS·UPI).
2024-11-05
Referendum Abolishing the Election — A constitutional amendment abolishing the 30-seat Shura Council election and restoring it to full appointment by the monarch, passed with 84% turnout (citizen voters) and 90.6% of valid votes in favor (Al-Monitor·Gulf News·IDEA).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Content of the Constitutional Amendment. The 2024 constitutional amendment, in addition to abolishing the Shura Council election, revised 14 articles, added 2, and deleted 3, and it allowed naturalized citizens to hold ministerial posts (Gulf News·IDEA). The election held once in 2021 remained the only parliamentary election.

Labor. Labor reforms after the World Cup and demands for an investigation into the causes of migrant worker deaths have led to a major focus of international human rights organizations. Amnesty stated that 'thousands of workers have returned in coffins without explanation over the past decade' (cited by CBS).

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 0–17 per year from 2017 to 2023. Records of collective action are scarce.
