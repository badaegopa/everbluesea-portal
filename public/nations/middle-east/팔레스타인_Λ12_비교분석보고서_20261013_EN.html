§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Governance Structure. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the Gaza Strip is moving toward a phased disarmament and transfer of governance centered on the U.S.-led 'Board of Peace', an International Stabilization Force (ISF), and the Palestinian Technical Civil Service Committee (NCAG), while the PA is moving toward resuming elections.

Fiscal. The PA, with Israeli clearance revenues withheld (approximately $5 billion), has moved to a structure dependent on funding from donors such as the EU, and it has implemented the value-added tax reform required by donors (2025) and the abolition of prisoner and bereaved family allowances (2026-02) (Series briefing).

Public Opinion. In the 97th survey of the Palestinian Center for Policy and Survey Research (PSR) (2026-08-05–08, 830 West Bank and 440 Gaza respondents, margin of error ±3.5%), 79% wanted President Abbas to resign; 83% perceived corruption in PA institutions, and 65% described the PA as a 'burden' rather than an 'achievement'. Trends in large-scale protests and strikes could not be confirmed within the scope of the Series review, and ACLED protest events declined from 1,696 in 2023 to 491 in 2024.
