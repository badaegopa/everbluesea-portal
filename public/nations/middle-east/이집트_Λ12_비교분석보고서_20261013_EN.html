Position within the Region — Why Egypt?

This report was selected for the North Africa rotation of the daily regional cycle pipeline as the country with the highest η according to the Country Analysis Series among those without a standalone edition. In the series, the η of the Arab Republic of Egypt (hereafter Egypt) is 0.5021(92nd among 197 countries), placing it above Tunisia (0.4886, 108th among 197 countries), Algeria (0.4867), and Morocco (0.4804). The difference with the region's lowest, Libya (0.4321, 179th among 197 countries), is 0.07.

However, the difference between 1st and 2nd in the region is merely 0.0135, and the entire region is clustered below the median of the 197 countries. Being 'the highest in the region' does not mean 'having low pressure.' According to the series, the dimensions within the 2024 threshold band are four—A2, A5, A7, and A8—among which the simultaneous inclusion of A7 and A8 matches the common configuration of the four KSDS v10.0 uprising observation countries (n=4, exploratory). This report places at the center of its observation this juncture where the η ranking and band configuration paint different pictures.

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1952-07
Free Officers' Revolution — The monarchy collapsed, and the military-origin presidential regime succession of Nasser, Sadat, and Mubarak began. This is the origin of the structure where the military stands at the center of state governance.
2011-01-25
January 25 Revolution — Following 18 days of mass protests, President Mubarak resigned on February 11. A prime example of the Arab Spring.
2013-07-03
Military Ouster of President Morsi — The Muslim Brotherhood-affiliated president elected in 2012 was ousted by the military. During the dispersal of Rabaa Square on August 14, at least 817 people were killed according to HRW counts (HRW, All According to Plan, 2014).
2014-06
Inauguration of President El-Sisi — Assumed the presidency after serving as Minister of Defense (Series status briefing).
2016-11
Transition to Floating Exchange Rate · IMF $12 Billion EFF — The pound dropped by more than half, and inflation reached 29.5% in 2017 (WDI, engine raw data inflation_cpi).
2019-04
Constitutional Amendment Referendum — Extended the presidential term from 4 to 6 years and allowed President El-Sisi to run for additional terms.
2019-09
Anti-Government Protests and Mass Arrests — Following corruption exposé videos by businessman Mohamed Ali, protests broke out in Cairo, Suez, and elsewhere, followed by the largest wave of arrests since El-Sisi took power (Amnesty International and HRW reports). Protests also occurred on the same date the following year.
2023-12
Presidential Election — Won a third term with an 89.6% vote share and 66.8% turnout announced by the National Election Authority (NEA) (engine raw data voter_turnout 2023=66.8).
2024-02~03

2025-10-13
Sharm El-Sheikh Summit — The United States, Egypt, Qatar, and Türkiye signed a document agreeing to end the Gaza War (Series timeline).
2025-11~2026-01
House of Representatives Election Annulment and Rerun — The NEA annulled 19 constituencies, and the Supreme Administrative Court annulled an additional 28 constituencies, resulting in reruns across 47 constituencies. The final voter turnout was 32.4% (International IDEA, Series timeline).
2026-03
Fuel Price Hike of 14–30% — Following the escalation of the US-Iran conflict, the government raised fuel prices, and the president stated that Suez Canal losses reached $10 billion (Series timeline).
2026-07-30
IMF Executive Board Approves Seventh Review — Completed the seventh EFF and second RSF reviews, approving the disbursement of approximately $1.77 billion. The program is set to conclude in December 2026 (Ahram Online).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Macroeconomy. In its July 2026 World Economic Outlook update, the IMF raised Egypt's 2026 growth forecast to 4.6% while projecting a −0.5% contraction for the Middle East and North Africa as a whole (Ahram Online 2026-07-08, state-run Al-Ahram affiliate, citing IMF). In approving the seventh review on July 30, the IMF Executive Board projected 5% growth for Q3 of FY2025/26 and 4.4% growth for FY2026/27, while noting that inflation could reach 16.7% in the second half of 2026. Resurgent regional tensions were cited as downside risks.

Inflation. On October 10, 2026, the Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics (CAPMAS) announced that the annual urban inflation rate for September was 13.9% (14.5% in August) and the nationwide inflation rate was 12.8%. Monthly urban consumer prices rose 1.3% due to a 15.1% increase in vegetable prices (Daily News Egypt 2026-10-10, citing CAPMAS).

Suez Canal. Suez Canal Authority (SCA) Chairman Osama Rabie stated on June 28, 2026, that dollar revenue for FY2025/26 increased by 23% to $4.67 billion (Ahram Online). According to Central Bank balance of payments statistics, toll revenue from July 2025 to March 2026 rose 22.1% to $3.2 billion. This remains far below the 2023 peak (approximately $9.4 billion to $10.2 billion, depending on the source).

Political and Mass Trends. In the 2025 House of Representatives election, 47 constituencies held reruns amid fraud allegations, with a final turnout of 32.4% (International IDEA). In February 2026, a cabinet reshuffle involving 22 positions took place (Series briefing). Within the scope confirmed by the series, there are no recent reports of large-scale collective action. According to ACLED tallies, protests decreased from 241 events in 2016 to 35 in 2021, and 47 in 2022. In an environment where public assemblies are strictly restricted by law, 'few records' does not equate to 'little discontent.'

§ 03-B

For the same Suez Canal, the president's 'losses' and the canal authority's 'revenue increases' emerge simultaneously, poverty rate publications have ceased, and official turnouts across two elections differ by a factor of two. White Dwarf (WD) = suppression and latency · Supernova (SN) = collapse followed by regeneration · Black Hole (BH) = implosion and irreversibility
The decisive variable dividing SN and BH is C_internal (intra-elite conflict energy). This is a metaphor and does not claim astrophysical correspondence.

Egypt does not have canonical E_A2 and C_internal values calculated. Recording only observations, with A2 institutional trust at 0.2688, the place where E_A2 sits is on the lower side, and public conflict among the military, the presidency, and intelligence agencies has not been recorded since 2013. This combination is closer to the WD (White Dwarf: suppression and latency) condition side. This is a direction of observation, not an assessment.

Egypt has already experienced 2011 (popular uprising) and 2013 (military intervention). While this experience lies outside the engine time series (2016–2024), it is a background that must be considered alongside interpreting the current low assembly records and high official vote shares.
