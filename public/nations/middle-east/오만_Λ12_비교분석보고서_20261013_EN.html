§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2020-01
Sultan's Succession — Sultan Haitham bin Tariq succeeded to the throne following the death of Sultan Qaboos (general fact). In the same year, central government debt reached its peak at 64.3% of GDP (Trading Economics).
2021-05-23~
Unemployment Protests — A small protest outside the Ministry of Manpower in Sohar spread to more than ten cities, demanding jobs and the eradication of corruption. Tear gas was used and some were arrested on May 26. The Sultan instructed the creation of 32,000 jobs for Omanis in 2021 (The New Arab · MEDC).
2024-07-15
Wadi Khabour Mosque Attack — Shia mosque attacks during Ashura. Initial police and Pakistani government statements reported 4–6 deaths; subsequent reports stated 9 dead (4 Pakistanis, 1 Indian, 1 policeman), including the 3 attackers, and 28 wounded. IS claimed responsibility (Al Jazeera·The National·SBS).
2024
Debt Reduction — IMF: Central government debt at 35.5% of GDP (37.5% in 2023); public enterprise debt approximately 31% (IMF PR 25/172).
2025-06-22
Introduction of Personal Income Tax Announced — Royal Decree 56/2025, 5% on annual net income exceeding 42,000 Omani rials from January 2028. The General Tax Authority stated that 99% of the population is not subject to taxation. First personal income tax in the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) (KPMG·Gulf News).
