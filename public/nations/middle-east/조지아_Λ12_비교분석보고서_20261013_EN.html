§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2024-05-28
Foreign Influence Transparency Act — Passed a law requiring organizations whose foreign subsidies exceed 20% of their funding to register as 'foreign influence proxy organizations.' The government described it as modeled on the US Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA), while bar associations and others countered that it differs from US law in its scope of application. The Constitutional Court heard the case after consolidation without suspending its effect (JAMnews · ecoi.net).
2024-10-26
General Election — A fully proportional system of 150 seats. Central Election Commission (confirmed 11-16): Georgia's Dream 53.93% · 89 seats; opposition coalition of four parties 37.79%. The opposition refused to recognize the results and declined to take its seats, alleging large-scale fraud. Initial OSCE/ODIHR statement: competitive and efficient, but polarization and fiscal disparities are severe. The December final report noted a lack of oversight of political finance and an imbalance in resources (IDEA · JAMnews).
2024-11-28~
Announcement of Suspension of EU Negotiations and Daily Protests — Following the government's announcement of the suspension of EU accession negotiations, daily protests occurred through 2025, accompanied by searches, clashes, mass arrests, and restrictions on assembly (Amnesty · Kavkaz-uzel).
2025-03
Opposition Plans to Sue Ruling Parties for Unconstitutionality — The ruling party's secretary-general announced that he would sue opposition parties including the United National Movement for unconstitutionality before the Constitutional Court and prevent them from participating in elections until a ruling is issued. The outcome was not confirmed in this investigation (Novaya Gazeta Europe).
2025
Assembly Restrictions and Arrests — Amnesty: more than 100 arrested after peaceful protests following the new assembly regulation bill. JAMnews: political prisoners exceeded 100 for the first time since independence in 2025 (Amnesty · JAMnews).
2025-10-04
Attempt to Enter the Presidential Palace on Election Day — In a local election boycotted by the opposition, the ruling party claimed victory. Some protesters attempted to enter the presidential palace; water cannons and tear gas were used in response. Reports differ on turnout, ranging from approximately 20,000 to approximately 100,000. Injuries: 21 security personnel and 6 protesters from the Ministry of Health; 14 police officers from the Ministry of Internal Affairs. 64 arrests (Wikipedia). The Prime Minister stated that 'no one can escape punishment,' while the former president claimed that the entry was staged (no independent evidence) (Al Jazeera · Meduza · intellinews).
