Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2014
New Constitution — Adoption of a consensual constitution, followed by two presidential and parliamentary elections (general facts).
2021-07-25
Suspension of Parliament — President Saied suspends parliament and begins rule by presidential decree (Series briefing). The five-year consecutive fall period of A8 begins around this time.
2022 · 2023
New Constitution and General Election — Adoption of a new constitution strengthening presidential powers through a 2022 referendum; the subsequent general election was held with very low turnout (general facts). Parliament reduced the jurisdiction of the Administrative Court over electoral disputes (Model Diplomat, opinion piece).
2024-10-06
presidential elections — ISIE announcement: Saied 90.69%, turnout 28.8% (48.98% in 2019). Despite an administrative court ruling to reinstate three candidates, ISIE excluded them; candidate Ayachi Jammal was sentenced to 12 years in prison for electoral offenses (IFES · ICJ · Wikipedia).
2025-03 · 11
Replacement of the Prime Minister and Major Trials — Appointment of Saida Ajouz Jerzani as prime minister in March. In November, the appeal trial of the 'state security conspiracy' case sentenced approximately 40 people, including Ahmed Nejib Chebbi (12 years) and Chahma Eissa (20 years); protests in Tunis drew 1,000–2,000 participants (Series briefing).
2025-12 · 2026-01
Regional and Labor Issues — In December, approximately 2,500 protested against pollution from a chemical plant in Gabès. The UGTT called a general strike for January 21 (scale of implementation unverifiable). On January 30, the state of emergency was extended to December 31 (Series briefing).
2026-06 · 07
Life Sentences and the Fifth Anniversary — In June, in the 'secret organization' case, Ennahda leader Rached Ghannouchi received a life sentence (the date of sentencing varies among sources: June 1–3). On July 25, on the fifth anniversary of the 2021 measures, thousands led by the opposition coalition 'Nafas' demanded the president's resignation and the release of political prisoners (Series briefing).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Direction of Governance. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government presents the 2026 budget as a 'strategic instrument' for addressing regional disparities and strengthening economic diplomacy, emphasizing macroeconomic stability and credit rating improvements. In the judicial sphere, criminal prosecutions of opposition leaders, journalists, and activists continue.

Employment. According to the National Institute of Statistics (INS), the unemployment rate in the fourth quarter of 2025 was 15.2% (15.4% in the third quarter). The same data show that the youth unemployment rate for those aged 15–24 was 38.4%, and the unemployment rate among higher education graduates was 22.5% (30.5% for women), while the labor force participation rate fell by 0.2%p to 45.9% — part of the decline in unemployment stemmed from a reduction in the number of jobseekers (reports by Kapitalis and Financial Afrik).

Public Trends. In November–December 2025, protests over freedom of expression and political prisoners as well as environmental protests in Gabès were repeated, and in July 2026 a rally demanding departure drew thousands of participants (Series briefing). The Series notes the president's July 2026 hospitalization as unconfirmed by official announcements (Wikipedia).
