Zinsou Case — In March, writer Boualem Sansal was sentenced to five years in prison on charges of 'undermining national unity.' On November 12, he was pardoned on humanitarian grounds at the request of the German president. At the same time, Mohamed Tadjedit, a Hirak figure, was sentenced to five years in prison on charges including 'support for terrorism' (Al Jazeera · The New Arab).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Record of Elections. The 2024 presidential election is recorded as a case in which the vote share of the same election differed by more than 10%p across official institutions, and even the winning camp challenged the electoral commission's figures. The confirmed turnout of 46.1% is higher than that of 2019 (over 60% abstention).

Expression and the Judiciary. The pardon of Salah was presented as a humanitarian measure responding to external requests, while long sentences were handed down to Hirak activists at the same time. Recent prisoner counts by the CNLD (2025–2026) could not be found in this survey.

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 340 in 2022 and 142 in 2024, less than one-tenth of the levels during the Hirak period. Conflict fatalities numbered 35 in 2024.
