§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events that shaped the structure

1986
People Power — Marcos senior's dictatorship fell to a civilian revolution (general fact). 36 years later, his son became president.
2016–2022
Drug War — Duterte's government's 'War on Drugs'. Police reported about 6,200 deaths from 2016 to 2022, while human rights groups estimate up to 30,000 (HRW). ACLED reported 4,358 conflict deaths in 2017.
2022-06
Marcos-Duterte Alliance — Marcos junior and Sara Duterte won as running mates. Their relationship deteriorated afterward (Series Briefing).
2025-03-11
The Hague Transfer — Former President Rodrigo Duterte was arrested at Manila airport on an ICC warrant (genocide) and sent to The Hague (AP). Pre-trial hearings were held from February 23 to 27, 2026 (HRW).
2025-11
Flood Prevention Corruption Rally — Over 500,000 people gathered in Manila for three days in 2022, led by Iglesia Ni Cristo (INC), which supported Marcos. The president announced the resignation of two ministers suspected of corruption and froze assets worth about $3 billion (Al Jazeera).
2026-05 · 07
Vice President Impeachment — May 11, House of Representatives second impeachment passed (257 in favor, 25 against, 9 abstentions), May 18, Senate impeachment court convened shortly after the Senate president's change, July 6, trial began (6,000 police deployed, about 400 protesters gathered outside). On July 28, the vice president stated that the president could not escape responsibility for flood prevention corruption (Series briefing).
2026-09-23
Quorum change — The Senate impeachment court lowered the quorum for a guilty verdict. Some senators missed the trial due to theft charges, ICC arrest warrants, and medical leave (The Star). The final verdict could not be confirmed.
§ 03

Recent situation (2025–2026)

ICC trial. The indictment includes 19 killings and attempted killings during Duterte's term as mayor of Davao City (2013-2016), 14 high-ranking officials during his presidency (2016-2017), and 45 killings and attempted killings during the barangay anti-drug operations (2016-2018) (HRW, Arab News). Duterte refused to attend the trial. In April 2026, the indictment was reported by a single source (SunStar), which could not be confirmed by ICC records.

Flood prevention corruption. According to government data, the flood prevention budget over the past 15 years was $2.6 billion, and according to witness statements, about 25-30% of this was embezzled as kickbacks (Series briefing).

Public sentiment. During the impeachment trial, rallies supporting Duterte and those seeking accountability were held outside the Senate. According to ACLED, there were 406 protests in 2023 and 344 in 2024, with 652 deaths in 2024 (341 civilians).

§ 09

Collapse path classification — WD / SN / BH

Path classification criteria (UI standard version, stellar evolution metaphor)
WD: E_A2 < 0.3, C_internal < 0.4 · SN: E_A2 0.3~0.5, C_internal > 0.5 · BH: E_A2 < 0.2, C_internal > 0.7
White Dwarf (WD) = suppression and latency · Supernova (SN) = collapse and regeneration · Black Hole (BH) = implosion and irreversibility
The decision variable separating SN and BH is C_internal (elite internal conflict energy). This is a metaphor and does not claim a physical astronomical correspondence.

E_A2 (institutional trust effect) is low when A2 is within the band, and C_internal (elite internal conflict) is high during the Marcos-Duterte split and impeachment. However, the conflict is proceeding within institutional procedures such as impeachment, ICC, and elections.

The SN (collapse and regeneration) condition variables are observed, but the regime change on the streets as in 1986 is not happening. The focus is on the impeachment verdict and the 2028 presidential election scenario. This is an observation list, not a judgment.
