Research Purpose

This report records the reality of the 18 million Cambodian people from an observer standpoint.

In the Killing Fields (1975–79), a quarter of the population was massacred. It was Vietnam that drove out the Khmer Rouge who led the massacre, and it was Hun Sen who seized power with Vietnam's help. Thirty-eight years from 1985 to 2023. And he passed that power down to his son.

Λ¹² asks — how does a power that started from trauma use trauma as a governance resource? How is that structure connected to the cyber scam industry today?

§0 · Glossary
Killing Fields (Killing Fields)
Cambodian genocide by the Khmer Rouge from 1975 to 1979. At least 1.5 to 2 million deaths. Intensive damage to intellectuals, urbanites, and ethnic minorities. Core component of TBI.
CPP (Cambodian People's Party)
De facto one-party rule system since 1985. Complete transition to a one-party state after the dissolution of the last opposition party in 2017.
Dynastic Succession
Dynastic succession of the Prime Ministership from Hun Sen to Hun Manet in 2023. Ministry of Interior and Ministry of National Defense also handed down from father to son. Hereditary power in the guise of democracy.
Cyber Scam Industry
Estimated at 40–60% of GDP. Forced labor of 100,000 to 200,000 foreigners. Suspected protection by the highest echelon of the state. UN estimated at several billion dollars annually.
Σ_rep (Suppressive Discontinuity)
CPP oppresses opposition forces through arrest, deportation, and intimidation. Dissolution of the Candlelight Party, closure of independent media, assassination of opposition politicians.
Cambodia–Thailand Border Dispute
Armed conflict on 2025.07.24 — 38 dead, 200 injured, 300,000 refugees. Collapse of the ceasefire on 12.07. External pressure of TBI added.
Ⅰ · Observer Standpoint

Why Is a Country with 7% GDP Growth at the Highest Risk in Southeast Asia?

Cambodia appears to be a Southeast Asian economic success story, growing at an annual average of 7% over the past 20 years. However, Λ-Score 0.462 — highest risk among 6 Southeast Asian countries. This paradox is the essence of Cambodia.

The fruits of growth did not reach the people. Informal employment at 88%, minimum wage at USD 208/month (2025), lowest GDP per capita. There was growth, but no distribution. The CPP elite and the Hun Sen family have seized control of the structure of growth.

ⅩⅡ · Conclusion Orbit

Cambodia is one of the countries practicing the most sophisticated trauma governance in the world. "If it weren't for us, the Khmer Rouge would have returned" — this narrative has sustained 38 years, and has now been passed down to the son.

What TBI 0.820 tells us is that the memory of the Killing Fields remains as a tool of power without being assimilated. There has been no truth-seeking, and some of the perpetrators still run local governments. This is not the healing of trauma, but the freezing of trauma.

However, the generation that remembers the Killing Fields is gradually decreasing. The youth generation did not grow up looking at that fear, but at the USD 208 minimum wage and the cyber scam industry. The reasons for them to believe the CPP's narrative are wearing increasingly thin.

Λ-Score 0.462 points to the highest risk in Southeast Asia. When and in which direction that figure will move — it will be decided by the youth of Cambodia, not by Hun Manet.
