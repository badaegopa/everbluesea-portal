Historical time series — Events that shaped the structure

1962

1988
8888 Democracy Uprising — Nationwide protests and military's bloody suppression, new military regime (SLORC) took power (general fact).
1990
Invalidation of election results — The National League for Democracy (NLD) led by Aung San Suu Kyi won overwhelmingly, but the military refused to transfer power (general fact).
2007
Saffron Revolution — Monks-led protests against fuel price hikes, suppressed (general fact).
2008
New Constitution — Reserved 25% of parliamentary seats for military nominees, and stipulated that defense, interior, and border ministers be appointed by the military commander-in-chief (general fact).
2011
Quasi-civilian government — The Thein Sein government after the 2010 election, opening and reform (general fact).
2015
NLD overwhelming victory — The NLD government took office in 2016. The military maintained its constitutional share (a general fact).

2021.2
Coup — After the military seized the elected government leadership and established the State Administrative Council, citing allegations of election fraud in 2020, civil disobedience movements emerged. ACLED recorded 7,780 protests.
2022–24
Widening civil war — Clashes between resistance forces and ethnic armed organizations and the military. ACLED recorded 11,792 political violence incidents in 2022 and 13,112 in 2024.

2025.4

2025.12–26.1
Three-stage general election — Held in three stages (December 28, January 11, January 25) due to conflict, with major opposition parties like NLD boycotting. In the first stage, 102 townships out of 330 participated, with USDP winning 88 out of 96 seats in the lower house (Total Series Briefing).
2026.3–4
Selection of President Min Aung Hlaing — On March 30, he transferred his position as commander-in-chief to Mya Tun Oo and was nominated as a presidential candidate, and on April 3, he was elected with 429 out of 584 votes in parliament. § 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Politics.The military administration held general elections in three stages on December 28, 2025, January 11, and January 25, 2026. Due to conflicts, nationwide simultaneous voting was not possible. Major opposition parties like NLD boycotted, and opposition groups and independent observers criticized the elections as neither free nor fair (Total Series Briefing). The military holds a quarter of parliamentary seats by constitutional appointment. On April 3, 2026, parliament elected Min Aung Hlaing as president, with his close aide Mya Tun Oo taking over as commander-in-chief due to the constitution preventing the president from also serving as commander-in-chief. The Total Series Briefing recorded this as a 'transfer of power' structure where the military's top leader moved to the civilian presidency.

Conflict.Following the offensive by the United Nationalities Federal Council (Operation 1027) in October 2023, the military lost significant territory in northern border areas. In 2025, a Chinese-mediated ceasefire returned some cities, including Lashio, to military administration. The effective control area of the military varies widely, and this report does not adopt specific figures. Post-coup collective actions have shifted from open protests in cities to armed conflicts (ACLED protests 7,780 in 2021 → 1,207 in 2024, political violence 7,672 in 2021 → 13,112 in 2024), and the UN warned that reduced international aid is worsening the situation for conflict-affected civilians.

Economy and Disaster.The World Bank Myanmar Economic Monitor (June 2025) estimated direct asset losses from the March 2025 earthquake at $11 billion (14% of GDP) and projected a 2.5% decline in GDP for the 2025/26 fiscal year. The same report estimated the poverty rate in 2024 at 31.0%, higher than the last official estimate of 24.8% in 2017. The June 2026 report noted some recovery but also that business activity was far below pre-2021 and pre-earthquake levels.

Connection with the Engine.This phase directly connects to two axes of Λ¹²'s attractor. The coup is an event where A8 (executive) attacks A2 (electoral system) (Axiom A3 scene), and in the Total Series trajectory, A2 and A8 fell together in 2021. However, the final observed BSLI is from 2017, so the increase in the poverty rate in 2024 has not yet entered the engine.
