§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events that shaped the structure

2002
Independence — Independence in 2002 following a referendum in 1999 (general fact).
2006
Parliamentary Lifetime Pension Act — Law establishing lifetime pensions for former members of parliament and high-ranking officials (CIVICUS·IDN Financials).
2023
Oil Fund Dependency — Government budget heavily reliant on oil fund withdrawals, with 88% of GDP reported in 2023. Fund balance 18.2 billion USD (10.6 times GDP) or 18.95 billion USD (varies by source). Withdrawals since 2007 are at about three times sustainable levels (GlobalEdge·East Asia Forum).
2025-06
Bayu-Undan Production End — The only operational gas field ceased production. Hydrocarbons accounted for about 95% of exports (Coface).
2025-09-15~26
Student Protests and Privilege Revocation — Student-led protests against a parliamentary vehicle purchase budget of approximately 4.2 million USD, police tear gas, and activist arrests. On September 16, the parliamentary vehicle purchase was canceled, and on September 26, 62 out of 65 members unanimously voted to abolish the lifetime pension clause (awaiting presidential signature). IDEA recorded it as the largest organized protest since independence with over 2,000 participants (CIVICUS·Tatoli·IDEA).
2025-10-26
ASEAN Membership — Joined as the eleventh member at the 47th Kuala Lumpur Summit. First expansion since 1999 (Vietnam News·Eastern Mirror).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Fiscal cliff.Projections for the depletion of the oil fund vary by source: 2034 (official estimate cited), 2036 (GlobalEdge), and the late 2030s (Coface) (East Asia Forum·StratSea).

Protests and reactions.In September 2025, protests followed two parliamentary decisions (cancellation of vehicle purchases and abolition of lifetime pensions) within about ten days. Activists were also arrested during the protests (CIVICUS).

Public sentiment.According to ACLED, there were 5 to 12 protests per year from 2020 to 2024 (excluding the September 2025 protests). Conflict fatalities were 0 to 2 per year.
