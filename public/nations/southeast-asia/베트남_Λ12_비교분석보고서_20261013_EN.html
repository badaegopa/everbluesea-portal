§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Direction of Governance. According to the National Analysis Compendium Briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government aims for 10% annual growth, emphasizing private sector development and international economic integration. Additionally, the Rumm regime has led administrative reforms such as gender consolidation and downsizing of bureaucratic organizations.

Growth Rate. The General Statistics Office (GSO) announced a 2025 growth rate of 8.02% (Q4: 8.46%) — second highest after 2022 among 2011–2025. Prior to the announcement, the World Bank (2025-09) projected 6.6%, and the IMF (2025-10) projected 6.5% (VnExpress·Trading Economics·The Star).

Public Trends. Within the scope of compendium verification, there are no reports of large-scale collective action. German dpa reported that critical voices were suppressed and activists arrested repeatedly, and human rights group '88 Project' tallied 56 political arrests in 2025 — a three-year consecutive increase, double that of 2022 (AP report).
