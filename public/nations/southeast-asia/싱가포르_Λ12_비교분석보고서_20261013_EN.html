§ 03

Recent political situation (2025–2026)

Politics.The May 3, 2025 general election was the first after Lawrence Wong became Prime Minister. According to the Election Department (ELD) results and reports, PAP received approximately 65.6% of the vote, a 4% increase from 2020 (61.2%). PAP secured 87 out of 97 seats (including 5 uncontested GRC seats), while WP maintained its 10 seats. Voter turnout was around 92%. Although opposition seats have gradually increased since 2011, the election results indicate no realistic possibility of a change in government. The results also show that the opposition support base remains stable.

Economy.The Ministry of Trade and Industry (MTI) reported a growth rate of 5.0% for 2025. In November 2025, it forecasted a 1.0% to 3.0% growth for 2026 due to a slowdown in global demand following US tariff measures. In February 2026, it revised the forecast to 2.0% to 4.0%, and in May, it maintained the range citing increased downside risks due to Middle East conflicts. On August 11, it revised the forecast to 4.5% to 5.5% based on a 6.1% growth rate in the first half of 2026 and accelerated global AI-related capital investment. The report also noted a decline in food and beverage services due to an increase in residents' overseas travel and a decrease in visitors.

Connection with the engine.This phase connects with the external connection axis of Λ¹². However, the actual input for Series A4 (Economic Stability) is GDP and manufacturing, and the tariff and AI demand changes in 2025-2026 have not yet been reflected in the Series value (based on 2024). The actual input for Series A10, the number of tourists, according to the Series data note, is the final actual measurement for 2020 (+4 years carry-over), so the decrease in visitors in 2026 is not reflected in this indicator.
