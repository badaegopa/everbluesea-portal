§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Two Explanations for the Deaths. AP-affiliated reports attributed the deaths to clashes between security forces and protesters, while reports citing the Ministry of Interior stated that two detainees died in custody and one subsequently died in the hospital. The Ministry of Interior did not disclose further details (Morocco World News).

Migration-Related Expulsions. Reports citing Ministry of Interior data listed the number of expulsions in 2024 at 10,753 (a 14% increase year-on-year) (Global Voices). Human rights groups criticized this by linking it to cooperation with the EU.

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 125 in 2019, 323 in 2020, 276 in 2021, 217 in 2022, 233 in 2023, and 269 in 2024.
