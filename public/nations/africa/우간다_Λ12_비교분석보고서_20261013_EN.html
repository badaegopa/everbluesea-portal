§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Discrepancies in Tallying Stages. The initial provisional announcement based on a small number of polling stations was 61.7% (Monitor), other early reports indicated 76% (Al Jazeera), and the final result was 71.65%. The final vote count also varied slightly across reports, between 7,946,772 votes and 7,944,772 votes.

Observer Missions and Litigation. Observers expressed concern over instances of intimidation (aggregate reports). Museveni's side countered in court that the petition had 'incurable defects' (New Vision). The ruling outcome was not confirmed in this investigation.

Public Trends. ACLED protests peaked in 2021 at 469 incidents and stood at 143 incidents in 2024. Civilian fatalities were 297 in 2021, 290 in 2023, and 212 in 2024.
