Executive Summary

Mauritius has a Series η of 0.4845 (116th out of 197 countries). The cross-sectional maximum pressure is A5 · P=0.601 (measured: R&D/GDP), which is the only one within the band. The Series maintains this country as a data-edition entry without a narrative briefing — the political situation was drawn from open sources in this survey.

§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Fiscal affairs. Prime Minister Ramgoolam (concurrently serving as Finance Minister) criticized the previous government for misreporting GDP, deficits, and debt over several years. Rating agency CareEdge explained that a significant portion of the debt revision stemmed from methodological changes (no longer netting out government debt held by certain public enterprises) and estimated the FY25 deficit at 9.8% and public debt at 90%. Public sector debt at the end of June 2026 stood at 683.4 billion rupees (AllAfrica French edition).

Dispute over accountability. The former Finance Minister and former Central Bank Governor face fraud charges over separate embezzlement allegations at a state-owned enterprise; they deny the allegations and are out on bail. The intention behind misrepresenting statistics was not confirmed in the sources within the scope of this survey (Africa Confidential·CareEdge).

Public trends. ACLED protests numbered 47–119 annually from 2020 to 2024, with 2–3 fatalities per year.
