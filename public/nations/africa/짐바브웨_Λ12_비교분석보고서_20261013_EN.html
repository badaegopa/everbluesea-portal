2026-06-18
Term Extension Constitutional Amendment Bill — The Minister of Justice introduces a constitutional amendment bill postponing the 2028 election by two years to extend the presidential term to 2030, increasing presidential and parliamentary terms from 5 to 7 years, and shifting presidential election from direct popular vote to parliamentary election. Passed in the National Assembly 216 to 42, approved in the Senate 75 to 4 (Al Jazeera, AP, eNCA). Critics called it a 'constitutional coup' and reported arrests and assaults of activists.
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Institutional extension. Reports confirmed that the constitutional amendment bill still faces steps of re-vote in the National Assembly and presidential assent (eNCA). Whether it was signed and debates over the necessity of a referendum were not confirmed in this investigation.

Inflation and poverty. A World Bank economist stated that the extreme poverty rate based on the international poverty line ($2.15 per day) peaked at 49% in 2020 and fell to 42% in 2023, with projections of 47.6% in 2024 → 45.8% in 2025 also presented (cited by NewsDay). The World Food Programme (WFP) uses 42%. As years and revisions vary by source, it is recorded only as a range.

Public trends. ACLED protests decreased from 107 events in 2018 to 30 in 2024, and political violence decreased from 173 events in 2023 to 53 in 2024. Although there are reports of activist arrests during the 2026 constitutional amendment process, reports of large-scale protests were not gathered within the confirmed scope.
