§ 02

Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

2017~
Armed Conflict in Anglophone Regions — Following escalation in 2017, clashes erupted between separatist armed groups and security forces. In May 2019, ICG estimated 1,850 deaths and 530,000 internally displaced persons (IDPs), while government estimates in the same year reported 1,600 deaths. Subsequent ICG estimates were reported as over 6,500 deaths and over 1 million displaced (Business in Cameroon, Al Jazeera, etc.).
2025-10-12
Presidential Election — Immediately following voting, candidate Issa Tchiroma Bakary declared victory. Proclaiming results before the official announcement was prohibited (GlobalSecurity, etc.).
2025-10-27
Constitutional Council Announcement — President Biya 53.66%, Tchiroma 35.19%. Tchiroma rejected the results, claiming 54.8% for himself and 31.3% for Biya based on his own tally. Voter turnout reports diverged between 46.3% and 57.7% (AllAfrica, Prime Business Africa).
2025-10~11
Post-Election Protests — Regional governor announced 4 deaths (October 26, prior to result announcement), civil society group Stand Up for Cameroon reported at least 23, and UN sources (cited by Reuters) reported 48. The government did not release an aggregate death toll (The Citizen, bluewin).
