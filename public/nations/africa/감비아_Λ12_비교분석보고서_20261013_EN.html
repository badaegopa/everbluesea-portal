Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1994–2016
Jammeh's Rule — Yahya Jammeh ruled for 22 years following a 1994 coup (general fact).
2016-12 · 2017-01

2018–2021
TRRC — The Truth, Reconciliation and Reparations Commission recommended the prosecution of Jammeh and 68 former officials (ICJ · Just Security).
2020 · 2025-07-07
Rejection of Draft Constitution — The new draft constitution was repeatedly rejected in parliament, maintaining the 1997 constitution with no term limits (Africa Center · Wikipedia).
2024-12-15
Special Tribunal Statute — ECOWAS heads of state adopted the statute for a 'Special Tribunal for The Gambia' to try Jammeh-era crimes (based in The Gambia, one Gambian judge on a three-judge panel). In March 2025, reports noted a 'special tribunal in search of funding' (ICJ · AllAfrica).
2025-08 · 12
Migrant Vessel Maritime Disasters — On August 27, at least 70 died off the coast of Mauritania (government confirmed). December 31 capsizing off Jinack: Ministry of Defence initially reported 7 → government official reported 39 on January 8 (Reuters). The government intercepted over 2,700 departures in 2025 (The Point · Reuters).
2026-12-05
Upcoming Presidential Election — President Barrow seeks a third term (National People's Party), while the largest opposition party UDP nominated Ousainou Darboe. National Assembly elections scheduled for April 10, 2027 (Africa Center · Dakaractu).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Elections and Constitution. The 1997 Constitution contains no presidential term limits, permitting a third-term bid. In a survey by Gambia Participates and CepRass, 55% of respondents opposed a third-term candidacy, while 35% supported it (Africa Press).

Transitional Justice. While the statute for the Special Tribunal was adopted, funding issues were reported, and whether a special prosecutor was appointed or indictments filed could not be confirmed in this survey. The EU Country Report cited the sale of Jammeh's forfeited assets as a prominent controversy during the reporting period (EU capacity4dev).

Migration. NGO Caminando Fronteras tallied over 10,000 deaths along the entire West African Atlantic route in 2024, while EU inflows dropped 60% from January to November 2025 (Frontex). Popular collective action recorded in ACLED stands at 2 to 19 incidents per year.
