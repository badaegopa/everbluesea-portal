§ 03

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Third term. In the 2025 presidential election, held after the 2023 constitution abolished term limits, the incumbent was elected in the first round. Turnout was higher than in 2020 (35.3% → 52.4%). The second round of legislative elections was scheduled for 2026 (IDEA).

Security. Armed groups remain active across substantial portions of the national territory, and the government has relied on support from foreign military partners and the UN peacekeeping mission (MINUSCA) (general fact). ACLED recorded 245 events of violence against civilians in 2024.

Public Trends. ACLED recorded between 22 and 63 protests annually from 2017 to 2024.
