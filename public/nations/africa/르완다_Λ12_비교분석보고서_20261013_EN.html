§ 03

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Poverty Statistics. The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR)'s 7th Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey (EICV7, 2023-10 to 2024-10, 15,066 households) announced that the poverty rate dropped from 39.8% in 2017 to 27.4% in 2024, and the extreme poverty rate dropped from 11.3% to 5.4%. NISR stated that because of methodological changes the two surveys could not be directly compared, so comparisons were conducted via modeling (NISR).

External Affairs. The government has framed its actions in eastern Congo as defense against threats from the FDLR (a Hutu armed militia linked to the 1994 genocide) and has not officially acknowledged its own troops operating inside Congo. A government spokesperson stated that the 2025 UN report 'misrepresents Rwanda's longstanding security concerns' (Africanews).

Public Trends. ACLED protests were 1–4 incidents per year between 2017 and 2024, and political violence also decreased from 50 incidents in 2019 to 14 in 2024. Within the verified scope, there are no reports of large-scale collective action.
