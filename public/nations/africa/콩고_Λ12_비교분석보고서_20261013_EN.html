§ 02

Historical Timeline — Events That Shaped the Structure

1997
Civil War and the Return of Sassou Nguesso — Denis Sassou Nguesso returns to power at the end of the civil war (general fact).
2015-10
Constitutional Referendum — A new constitution changing presidential age and term limits (general fact).
2016-03
Presidential Election — Sassou Nguesso elected. Official turnout 67.57%. Opposition candidates Jean-Marie Michel Mokoko and André Okombi Salissa were subsequently imprisoned and have remained detained for nearly 10 years (AP).
2017
Debt Recalculation — In March, the IMF recorded public debt at 77% of GDP. In October, an IMF mission announced public and publicly guaranteed debt at 5.329 trillion CFA francs (approximately $9.14 billion) as of end-July, which news reports stated as approximately 110% of GDP. This figure excludes disputed claims in litigation and domestic arrears since 2014 (IMF PR 17/79, 17/387; Financial Afrik).
2026-03
Presidential Election — Sassou Nguesso reelected with provisional 94.8%, finalized 94.90%. Turnout announced by state TV at 84.65%. Second place 1.48%. Multiple opposition parties boycotted, activists arrested, nationwide internet shutdown on election day. Constitutional Court confirmed results on March 28 (AP, TimesLive, INAFRAN).
§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Long-term Rule. Sassou Nguesso has ruled from 1979 to 1992 and since 1997, renewing his mandate in the 2026 presidential election (general fact, AP).

Debt and Oil. In an economy dependent on oil exports, public debt was recalculated in 2017 following the 2014 oil price drop, followed by IMF programs and debt restructuring (IMF, Financial Afrik).

Public Dynamics. ACLED recorded events are small-scale, with 28 fatalities in 2018 and 19 in 2022.
