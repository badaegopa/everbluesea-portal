Executive Summary

Cabo Verde has a Series η of 0.4913 (104th out of 197 countries). The maximum cross-sectional pressure is A5 · P=0.620 (measured: R&D/GDP), and up to the 2nd-ranked A7 (0.4966, measured: aging/population growth) falls within the cross-sectional band. 3rd-ranked A4 Economic Stability (0.4270) is below the threshold, and the absorber dimensions A8 (0.2532) and A2 (0.2147) rank low at 7th and 8th.

ACLED protests range from 47 to 56 cases per year, and political violence ranges from 1 to 4 cases per year.

On the political situation axis, the 2021 presidential election, high public debt and IMF program, and the May 2026 general election and regime change are recorded. The Series treats this country as a data-edition entry without narrative briefings — the political situation was drawn from open sources in this survey.
