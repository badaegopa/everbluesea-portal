Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Three figures of fatalities. The government did not release casualty figures, with the foreign minister stating 'there are no figures' and dismissing opposition counts as 'grossly exaggerated.' The UN cited credible reports that at least 10 people were killed in three cities. Chadema claimed 'over 800 people' based on hospital monitor reports.
