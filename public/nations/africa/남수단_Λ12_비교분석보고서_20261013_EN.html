§ 02

Historical Timeline — Events that Shaped the Structure

2011~2018
Independence and Civil War — Independence in 2011, civil war from 2013 to 2018, and the 2018 Revitalised Peace Agreement (general fact). ACLED 2017 conflict fatalities: 4,859.
2024-03
Pipeline Disruptions — Sudan's Petroleum Minister declared force majeure on pipeline deliveries toward Port Sudan. Daily production decreased from an average of 150,000 barrels in 2023 to 90,000 barrels in March 2024. Finance Minister: Oil accounts for over 90% of fiscal revenue (Reuters citation report · BNN Bloomberg).
2024-07
Civil Servant Wage Arrears — Ministry of Petroleum official: Due to a sharp drop in revenue, civil servant salaries could not be paid for the past 9 months. Plans announced in October to resume exports via Sudan (PGJ · Business Recorder).
2024-09-13
Election Postponement — The Office of the President extended the transitional period by 2 years and postponed the December 2024 election to December 22, 2026. The transitional period lasts until February 2027 (News24 · Monitor).
2025-03
Nasir Attack and Machar's House Arrest — Following an attack on the Nasir military base (commander and multiple soldiers killed), First Vice President Riek Machar was placed under house arrest (PBS · Eastleigh Voice).
2025-09-22
Commencement of Treason Trial — Machar and seven others face trial on charges of treason, crimes against humanity, mass murder, terrorism, and destruction of property. A special court dismissed all jurisdictional, unconstitutional, and immunity objections. Machar claims it is a 'political witch hunt' (Namibian · Radio Tamazuj).
2026
Election Preparations — Civil society organization CEPO: The National Elections Commission received only 4% of the requested budget, raising the risk of the December election being postponed for a fifth time. An official postponement announcement has not been confirmed (Radio Tamazuj · Radio Yei).
§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Finance and Oil. Most fiscal revenues depend on pipelines running through Sudan, and disruptions in 2024 led to persistent salary arrears. The 2026 export status could not be verified in this investigation (BNN Bloomberg · PGJ).

Political Schedule. The election on December 22, 2026, remains on the official schedule, while civil society groups have warned of the risk of further postponement (Radio Tamazuj). This report does not predict whether the election will take place.

Public Trends. ACLED recorded 20–67 protests per year. Political violence accounted for 1,750 events in 2021 and 1,235 in 2024, with more than half targeting civilians (707 events in 2024).
