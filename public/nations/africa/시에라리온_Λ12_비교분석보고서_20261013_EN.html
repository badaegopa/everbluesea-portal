Historical Time Series — Events That Shaped the Structure

1991~2002
Civil War — Revolutionary United Front (RUF) civil war and its end in 2002 (general fact). Prior to the trajectory years.
2023-06-24~27
Presidential Election — Electoral Commission: Julius Maada Bio (SLPP) 56.17% (1,566,932 votes), Samura Kamara (APC) 41.16%, voter turnout 83%. Civil society coalition National Election Watch (NEW): self-calculated turnout 75.4–79%, Bio vote share 47.7–53.1%. Carter Center: Some flaws existed, but voting proceeded credibly. APC alleged fraud and demanded a rerun (AP, VOA, Africanews).
2023-11-26
Freetown Armed Attack — Attacks on the Wilberforce Barracks armory near the presidential lodge, the central Pademba Road Prison, etc., escape of approximately 1,890–2,200 inmates, nationwide curfew. Fatalities: AFP 21, Wikipedia 14 military, 1 police, 1 prison officer, 3 attackers; other reports 18 security personnel. Government categorized it as an attempted coup (Wikipedia, JURIST).
2024-01
Treason Indictment — Former President Ernest Bai Koroma indicted on treason charges, personally denying involvement (later granted permission to travel abroad for medical treatment). Subsequently, 11 individuals were convicted, with the 'ringleader,' a former bodyguard of Koroma, sentenced to 182 years in prison (JURIST, Irish News).
2024-04
Kush State of Emergency — President Bio declared a state of emergency over the spread of the synthetic drug 'kush' and formed a national response task force (date April 4 or 7 depending on reports) (Wikipedia '2024 in Sierra Leone').

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Post-election. Following the 2023 presidential election results, the opposition APC demanded a rerun, and President Bio began his second term (The EastAfrican, AP).

Judicial proceedings. Eleven individuals connected to the November 2023 attack were found guilty, and the former president continues to deny charges (JURIST, Irish News).

Public trends. ACLED protests peaked in 2019 at 43 events and stood at 4 events in 2024. Political violence recorded 68–69 events per year in 2018–2019 and 43 events in 2023.
