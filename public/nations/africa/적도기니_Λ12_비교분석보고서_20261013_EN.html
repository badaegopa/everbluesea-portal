§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

After Oil. Completing the third review under the surveillance program in February 2026, the IMF projected that the economy would experience modest contraction over the medium term due to declining hydrocarbon production, with public debt estimated to rise from 36.4% of GDP in 2024 to 39.2% by the end of 2025 (IMF PR 26/035). GDP per capita in 2024 was $6,745 (PPP $17,567) according to World Bank standards (cited by Trading Economics).

Silence of the Island. International digital rights organizations have demanded the lifting of the internet shutdown on Annobón Island, but whether it persists as of 2026 could not be confirmed in this investigation.

Public Trends. Protests recorded by ACLED numbered 0 to 2 per year between 2017 and 2024. Compendium data cautions (A2·A8 comparison group extremes) coexist with an absence of collective action records — that 'no events' does not mean 'no pressure' is kept as a Layer 1 record.
