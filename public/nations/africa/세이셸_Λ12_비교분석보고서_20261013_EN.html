Executive Summary

Seychelles ranks 1st in East Africa with a Compendium η of 0.5292, and its cross-sectional maximum pressure is A5 · P=0.607 (measured: R&D/GDP). § 03

Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Post-Transition. President Herminie presented anti-corruption measures—such as strengthening the authority of independent oversight bodies, monitoring public expenditures, and preventing conflicts of interest—alongside welfare expansions (13th-month salary, long-service allowance, living wage, lowered retirement age) in his first address to the National Assembly. He ordered a review of the Assumption Island agreement signed with India, citing sovereignty protection, infrastructure project transparency, and environmental conservation. As part of the policy summary relies on non-mainstream media (seychelles.bg) reports, details must be read with caution.

Tourism. According to National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) figures, visitor arrivals from January to November 2025 reached 361,422, up 12.4% from the same period of the previous year (321,687), and fourth-quarter arrivals reached 115,551, up 17.6% (Seychelles Nation). This nears recovery to the 2019 peak of 384,204. Germany, France, and Russia constitute the main markets.

Heroin. In the Afrobarometer survey (published 2025-10-17), respondents identifying drug control as the government's top priority reached 36%, an 11%p increase from 25% in 2022, ranking second after the cost of living. Positive evaluations of the government's drug response fell by 5%p from 2022 to 50%. Trust in the drug response agency (DSAPTR) stood at 35%, and the police at 24%. 2025 reports indicate that the price per dose of heroin plummeted by 80%, from $35 to $7 (secondary report).

Public Sentiment. According to ACLED data, protests in Seychelles range between 2 to 8 per year, with 0 recorded incidents of political violence or fatalities (2020–2023, vault acled_link). Pressure found expression through elections rather than the streets.

Path Classification Criteria (UI Canonical, Stellar Evolution Metaphor)
WD: E_A2 < 0.3, C_internal < 0.4 · SN: E_A2 0.3~0.5, C_internal > 0.5 · BH: E_A2 < 0.2, C_internal > 0.7
White Dwarf (WD) = suppression and latency · Supernova (SN) = collapse and regeneration · Black Hole (BH) = implosion and irreversibility
The determining variable separating SN and BH is C_internal (elite internal conflict energy). This is a metaphor and does not claim astrophysical correspondence.

E_A2 (institutional trust effect) falls on the higher condition as A2 and A8 are both high, while C_internal (elite internal conflict) is observed to be low since both regime changes ended in concession. It remains far from the conditions of any collapse path among WD, SN, and BH.

Because the three paths form a framework categorizing post-uprising and post-collapse evolution, cases like Seychelles that release pressure through elections are more accurately described as 'stability outside the paths.' Remaining pressures (A5, A7) are structural and slow, and the overlap of the heroin problem with pressure on A7 via the working-age population is an object of observation. This is an enumeration of observations rather than a judgment.
