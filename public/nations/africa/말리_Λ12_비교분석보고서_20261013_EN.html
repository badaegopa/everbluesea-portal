Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Realignment of Alliances. According to the Country Analysis Series briefing (as of 2026-10-07), the government is moving toward regional integration within the AES framework, such as joint military forces and the Sahel Investment and Development Bank, and externally toward restoring relations with Algeria (Algerian Prime Minister's visit in 2026-09) in addition to Russia. At the 2nd AES Summit in December 2025, the rotating presidency was passed to Traoré of Burkina Faso.

The Blockaded Capital. In response to JNIM's fuel blockade, the government maintains a military-escorted fuel convoy system. In April 2026, JNIM urged the Malian public to 'overthrow the military junta' and adopt Islamic law (Series briefing).

Public Trends. Although the fuel blockade caused school closures and commercial paralysis, no large-scale anti-government protests were collected within the reporting period from November 2025 to September 2026 (Series briefing). Just before the dissolution of political parties in May 2025, pro-democracy protests involving several hundred people and an increase in abductions of activists were reported (AFP).
