Recent Developments (2025–2026)

Direction of the Coalition. Public Safety. South African Police Service (SAPS) quarterly statistics indicate that murders from January to March 2026 stood at 5,181 cases, down 9.5% from the same period last year, but still approximately 60 cases per day. Police and analysts added that unreported crimes are not captured in the statistics (BusinessTech·The Citizen).
