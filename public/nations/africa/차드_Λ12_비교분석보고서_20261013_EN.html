§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Institutional Lock-in. The October 2025 constitutional amendment applies 7-year terms with unlimited reelection starting from the next presidential election. Former Prime Minister Pahimi Padacké sent a letter to lawmakers calling the amendment 'unconstitutional and authoritarian' (Citizen Digital). Masra's camp announced an appeal to the Supreme Court.

Weight of the East. According to UNHCR, registered and pre-registered Sudanese refugees stand at 935,681, with a relocation rate to settlements of 68.15%. The total number of refugees of all nationalities is 1,541,646 (2026-07-06). This population impinges simultaneously on demographic indicators like A7 (measured: population growth) and food and water pressures in the eastern region (interpretation).

Public Trends. ACLED protests dropped from 113 events in 2022 to 30 in 2024. Following the 2022 crackdown, the 2023 amnesty, and the 2025 imprisonment of the opposition leader, no reports of large-scale protests have been collected within the verified scope.
