§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

The Form of Transition to Civilian Rule. The 2021 coup leader became president with a 7-year term through the 2025 new constitution (allowing military junta officials to run) and presidential election. Major opposition parties were in a state of suspension, boycott, or exclusion, and the top opposition leader was barred from the election (Al Jazeera report).

Resource Economy. In September 2025, national accounts were rebased, and the IMF projects growth of 6.5% in 2025 and approx. 10% the following year when Simandou production begins in earnest (Daba Finance report, IMF original text unverified). Simandou has a 3-month trial operation followed by a 30-month ramp-up plan.

Public Trends. Within the scope examined for 2025–2026, no reports of large-scale protests were collected. ACLED recorded 212 protests in 2023 and 175 in 2024. The voter turnout controversy of the 2025 referendum and presidential election is the largest public issue identified in this investigation.
