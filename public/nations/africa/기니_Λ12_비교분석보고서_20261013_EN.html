§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Form of Transition to Civilian Rule. The 2021 coup leader became president with a 7-year term through the 2025 new constitution (allowing junta members to run) and the presidential election. Major opposition parties were suspended, boycotted, or excluded, and the top opposition leader was barred from the election (Al Jazeera report).

Resource Economy. National accounts were rebased in September 2025, and IMF forecasts are 6.5% growth in 2025 and approx. 10% the following year as Simandou production starts in earnest (Daba Finance report, IMF original text not cross-checked). Simandou has a 30-month ramp-up plan following a 3-month trial operation.

Public Trends. Within the confirmed scope of 2025–2026, no reports of large-scale protests were collected. ACLED protests numbered 212 in 2023 and 175 in 2024. Turnout disputes over the 2025 referendum and presidential election represent the largest public controversy identified in this survey.
