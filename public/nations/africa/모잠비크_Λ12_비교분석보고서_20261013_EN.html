§ 02

Historical Timeline — Events That Shaped the Structure

1975 · 1992
Independence and End of Civil War — Independence in 1975; the FRELIMO–RENAMO civil war ended with a peace agreement in 1992 (general fact). FRELIMO has remained in power continuously since independence.
2016
Hidden Debts — Undisclosed loans under state-owned enterprises (amounting to approx. $2 billion) were uncovered, leading to IMF suspension of support and a debt crisis (general fact).
2017-10~
Cabo Delgado Insurgency — Armed insurgency began in northern Cabo Delgado. Of 2,251 violent incidents since 2017-10, 2,077 were related to 'Islamic State–Mozambique' (citing AIM). ACLED fatalities recorded 1,773 in 2020 and 1,830 in 2021.
2024-10-09 · 18
General Elections and Assassinations — General elections held. EU Election Observation Mission: irregularities during counting, 'unjustified alterations of results at polling station and district levels,' and barring of observers from parts of the tabulation process. Protests began after the assassination of opposition candidate Mondlane's lawyer and a PODEMOS official on October 18 (Africanews·AP).
2024-12-23
Constitutional Council Certification — FRELIMO's Daniel Chapo confirmed elected, with vote share revised downward from approximately 70% announced by the electoral commission to around 65% (VOA·Zitamar). Mondlane claimed victory.
2025-01 · 03
Inauguration and Dialogue — Chapo inaugurated in January, accompanied by opposition protests. In March, the president and political parties signed a political dialogue agreement (Mondlane absent); first Chapo–Mondlane meeting took place on March 23 (AllAfrica·ISS).
2025-09 · 10
Northern Displacement — Armed attacks between September 22 and October 6 displaced approximately 40,000 people across Cabo Delgado and Nampula, with over 26,000 displaced from Mocímboa da Praia (IOM, citing AIM).
§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Post-election. The March 2025 political dialogue agreement aimed to 'restore political and social stability,' and the Chapo–Mondlane meeting discussed halting violence and the possibility of amnesty for protest-related detainees, but failed to reach a definitive agreement (ISS·Daily Maverick). Reports of Mondlane's prosecution and participation in the Council of State emerged in the second half of 2025, but this survey only verified headline-level information.

Northern conflict. The Africa Center for Strategic Studies counted at least 349 fatalities from attacks in northern Mozambique in 2024 (a 36% increase over the previous year) (citing AIM). IOM warned in November 2025 that displacements continued toward Muidumbe and other areas, with violence threatening to spill over into Nampula.

Public trends. ACLED recorded 437 protest events in 2024, the highest between 2016 and 2024. Protest and northern conflict trends for 2026 were not collected in this survey.
