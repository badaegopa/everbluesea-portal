§ 03

Recent Situation (2025–2026)

Fiscal Policy and Inflation. Following debt restructuring and the IMF program, inflation fell from the 20% range in early 2025 to the 3–5% range in 2026 (Ghana Statistical Service). Whether the new administration renegotiated IMF terms could not be confirmed in this survey.

Regime Change. The 2024 presidential election resulted in a transfer of power following the ruling party candidate's concession of defeat (AllAfrica).

Public Trends. ACLED protests numbered 43 in 2018, 179 in 2020, 100 in 2021, 78 in 2022, 118 in 2023, and 161 in 2024. Incidents targeting civilians increased from 23 in 2018 to 201 in 2024.
