§ 03-B

Published Figures vs. Independent Sources — Examining the Gaps

Kenya's gap lies in the fact that protest deaths from the same demonstrations are tallied over different periods and with different figures by the police oversight body and the national human rights commission, while official government figures remain unconfirmed, and that the aftermath of the same protests is addressed by the government through compensation programs and by protesters through demands for justice and investigations.
