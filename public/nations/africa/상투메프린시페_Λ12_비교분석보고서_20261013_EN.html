§ 03

Recent Political Situation (2025–2026)

Political Landscape. Following the 2025 dismissal, conflict between the president and the former prime minister/ruling party carried over into the two elections of 2026. In the presidential election, the ruling party did not endorse the incumbent, and in the general election, the opposition MLSTP was reported to have become the largest party in parliament (GlobalSecurity, Wikipedia; verification required).

Aftermath of the 2022 Event. 12 military personnel and 4 civilians, including former National Assembly President Delfim Neves, were indicted on charges of attempting a coup. Neves called the event a 'charade' and claimed that authorities killed those who knew the truth (ADF). Final results of the investigation and trial were not found in this review.

Public Trends. ACLED demonstrations numbered 2 to 9 annually between 2020 and 2024.
